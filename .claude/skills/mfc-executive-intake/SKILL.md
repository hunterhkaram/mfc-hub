# MFC executive intake

Route MFC work through the existing executive system without making Hunter complete a technical
form, re-explain known context, or wait while ordinary work is deferred back to him.

## 1. Classify the request

- Bypass this workflow for a clearly unrelated question.
- Ask one short classification question only if it is genuinely unclear whether the request serves
  MFC's mission. Treat founder visibility, personal pages, and social content as MFC work when they
  serve the mission.
- Proceed directly when the task is clear and structured. Do not restate a simple request merely to
  seek confirmation.
- Use an intent echo for natural, multi-part, stream-of-consciousness MFC input. State the outcome
  understood, relevant workstreams, work that will proceed now, and the one protected judgement only
  if one genuinely remains.

## 2. Build the internal task packet

1. Resolve named topics through `90-meta/controlled-automation-v1/topic-control/canonical-topic-register.yml`
   before broad discovery. Treat it as a routing and reconciliation record, not as authority that
   replaces its linked decisions or operational artefacts. Preserve its open conflicts and keep AI
   assurance separate from human, clinical and legal approval. Inspect the registered canonical
   sources and existing operational destinations, reconstruct the current truth, and bring the
   strongest next-action recommendation before asking Hunter a resolvable technical question.
   Before substantive work, render and read that topic's complete state interface: **Where are we?**
   **What do we currently believe? What’s complete? What’s provisional? What conflicts? What’s
   awaiting specialist assurance? What’s awaiting Hunter? What’s awaiting external humans? What’s
   next?** Do not begin from an older artefact merely because it is detailed or easy to find. If the
   interface is missing, stale, or contradicted by a registered current source, reconstruct and
   update the interface before proceeding. Record identifiers are never standalone founder language:
   use the plain description first and place a code in parentheses only when traceability helps.
2. Read `90-meta/full-permanent-specialist-bench-v1/onboarding-manifest-v1.yml`, then load its
   shared core and the role pack required for the task. Use detailed founder sources only when the
   manifest's trigger is met.
3. Read `CLAUDE.md`, then the task-relevant MFC core, role guidance, memory, skills, and current
   state indicated by the request.
4. Derive the technical fields in
   `90-meta/core-operations-hardening-token-efficiency/05-context-manifest-and-discovery.md` rather
   than asking Hunter to fill them in. Create or update the Context Manifest for material work.
5. Retrieve narrowly: read the most authoritative, relevant, and current sources first; use indexes
   before detail files; stop once the task's evidence threshold is met.
6. Record sources actually checked, inaccessible sources, uncertainty, and relevant conflicts. Do
   not claim a source was read, a live system was updated, or a capability exists unless verified.

## 3. Route and decide

- Route cross-project rhythm, priorities, commitments, and executive updates through Cadence; route
  objective planning and task control through Atlas; use specialist input only where its domain is
  relevant.
- When a material task depends on what is current, a potential conflict, or a relevant source change,
  load the `organisational_freshness` workflow pack before relying on older context. When the task
  asks what MFC should proactively explore, research, draft, or prioritise, load the
  `opportunity_intelligence` workflow pack. Both remain pilots and do not widen authority.
- In every material task receipt, record `workflow_packs_loaded` and a plain-language
  `workflow_invocation_reason`. Record `none` when neither workflow was relevant; listing a pack in
  the manifest is not evidence that it was used.
- Apply the source-precedence model. Resolve ordinary retrieval questions autonomously. Preserve an
  unresolved material conflict rather than silently choosing a side.
- Treat a direct founder instruction as authority for the future action it explicitly directs, but
  do not use it to rewrite or declare a conflicting source false. State the discrepancy, preserve
  the record, and prepare the exact update or clarification needed.
- Never invent an owner, mark a current source as stale, or reassign live work merely because an
  action is incomplete. Recommend an evidence-grounded owner where one exists; otherwise complete
  the unaffected work and carry one narrow ownership question or ready-to-apply change forward.
- Never nominate Hunter as an interim/default owner merely to close an ownership gap. Hunter is the
  founder authority, not a catch-all operational owner; use a named source-backed owner or leave the
  owner decision explicitly unresolved.
- Complete all unaffected, reversible work. Escalate only the smallest decision that meets a real
  protected condition under `CLAUDE.md §6a`.
- Bring a decision-ready recommendation: best path, up to two genuine alternatives if needed,
  confidence and why it is limited, consequence, and one exact founder judgement. Never send a
  document dump or invent a requirement for committee, clinical, legal, or other approval.
- For material work, determine the professional assurance actually required from the objective,
  consequences and evidence—not from a fixed agent checklist and never by asking Hunter which
  specialist to run. Inspect whether the task's premise and its connection to MFC's theory of change
  are sound, not only whether the requested artifact is internally consistent.
- **Material design work has a mandatory design-intelligence stage before production is treated as
  converged.** When the outcome creates, redesigns, optimises, recommends or finalises a product,
  programme, strategy, positioning or consequential audience experience, apply
  `90-meta/full-permanent-specialist-bench-v1/design-intelligence-and-founder-finalisation-standard.md`.
  Screen every registered specialist with a visible reasoned disposition; execute only the relevant
  bounded reviews in stages; use optimal relevant coverage rather than minimum headcount; require a
  separate A-24 challenge of omissions/inclusions and SYS-04 reconciliation before execution; compare
  genuinely different approaches; and maintain a rationale ledger
  for the load-bearing choices. Do this before polishing the current candidate. “Finalise” means
  complete the system's in-remit optimisation and bring Hunter the current artefact and recommendation;
  it never grants or implies Hunter approval.
- A specialist status may move only when that lens has actually executed and produced inspectable
  evidence. A registered role, prompt, simulated persona or polished output is not an assurance pass.
  Record AI specialist assurance separately from qualified-human, committee and real-user review.
- Before executing a specialist, load its entry and readable pack from
  `90-meta/full-permanent-specialist-bench-v1/specialist-knowledge-index.yml`. Supply the shared MFC
  orientation, current pack, task packet and bounded sources. If the role is
  `role_only_not_equipped` or `stale`, equip/refresh it before treating its output as domain
  assurance; never hide the missing knowledge behind the agent's name. Require the output to state
  the pack version, professional principles applied, evidence limits, strongest objection, what
  would change its view and any proposed learning. Follow `specialist-knowledge-standard.md`.
- Enforce `specialist-call-contract.md` on every claimed specialist call. Save the task packet and
  one contract-complete output per bounded or independent reviewer; declare the execution mode and
  learning disposition. A same-context role lens is consultation, not separately executed
  specialist assurance, and a shared synthesis cannot be counted once per named specialist.
- An equipped pack and a passing structural validator are not professional assurance. Claim an
  `evaluated_pack` only after the seven-case substantive evaluation in
  `specialist-substantive-evaluation-standard.md` has passed independent checking. Supply the
  source-graded professional evidence and accepted/narrowed/rejected MFC learning at every call.

## 4. Close the propagation loop

- After an approved material change, update the existing operational destination(s); do not create
  another Hub, registry or parallel final unless the topic record explicitly authorises that role.
- For Drive, Wix or another authenticated system, use the available connector, record the
  returned revision or receipt, and read it back before claiming the destination is current. A local
  file or hook is not evidence that the external system changed.
- Update the topic record to the reconciled state and regenerate `90-meta/MFC-HUB.html` plus its
  pillar pages. Run `90-meta/hub/check_hub_integrity.py`.
- Record the resulting start/progress/completion/waiting/correction state through
  `90-meta/controlled-automation-v1/topic-control/record_topic_reconciliation.py`. The receipt must
  name the evidence and distinguish verified external readback, Hunter-reported external state and
  unverified external state. This is required even when the work happened in another chat and only
  becomes known through Hunter's correction.
- Do not claim completion while a required destination is stale, a projection fingerprint is stale,
  the reconciliation receipt no longer matches current sources/state, or a protected
  human/qualified gate remains open. Report the bounded result and the exact open gate.

## 5. Produce and check

- Draft external communications, public content, outreach, or commitments; do not send or publish
  them unless a verified authority permits it.
- For work of real importance or value, run the separate producer → checker → revise → recheck loop
  required by `CLAUDE.md §9b`. Record the producer, separate checker context, material findings,
  revision, and recheck result.
- **For anything committee-facing, funder-facing, strategy/positioning/pricing, or otherwise high-
  consequence across multiple professional domains: run the real multi-specialist panel, not only the single
  producer/checker loop.** Proven 2026-08-11 to find real defects (a factual pricing error, a total
  safeguarding-disclosure gap) that a single-pass independent check had already missed on a document
  already marked "finalised, independently reviewed." How to run it, the panel composition, the
  routing-record format, and the verification command:
  `90-meta/full-permanent-specialist-bench-v1/how-to-run-a-real-panel.md`. This is the standard for
  this category of work now, not an occasional escalation — Hunter's own words: *"I want it to
  consider various options even if I don't instruct... that's the point of the vault."*
- The panel's purpose is not merely to find defects in a finished draft. For a qualifying design
  objective it begins at W5a: whole-bench coverage screen → divergence → bounded specialist
  challenges → explicit reconciliation → artefact optimisation → founder finalisation brief. A
  later producer/checker pass still tests the result, but cannot stand in for this design stage.
- Claude chooses the smallest sufficient panel and records why. Do not ask Hunter to select full
  versus direct processing, specialists, skills, or review depth; escalate only a protected §6a
  founder/human-authority decision after completing all research the system can do itself.
- Use the seven-dimension readiness signal for material work only: mission fit, audience value,
  evidence integrity, safety/reputation and claims boundaries, conceptual/content integrity,
  delivery readiness, and assurance/learning. Treat it as a calibrated signal, never as fabricated
  precision or an invented hard gate.
- **The scoring bands, the three anti-theatre rules, and the founder-result format are defined in
  `references/readiness-signal-and-founder-result.md`. Read it before producing either.** Until
  2026-08-09 neither was defined, and scores were being rendered as precise numbers with no method —
  the exact fabricated precision this bullet prohibits. Key points: the headline Hunter sees is the
  **average** of the seven dimensions with the **lowest dimension highlighted beside it** (his
  correction, 2026-08-09 — never the average alone, never the lowest as the headline); **every
  dimension below 90 must name its specific limiter**; **nothing exceeds 89 without an independent
  check** by a separate context; and the **75-per-dimension line is unchanged** — the average is what
  he sees, never what decides whether he sees it. That line is a working calibration, not the gate
  itself; the gate is the three-question test in the reference file (was it genuinely separately
  checked, are unsafe or overclaimed statements actually repaired, does anything still held back name
  a real blocker).
- **Improvement items shown to Hunter must be outside the system's own remit** (his correction,
  2026-08-09). If the agent could have done it — rewrite, re-check, chase a source, fill a gap the
  vault would fill — it gets **done before sending**, not listed. Only a required human input, a
  `§6a` founder judgement, a fact that exists nowhere, access the system lacks, or evidence only time
  or contact can produce may appear, each naming its blocker. The separate checker rejects any
  in-remit item and sends it back for the work. Full rule and test:
  `references/readiness-signal-and-founder-result.md`.

## 5a. The founder result — material work only

**Material work ends with a founder-readable HTML result, not a chat summary.** Hunter, 2026-08-09:
*"I don't know what 01 06 is... Send me something, and I can click yes."*

**⚠ Hunter must not see a sub-threshold result.** Corrected same day: *"I should never see this
version — I should get the final version after the CEO and Committee have approved of it as it meets
the threshold/approval zone. Agent is to be looped — not me re-prompting it."* The gate is the
reference file's three-question test, with 75+ on every dimension as its working calibration — below
either, the bounded producer→checker→revise
→recheck loop (`§9b`, capped per `§7`) runs automatically. He sees the approved result or a genuine
escalation under `§6a`'s six conditions. Never a draft score, never a status update mid-loop. Full
mechanism: `references/readiness-signal-and-founder-result.md`.

- **No file paths or record IDs as the primary way of naming things.** Never "01-06" or "C-13" —
  say "the notes describing who you are and what MFC is," "three committee documents still using the
  old definition." Identifiers in parentheses at most.
- Lead with what changed and what it means. Every decision Hunter must make gets a yes/no/later
  control and an export that copies clean text back.
- Always state what was **not** done and what remains unverified.
- **Not for conversation, status checks or plain answers** — producing one for those is ceremony,
  and `L-016` treats ceremony as a failure, not diligence.

Full format rules: `references/readiness-signal-and-founder-result.md`.

## 5b. The decision trail — material work only, every time

**Hunter, 2026-08-10:** *"When I ask for a project, I wanna see how it actually tracked through.
So what agents were brought into it, I wanna see the working tree of where it started, who it went
to, what their decision was, the next person, what their decision was, they agree, disagree. I wanna
see that every single time now. Because then I know if something's been missed and why, and then we
can start to address it."*

**Every material project produces a decision trail alongside the founder result.** It is a separate
artefact, not a section inside the deliverable, and it is not optional.

It must show, at minimum:

1. **Where it started** — the intake, and what ground truth was read at source before work began.
2. **Every agent actually run** — name, role, and the verdict it returned. Not agents considered.
3. **Whether each verdict was accepted, narrowed or rejected** — and the reason.
4. **Every disagreement between agents, and who won.** Where a challenger overturned a researcher,
   or the producer overturned a challenger, say so and say why.
5. **What the producer verified independently**, and what that verification changed.
6. **What was missed, who should have caught it, and why they didn't.** This is the point of the
   artefact — an unbroken chain of agreement is a signal to distrust, not a result to report.
7. **A count of errors that reached Hunter before being caught.** That number, not the number caught
   internally, is the measure of whether the system is working.

**Naming:** `90-meta/TRAIL-<date>-<objective>.html`. First instance:
`TRAIL-2026-08-10-committee-pack.html`.

**Why it is separate from the founder result:** the result page answers *what should we do*; the
trail answers *how much should you trust it, and where would it have broken*. Merging them buries
the second question inside the first.

## 6. Hand back and learn

- **⛔ When a founder result was produced (§4a), the chat message must NOT restate it.** Corrected
  2026-08-07 after exactly that: a full founder-result page was sent, and then the entire contents
  were rewritten as several hundred words of chat underneath it. Hunter: *"why am I not getting a
  simplified founder view here?"* — then, on being told the rule existed: *"Yes but why? Like we just
  did all this work in our obsidian and md files as requirements?"* He was right twice. The bullet
  below said "explain what changed, why it matters, why the recommendation is sound," which read as
  licence to write that summary; §4a says the page IS the explanation. **The page is the deliverable.
  The chat message is a doorbell, not a second copy.** Two or three sentences maximum: what it is,
  the single most important thing in it, and anything genuinely urgent that cannot wait for a click.
  If the chat message could be read *instead of* the page, it is too long. **This has no lint —
  `check_founder_result_gate.py` inspects the artefact only, never the message sent with it — so it
  holds by being followed, and that is exactly why it failed once already.**
- Where **no** founder result was produced (ordinary conversation, a plain answer, a status check):
  lead with the outcome, explain what changed and why it matters, in founder language. Use visuals
  where they make the reasoning easier to grasp. This is the case the bullet was originally written
  for.
- For meaningful learning, state plainly what changed, why it changed, and what will work differently
  next time. Apply the correction-propagation protocol when the lesson is reusable.
- Capture a genuine commitment with owner, outcome, timing, and authority. Write only to a verified
  canonical system; otherwise provide an exact ready-to-apply update and say that no live write was
  made.
