# D-0112 — One take-home leave-behind, not separate cards; Route B simplified to agreement, not live design

## The problem Hunter named directly

The current build issues up to eight separate per-person printed items (disclosure card, skills
card, SSD card, HOS sheet, State Check card, shift-toolkit sheet, Radar worksheet, cue card).
Hunter's own words: *"I don't know if we have a cue card... that just means they need to be
bringing paper, which is an extra cost for us, and they're probably just gonna chuck it in the
bin... that's the truth."* The realistic failure mode is cost without retention — printed pieces
people don't carry and don't keep.

Separately, Hunter does not want Route B (the shared/collective room-agreement moment) disabled,
but reframed: not the room co-designing new commitments live, but a simple, repeated "do we agree
we want this, understand it, value it" — and, where a cohort has that context, selecting from
implementation options already worked out with managers beforehand, not inventing new ones live.

## Decision

### 1. One consolidated take-home leave-behind replaces the separate reference cards

The disclosure card, skills card, SSD card, HOS sheet, State Check card and cue card are replaced
by **one single folded leave-behind per person** — a real "cheat sheet," not several loose items:
one panel per short reference (skills, SSD, State Check), and a scan/QR link encouraging
participants to complete their Radar on the MFC website. One object, sized to survive a desk
drawer, not a stack of cards.

**Corrected 2026-08-27, same day, direct from Hunter:** the leave-behind is **premade and identical
for every participant — nothing is written on it.** The if/then cue ("when X, I will Y") is written
on the participant's own phone (a note, reminder, or saved message), not on any physical object.
The fold-and-hold physical ritual folds the premade leave-behind exactly as printed; the gesture,
not an act of personal writing, is what closes that beat. The first draft of this record wrongly
described a write-in space on the leave-behind — this section supersedes that description.

**Not affected:** the Radar worksheet (A4, substantive, already theirs to keep) and the two measure
forms (data collection, not reference material) stay separate as before. The shift-toolkit sheet's
content, where still delivered, folds into the same single leave-behind rather than staying a
separate sheet.

**Butcher's paper stays exactly as already designed** for in-session writing (individual or shared,
per the existing Route A/B split) — this record does not touch that mechanism, only the take-home
object.

### 2. Route B reframed: agreement and predetermined-choice, not live co-design

Route B is **not disabled by this record** — it remains gated exactly as D-0110/D-0111 already
established (no shared/aggregate moment runs until the team-nomination module is separately
privacy-reviewed and approved per engagement). What changes, for when it is cleared to run: the
room is not asked to invent a new shared commitment live. It is asked two simpler things, repeated
at the start, middle and end of the session in Hunter's own words — *"do we all agree that we all
want this, that we understand it, that we can value it"* — and, where the cohort's managers have
already selected implementation options beforehand (e.g. "every Friday" or "end of each team
meeting"), the room chooses which of those predetermined options they're most likely to actually
follow through on. This is evidence-aligned specificity ("this is what we do, and we're
incorporating this") rather than a vague suggestion ("go for a walk").

**Framing discipline, repeated throughout the session per Hunter's direction:** the session states
plainly, more than once, what this is and isn't — mental fitness, not "all about mental health" or
"emotional openness." That distinction is not new content; it restates the existing claims-boundary
discipline (K-08) as an explicit, repeated in-room line rather than an implicit design choice.

## What this does not change

- Does not reopen D-0110 (Foundations remains individual-teaching-focused; Route B stays gated on
  the same terms).
- Does not reopen D-0111 (product architecture, manager-session unification unaffected).
- Does not authorise Route B to run for any cohort without its existing privacy-review gate cleared.

## Claim boundary

Authorises materials and room-design changes only. Does not authorise any claim that the
consolidated leave-behind or the reframed agreement moment improves retention, follow-through, or
organisational outcomes — untested, as with every other mechanism in this build. K-08 continues to
govern all external-facing statements.
