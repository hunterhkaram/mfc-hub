## 1. The category, derived from MFC's own definitions, not the market's boxes

**Why this section is written the way it is.** Onno, as chair, asked what the product category is: *"is it a behavioural intervention? a training product? a wellbeing programme? is it WHS? It
probably is all of it."* He was reaching for categories a committee already recognises, which is a
reasonable instinct for a governance reader. **But MFC has already done its own definitional work,
and the category has to come out of that.** Hunter, 2026-08-16: *"We know what we've already
defined. The difference between mental health, the difference with psychological intervention and
first aid. This is very different, and we know that."*

### 1.1 The one-sentence category

> **MFC delivers mental fitness training: teaching and rehearsing a practised procedure a person
> runs on their own state, in the ordinary moments of their own life.**

### 1.2 What that means, in the four distinctions MFC already holds

| | What it does | What MFC does differently |
|---|---|---|
| **Mental health support** | Addresses how someone *is doing*, treatment, support, recovery | MFC trains what someone can *do*. Mental health is a state; mental fitness is a capability. Neither replaces the other |
| **Psychological intervention / therapy** | Requires a clinician, usually a presenting problem, and takes place in a clinical relationship | MFC teaches, in a group, without diagnosis, without a clinician, and **without requiring anything to be wrong** |
| **Mental Health First Aid** | Trains a person to recognise and respond to **someone else's** crisis | MFC trains a person to work with **their own state**, in the moment, and not only in crisis |
| **Wellbeing programmes** | Deliver methods and content, mindfulness, gratitude, resilience sessions | MFC trains **the loop itself**. Methods are what you might use; the loop is how you get to using anything at all |

**Why the first-aid distinction is live, not academic.** The most significant new entrant to this
market, The Working Mind, is licensed from Canada's Mental Health Commission via Mental Health First
Aid International, with Australian delivery from 1 June 2026. **A buyer will reasonably ask how MFC
differs from it, and the answer is the third row above**: that is a different job, not a weaker
version of the same one. No claim is made here that any competitor's programme fails.

### 1.3 The differentiator, stated once

**Everyone else teaches methods. MFC trains the loop.** The failure the whole sector runs into is
not that people lack methods, it is that awareness arrives without a usable next move. Someone
notices they are about to do the thing they always do, and does it anyway. **Stop–Shift–Do is three
steps, in the moment, always.** The routes are taught so that "shift" has somewhere to go; they are
training content, not in-moment load.

*(Status: this mechanism is founder practitioner judgement and a design premise. It is consistent
with the transfer and implementation-intention evidence in the vault. **MFC has not demonstrated it
in its own participants.** Do not present it as an established finding.)*

### 1.4 Why the category is not "WHS training"

The regulated psychosocial hazards are environmental, workload, role clarity, support, exposure,
bullying. **Training does not remove any of them.** Victoria has written this into regulation:
information, instruction and training *"must not be the predominant measure (subject to reasonable
practicability)."* MFC is bought **alongside** an employer's controls, never as one. This is already
MFC's decided position (`D-0102`) and the 11 August paper had drifted off it.

## 2. The boundary statement, what MFC is and is not

**For use verbatim in the committee paper, and in any buyer-facing material once reviewed.**

> **Mental Fitness Collective delivers skills-based mental fitness training with measurement.**
>
> **It is not** clinical care or therapy. **It is not** a substitute for legal or work-health-and-safety
> advice. **It is not** a standalone psychosocial risk management system, and it is not a control an
> employer can rely on to discharge a duty.
>
> **What it can do** is teach a practised procedure people can use in real moments, and measure
> honestly whether they used it.

**Two things must be said alongside it, and neither is optional:**

1. **No lawyer and no WHS practitioner has reviewed any of MFC's positioning.** This wording is
   MFC's own careful drafting, not cleared advice.
2. **MFC's own claim red-lines are not yet ratified.** `K-08` records that the Claims Register is
   unadopted (`C-15`/`C-24`) and all eight board decisions remain pending. The boundary statement
   above is therefore a *working* boundary, not an approved one.

**One live conflict the committee should know about (`C-18`):** MFC's Framework prohibits claiming
it prevents mental illness, while the Constitution names *"the prevention and control of mental
illness"* as a principal activity. That needs qualified advice. **No legal conclusion is drawn here.**

### 3.1 The monthly session, an option, not a fourth product

A ~30-minute guided session, monthly, offered **after** an initial delivery, minimum three months,
extending only on request. Data captured between sessions shapes what each one covers.

**It goes into the committee paper as an option under development, not as a priced product.**
Reason, stated plainly: **it has no name, no decision record, and its design changed three times on
2026-08-15.** Presenting it as settled would be the first thing a careful committee member found
wrong.

**Confirm:** that this is the right treatment for now.

### 3.2 What is discontinued

- **The $95 per-seat online session** (`Source: 110-PRODUCT-AND-PRICING-BOOK`). Never sellable, and the most safeguarding-exposed item in the
  lineup. Removing it closes an open exposure at no revenue cost.
- **Introduction as a paid product first.** It is outreach that can be booked, not a revenue line.
