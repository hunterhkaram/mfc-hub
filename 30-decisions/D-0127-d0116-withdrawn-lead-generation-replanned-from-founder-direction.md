# D-0127. D-0116 is withdrawn

## The decision

Hunter, 2026-09-05, verbatim: *"remove D-0116."*

`D-0116` (2026-08-31) fixed the lead engine as social media first, MFC's own free online
Leadership Community second, and being in rooms third. **That ordering no longer stands.** Lead
generation is re-planned from the founder direction below, and nothing in `D-0116` is carried
forward by default.

## The founder direction that replaces it, verbatim

1. *"Not our actual community - joining other communities and going to explain or simply teach
   mental fitness (reps)."* — The rooms channel is walking into gatherings that already exist and
   teaching a rep in five to fifteen minutes. It is not founding a community.
2. *"180,000 followers is my friend Tom D (is interested) in being an ambassador - so the strategy
   is to incorporate an ambassador program."*
3. *"make the strategy for our instagram having 8800 followers now, and a few hundred on linked
   In. I also have socials which i am not active on at the moment. And don't want to be too active
   - although open to it if evidence supports this move/approach."*
4. *"If community build (our own) is the best approach or one of... then i am open to entertaining
   it. is it virtual, in real life? how do we reach people - perhaps track data etc?"* — MFC's own
   community is **an option to be evaluated**, not the engine.
5. *"i can give five hours to lead gen a week - thats at this stage, could be more if we need to
   sell the services or less if its up and running and orgs and communities are reaching out."*

## What this does and does not change

- **`D-0111` §3 is unaffected.** MFC's own community remains not authorised for build. `D-0127`
  authorises its *evaluation* inside the lead-generation plan, nothing more.
- **`FINAL-03` is not reissued.** The committee holds the 17 August paper. The reconciled position
  returns to them as part of the consolidated strategy, as `D-0116` itself said it would.
- **The ambassador programme draft** (`90-meta/PROGRAMME-2026-08-25-ambassador-v1.html`) and the
  Tom debrief stand as the starting point. Nothing about Tom is public and no agreement exists.
- **`R1`'s finding** — that personal-profile social posting is a trust asset rather than lead
  generation — is no longer overridden by a founder ordering. It is evidence the plan must weigh,
  which is what Hunter asked for: *"open to it if evidence supports this move/approach."*
