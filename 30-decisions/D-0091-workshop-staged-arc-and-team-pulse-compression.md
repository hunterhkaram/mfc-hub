# D-0091 — Workshop staged-arc exception and Team Pulse compression

## Why this exists

Hunter, 2026-08-09, on `D-0090`'s escalated recommendation to reopen Team Pulse: *"i just don't know
how we bring in the team pulse as we aren't doing 3-4 touch points now - the 2 products was a 1hr
workshop and 3hr workshop."* Then, directly: *"i should see you complete the project on the workshop
now."*

Two things were open when he said that. Both resolve together, because they're the same underlying
fact: **MFC's live products are two standalone sessions, not a multi-touchpoint relationship.**

## 1. The staged-arc conflict — resolved, written into K-07 directly

Flagged earlier the same session: `K-07`'s staged teaching arc (`D-0063`) holds back full five-skill
naming, State Check's full nuance, and three of the Shift menu's four options until "Stage 2 —
Practising, early ongoing/online contact." The built 3-hour and 1-hour workshops teach all of this in
full inside what the arc calls Stage 1.

**Resolved as an exception, not a reversal.** The arc's own logic — match content depth to the
contact a person actually gets — was applied correctly by asking what contact MFC's real products
actually offer. Both are standalone; there is no Stage 2 touchpoint most attendees ever reach. For a
standalone Foundations Session specifically, Stage 1 and Stage 2 now collapse into one delivery
moment in `K-07` — full detail, reasoning and the boundary this does **not** move (Stage 3 stays held
back) is written directly into `K-07`'s staged-arc section, not duplicated here.

**No change was needed to the workshop itself for this part.** The workshop already teaches at
Stage 1+2 depth; the framework record was the thing out of step, and it's now corrected.

## 2. Team Pulse — compressed into the two touchpoints that actually exist

`D-0090` found the evidence-preferred mechanism for shifting norms in a group already exists in
MFC's own source material (Team Pulse → Team Radar Snapshot → facilitated discussion → shared
direction → observable practice → fortnightly review) and was shelved because it was designed for
Offer 2's 3–4 touchpoint Implementation Pathway. **That pathway is not what MFC runs.** MFC runs one
pre-session form, one session, and one post-session sequence (Form 2, Form 3, and — for case
studies — the extended check-in `D-0056` already approved). Team Pulse needed to be redesigned onto
that shape, not deferred until a different product exists.

### The redesign, mapped onto what already exists

| Team Pulse step | Where it now lives | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Private individual preference | **Form 1** (already collected before any teaching) | One new nomination item, proposed not yet live — see workshop-package.html, "Our recommendation," item 08 |
| Anonymous aggregate reveal | **In-session**, at the existing Our-moments beat | Facilitator aggregates Form 1 nominations beforehand and reveals the common themes live, anonymously — replacing the current mechanism, where each person privately guesses alone with no aggregation at all |
| Facilitated discussion → shared direction | **Same beat**, extended by a few minutes | The room is asked whether there's genuine appetite to agree on one shared practice — not assumed, asked |
| One observable practice, phrased as a commitment | **Same beat** | Phrased "we-if-then" (`D-0090`'s adopted collective-implementation-intentions proposal), not a vague shared aspiration |
| Fortnightly review | **Already built** — `D-0056`'s case-study extended check-in and its fortnightly reminder/org check-in cadence | No new mechanism needed; the existing case-study-only wave now has something specific to review |

**Explicitly not adopted: a genuine-agreement gate or safety screen.** `D-0090` already rejected this
— a room without real appetite is told plainly it's fine to leave it, not pushed to a fake consensus.
That instruction is written into the redesigned beat itself, not assumed.

### Why this needs a facilitator prep step, stated honestly

Aggregating Form 1 nominations before the room requires the facilitator to do this between forms
closing and the session starting. **This is a real, new operational cost, not a free upgrade** — small
for a single session, but not zero, and it should be named rather than hidden inside "no participant
friction." Participant-facing friction is genuinely unchanged: one additional form question, no new
in-room burden beyond a few minutes of discussion that replaces private silent writing.

### The 1-hour format — deliberately does less, not less-well

The 1-hour has no room for a live facilitated discussion. **It keeps its existing, already-correct
decision to decline working Our moments live**, recorded as a named `D-0014` deviation. What changes:
the same Form 1 nomination question is still collected (it costs nothing extra — same form, both
formats), and the facilitator can hand the host organisation what people privately said they'd want
to work on together, even where there wasn't time to work it live. A smaller contribution, honestly
smaller, not disguised as the full mechanism.

## 2a. Added 2026-08-09 — what "Team Pulse" actually means now, stated precisely for Hunter

Hunter, same day, a genuinely fair concern: *"with the team pulse — we haven't figured out what that
will be and how it runs."* Checked directly against `K-07` rather than assumed. Two different things
share the name, and conflating them is exactly what produced the uncertainty:

**What's actually built and live, right now:** the compressed, in-session mechanism this section
designed — private nomination via Form 1, anonymous aggregate reveal at 3:04, the room agreeing (or
not) on one shared "when X, we will Y" commitment. It's in `workshop-package.html` today. This is
what "Team Pulse" means inside the current product.

**What was never built, and remains explicitly deferred:** the *source* mechanism this borrowed its
name and spirit from — *Team Pulse → Team Radar Snapshot → facilitated discussion → shared direction
→ observable practice → fortnightly review → follow-up evaluation* (Working Group 3, `K-07`'s own
Radar section). `K-07` states this directly: *"deliberately not adopted here... remains a working
concept awaiting its own review."* That status is unchanged by this record. **Hunter's instinct that
"how it runs" isn't fully figured out is correct for this bigger version — it was never meant to be
resolved by the in-session compression above, and shouldn't be assumed solved.**

**The one real gap worth closing now, distinct from the bigger deferred mechanism:** once a room
agrees a shared commitment, nothing currently checks on it before Form 3 arrives at day 21–28. For an
individual, a Mental Rep can fade the same way — but the workshop actively addresses that with a cue
card and named barriers (3:22). The shared commitment gets none of that. **Proposed, not yet built:**
a single templated reminder to whoever the room names as owning the commitment, sent around the
Form 3 reminder infrastructure's own midpoint (roughly day 10–14) — the same wording the shared
moment was recorded in, nothing more. This is deliberately **not** a facilitator touchpoint or a
second product — it costs no delivery time, doesn't reopen `D-0094`/`D-0095`'s ordering rule against
building a second product before a pilot, and is closer to an email template than a design decision.
**Genuinely untested, and should be labelled as an attempt, not a proven reinforcement mechanism** —
consistent with `D-0095`'s finding that reminder-style nudges show real-world effects far smaller
than their appeal suggests.

**Drafted, not yet wired into delivery infrastructure** — since this is genuinely an email template
rather than a design decision, drafting it is in the system's own remit and shouldn't wait on Hunter:

> Subject: Quick check-in on [the shared commitment]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> A couple of weeks ago your team agreed: **"[the exact wording the room recorded]."**
>
> Just a short check-in — how's it going? No need to report back, this is just a nudge to keep it
> live while it's still fresh.
>
> — MFC

**Send trigger and owner:** attached to the same Form 3 reminder infrastructure, at its own midpoint
(day 10–14), addressed to whoever the room named as owning the commitment at the time it was agreed
— captured as part of the same in-session beat, not a separate data-collection step. **Not yet
wired into the actual Form 3 send mechanism** — that integration work, and confirming the room
actually names an owner reliably enough to send this to, is the one piece still open, and is better
tested in a pilot than guessed at now.

## 3. What was implemented, not just decided

- `K-07`'s staged-arc section — the exception above, written directly (§1).
- `workshop-package.html`, 3-hour Walkthrough — the Our-moments beat (3:04–3:16) redesigned to the
  aggregate-reveal-and-agree mechanism; Rationale & Evidence updated to cite the actual mechanism now
  taught, replacing the earlier citations that described the old private-write version.
- `workshop-package.html`, Outcomes & Measurement — "Our recommendation" gains item 08 (the Form 1
  nomination question) and the case-study "Case study" tab is updated to name what the extended
  check-in now specifically reviews.
- The 1-hour Walkthrough and its own D-0014 deviation record are **not** changed in mechanism — only
  the shared framing (why it declines, and what it still collects) is clarified.

## 4. What this does not do

- Does not claim organisational or cultural change from the standard pathway. The redesigned beat is
  still individual-self-report at every measured point except the shared practice's continuation,
  which only the case-study cohort's extended check-in reviews.
- Does not add a fourth participant form. Same architecture as `D-0056`/`D-0090` already settled:
  three forms for everyone, one case-study-only extended wave.
- Does not resolve the genuine circularity `D-0090` already named — psychological safety is both a
  precondition for honest group agreement and something the mechanism hopes to help build. Naming
  that a room can decline is the mitigation adopted, not a claim the circularity is closed.

## 5. Confidence and what remains unverified

**High** that this is the right compression of an evidence-preferred mechanism onto MFC's real product
shape — it reuses infrastructure that already exists rather than inventing new touchpoints.
**Untested**: no pilot has run this exact beat in either form, old or new. The facilitator-aggregation
step has not been rehearsed. Whether a room genuinely reaches appetite for a shared practice, or
mostly declines, is unknown either way.
