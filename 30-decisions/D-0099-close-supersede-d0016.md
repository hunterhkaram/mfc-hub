  paper, Market overview, Offerings ladder, Forms instrument) — removed in the same session, per

input; not obtained.
3, 5. Delivery-host and MFC-side operational matters — not resolved.

6. Insurer/broker confirmation of cover — still requires confirmation; not obtained.

**This is Hunter's own decision to make, and it is made here.** The record exists so a future session

strategy work on them rather than to resolve them. If MFC delivers a session to a real participant,
whatever safeguarding practice actually exists at that time — reviewed or not — is what protects that
participant; this record does not supply one.
