# D-0110 — Foundations is individual teaching, not environment-readiness delivery, for now

## The question this resolves

A 2026-08-27 bounded design panel (three per-product reviews plus a Compass founder-lens check)
found that Foundations, as built, implicitly positions itself as the product that does the
"environment and implementation conditions" job in MFC's three-part intervention model (`KN-0006`
part 3) — it is the only format with a team mechanism and the only one with room to spare — but does
not actually deliver on that positioning. Compass's framing: *"does Hunter want Foundations to go
deeper on individual content, or actually become the format that does the organisational/leader
readiness job — because right now it markets itself as the latter and delivers the former."*

Hunter's instruction, 2026-08-27: *"Take the Foundations question first."*

## Decision

**For the current build cycle, Foundations' committed differentiator is the deepest individual
teaching of the three formats — not organisational or environment-readiness delivery.**

1. The team-shared-commitment mechanism (`D-0091`'s in-session Team Pulse compression, called
   **Route B** in the live facilitator guide) remains exactly as designed: a real, built capability,
   correctly gated in the current script behind a per-session requirement — *"[GATE] Use only when
   the exact nomination item was approved, privacy-reviewed, collected and safely aggregated before
   the session"* (`20-half-day-foundations-facilitator-guide-current.md`, 3:04–3:10 beat). **Stated
   precisely, not overstated:** that gate hasn't been cleared for the plain reason that MFC has never
   delivered any Foundations cohort at all yet — Route B hasn't specifically failed a review or been
   blocked, it simply hasn't been reached, and no standing process exists to clear it routinely once
   real delivery starts. **This record does not remove, redesign, or devalue Route B.** It stays
   built, available, and correctly gated.
2. **Route B is not to be treated, marketed, priced, or positioned as an active differentiator of
   the current Foundations product.** Every real session, absent a specific, separately-approved
   privacy-reviewed nomination process for that specific engagement, runs Route A — private
   individual Radar, identical in kind to Essentials. That is the honest routine floor, and product
   positioning should say so.
3. The team-nomination question on Form 1 (D-0091's "smaller, honestly smaller" contribution) stays
   exactly as designed: private signal-gathering that MFC can hand back to a host organisation, not
   an intervention and not claimed as one.
4. **This is a resourcing and sequencing decision, not a judgement that Route B is a bad idea.**
   Building Route B into standing, routinely-cleared infrastructure — a repeatable privacy-review
   process, tested aggregation, a real cohort to run it on — is real, separate, uncosted work that a
   one-person charity with no cohort ever delivered should not take on inside this build cycle
   alongside everything else still open (safeguarding, clinical, privacy/legal, participant testing).
   Nothing here forecloses building it later, once a cohort has actually run and there is a reason to
   invest in the collective mechanism specifically.

## Addendum, 2026-08-27 (same day) — extended to two more mechanisms found the same day

A reconciliation pass looking for other built-but-orphaned MFC design work (day-21 return session,
Essentials leader briefing, manager-forum spec) found two more mechanisms in the identical category
this record already covers — real, well-reasoned design work that gets ahead of what MFC can
currently deliver, each written before this record existed and each never reconciled against it or
against `D-0105`. Extending this record's logic explicitly rather than treating each as a fresh
question:

- **The day-21 return session's IMPLEMENT branch** (`12-evidence-first-rebuild/120-RETURN-SESSION-DESIGN.md`
  §6) — a collective team-Radar build that runs automatically once day-18 capture data crosses a
  threshold, with no per-engagement privacy-review gate analogous to Route B's. This is the same
  category of mechanism as Route B and is covered by this record on identical terms: real, stays
  designed, **not integrated into the live candidate or run for a real cohort until it has the same
  gate Route B has** (or a decision that supersedes this one). The return session's TEACH/PRACTICE
  branches are unaffected — individual teaching and practice content, exactly what this record says
  Foundations should be doing, and are cleared to integrate.
- **The manager forum and 12-month engagement specification**
  (`13-sustaining-practice-layer-v1/00-...` and `01-...`) — treats organisation/team-level, routinely
  running engagement as the normal shape of the product, and separately assumes a quarterly cadence
  for every engaged client that `D-0105` (2026-08-21) restricts to selected case-study organisations
  only. Both conflicts predate this record and `D-0105` respectively, and neither has been
  reconciled since. **This specification stays explicitly out of scope for the live candidate** —
  real, retained, not built into `workshop-package.html` as a standing offer. If pursued later, it
  needs to be re-scoped against both `D-0105`'s case-study-only cadence and this record's
  individual-teaching commitment before it's positioned as anything a general client receives.

Nothing here forecloses either mechanism being built later, on the same terms as Route B — once a
cohort has run and there's a specific, resourced reason to invest in the collective/organisational
layer.

## What this clarifies, and does not amend

**`D-0091` is unchanged.** It correctly built the compressed Team Pulse mechanism and correctly
recorded it as a real capability. This record does not reopen or weaken that work.

**`D-0092` is clarified, not amended.** `D-0092` justified Foundations' extension to a half-day
partly *"so the redesigned environment mechanism (`D-0091`'s anonymous-reveal-and-agree beat) has
real time to run properly."* Read literally today, that framing implies the mechanism is a routine
part of what a buyer gets — which is not accurate given the gate in point 1 above. `D-0092`'s pricing
and length decision itself is not reopened here (the extra time is used well regardless, per the
2026-08-27 panel's own finding that the half-day's genuine strengths — the engineered peak, the
repeated teach-then-apply structure — are real and should not change). What's corrected is the
*reason given* for part of that extra time: it should not be read as "this format routinely delivers
collective/environment change," because it does not, absent a specific approved gate per engagement.

## What a stronger version of "environment readiness" would need, if pursued later

Named here so it isn't lost, not because it's authorised now: a standing, repeatable privacy-review
process for the Form 1 nomination-and-aggregation step (rather than a one-off per-engagement
approval); a tested aggregation method; at least one real cohort where Route B actually ran, to know
whether the mechanism works as designed; and a genuine leader/manager-facing component — none of
which currently exists. This is future-facing context, not a commitment or a timeline.

## Claim boundary

This record does not authorise describing Foundations, in any customer-facing material, as building
team culture, organisational change, or environment readiness as a routine feature of the product.
It authorises describing Foundations accurately: the deepest individual teaching of the three
formats, with a genuine (but gated, not routine) option for a facilitated team moment where a
specific engagement has separately cleared the privacy-review requirement. `K-08` continues to
govern all such statements.
