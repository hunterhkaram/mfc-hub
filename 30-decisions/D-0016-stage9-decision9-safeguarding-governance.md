# D-0016 — Stage 9 Decision 9: Safeguarding, Distress-response and Escalation Governance

**This decision is explicitly named by Hunter's own delegation instruction as a matter requiring Hunter's and the appropriate MFC governance authority's acceptance for the material safeguarding-risk components. This record adopts a recommended design-level and facilitator-scope model now, within delegated authority, and records — without fabricating an answer to — every component that genuinely requires qualified human, legal, or organisational validation before the mandatory pre-pilot gate can be considered resolved.**

## Decision question (verbatim, agenda)

> What organisational safeguarding, distress-response, referral, and escalation model will MFC adopt?

## What is decided now (design-level and facilitator-scope only)

- The workshop is explicitly framed as non-clinical education and skill practice.
- A standard, simple disclosure statement ("if something is troubling you, here is what to do") is given to every participant at the session's start (exact wording deferred to Stage 10).
- Facilitators (per `D-0013`'s own approved competency 9) are equipped to recognise when a participant situation exceeds workshop/facilitator scope and hand off to a defined pathway.
- Facilitators do not provide counselling, diagnosis, or clinical intervention (consistent with `D-0013`'s non-clinical-scope competency).
- Facilitators may pause, redirect, or stop an activity for immediate participant safety (already approved as safety-driven deviation under `D-0014` §5 — unchanged by this decision).
- The light deviation record approved under `D-0014` captures safety-driven deviations at the workshop-design layer, disclosed here as a partial input to Decision 9's own future documentation needs, not a substitute for organisational safeguarding documentation.

**This is an MFC product/operating decision for the above components only** — not evidence-validated, and not itself a complete safeguarding model.

## What is explicitly NOT decided, and requires qualified validation before the pre-pilot gate resolves

1. **Distress-response content and referral-pathway design** — requires a qualified mental-health/clinical-governance professional's input. No fabricated content is supplied.
2. **Escalation thresholds** — requires the same qualified input, informed by (1).
3. **Emergency-response procedure** — requires host- and venue-specific input and MFC's own operational/insurance risk assessment.
4. **Mandatory-reporting and jurisdiction-dependent legal requirements** — requires legal advice specific to MFC's actual Australian/NSW (or other) delivery jurisdiction(s). **Not determined by this task.**
5. **Host responsibilities** — requires agreement with each specific host, informed by (1)–(4).
6. **Insurance and clinical-governance sign-off** for whatever model results from (1)–(5).

**No facilitator may be cleared for participant-facing pilot delivery, and no pilot may proceed, until items 1–6 are resolved by the appropriate qualified authority and Hunter/the appropriate MFC governance authority formally approves the resulting complete model.**

## Preserved exactly

> MUST RESOLVE BEFORE PILOT-READY APPROVAL AND BEFORE ANY PARTICIPANT-FACING PILOT DELIVERY

This gate is **not satisfied, narrowed, or advanced** by this decision. It is carried forward unresolved, with items 1–6 above as the specific, named requirements that must be met to resolve it.
