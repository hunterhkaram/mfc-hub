# D-0089 — Two Confirmations and a Reopening

## Decision

Hunter, 2026-08-07, answering the three items put to him after the framework readiness pass:

> • Progression: Keep Learn It, Build It, Use It.
> • Values-Based Action: Keep Values-Based Action.
> • Reopen workshop: Yes — workshop workstream reopened as of today.

## 1. Progression naming — confirmed, and the reasoning changes

**Learn It, Build It, Use It stands.** This matters because Hunter confirmed it *with the contrary
evidence in front of him*, not before it surfaced.

`D-0061` renamed the third stage from "Live It" on a claims-safety argument: as an achieved
end-state it implied a durable outcome `K-09`'s stage gates prohibit claiming. `D-0086` then read
the Drive-authoritative source directly and found that argument weaker than it looked — the source's
own "Live It" section already carries the guardrail, verbatim: *"This does not mean mastery or
perfection."* The Practical Pathway, independently, also uses "Live It."

**What changes: the justification, not the decision.** The naming now rests on its own merits —
three stage names that are each an action a person takes (learn, build, use), where "Live It"
describes a state rather than an act — rather than on a claims-safety argument that the source
document had already answered internally. **The divergence from both Drive documents is accepted,
deliberate, and recorded rather than reconciled away.** Anyone reading the source and the framework
side by side will see two different third-stage names; that is now a known, chosen difference.

## 2. Values-Based Action — confirmed

**The deep-tier term stays "Values-Based Action"** (renamed from "Acting on What Matters" by
`D-0085`).

`D-0086` flagged a real risk: the Drive source uses "values-based action" only inside its
evidence-map layer — listing the academic processes underneath the skills — while deliberately
keeping the skill's own name as "Acting on What Matters." The concern was that renaming the skill to
match its evidence-map term could collapse two layers the source keeps apart, reintroducing one
layer down the same one-word-two-jobs problem `D-0084` had just removed at the bridge-label
boundary.

Hunter reviewed that and kept the rename. **The divergence is accepted and deliberate.** This layer
is not participant-facing, which bounds the consequence.

**Still open, not resolved by this:** Values-Based Action's tag carries only the second half of its
own definition — "identifying what matters" is implied rather than stated. Recorded in `K-07`'s
disclosed costs.

## 3. Workshop workstream — reopened

`Current State and Next Action` recorded the workshop as **paused, not cancelled**, resuming *"only
on Hunter's separate, explicit decision."* This is that decision, made explicitly rather than
inferred from the request to start building.

**What this authorises:** workshop build and design work, now.

**What it does NOT touch:** `D-0016`'s gate on participant-facing delivery and pilot-ready approval.
Its scope was verified verbatim during the readiness pass rather than assumed — it gates *"pilot-ready
approval and any participant-facing pilot delivery"*, and design and build sit outside it. **All six
of its matters remain open.** Nothing in this decision moves that gate, and no new approval
requirement is invented alongside it.

## Why the framework is a sound base to build on

The readiness pass preceding this decision confirmed the framework is internally consistent — five
public skills, seven deeper skills beneath, no live contradictions across the foundations records —
and that MFC's evidence honesty holds under adversarial checking. The independent checker attempted
to find overclaiming and could not.

**One genuine caveat, stated plainly:** all of it is accurate and none of it is formally approved.
Nothing in `00-foundations/` is board-approved, and `K-09`'s evidence posture still awaits specialist
confirmation from the two named reviewers. "Our evidence position" is defensible; "our *approved*
evidence position" is not, yet.
