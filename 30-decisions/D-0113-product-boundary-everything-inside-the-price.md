# D-0113. What the price buys

## The decision

**The three-week return session, the written report to the organisation, and
the manager session all sit INSIDE the product price.** They are not optional
extensions, not separately contracted, and not separately quoted.

**The manager session is not separately priced.** D-0111 records Hunter saying
it "needs its own product"; that is confirmed as a statement about the
session's *design seriousness and shared shape across both products*, not about
its commercial packaging. It remains bundled.

**Prices are unchanged and were never in question:** Foundations $3,900 base +
$95 per person, cap 20; Essentials $1,250 (2-5) / $1,550 (6-10) / $1,800
(11-15); Introduction $600 booked or free as capped outreach. Founder decision
2026-08-16, recorded in D-0103 and the Product and Pricing Book.

## Why this was needed

On 29 August three service packs were written (`maintained_by: Codex`, status
`proposed_for_hunter_decision`) defining each product's teachable core, with
"core exclusions" that placed the manager component, the return contact and the
routine data/report outside it. Independent verification on 2026-08-30 found:

- Read as a commercial boundary, those exclusions would move **8.25 to 9.25 of
  Foundations' 15.75 costed facilitator hours (52% to 59%)** and **3.5 to 3.75
  of Essentials' 8.25 hours (42% to 46%)** outside the priced product.
- The exclusions do **not** originate in D-0110 or D-0111. D-0110 excludes only
  the return session's IMPLEMENT branch and states the TEACH/PRACTICE branches
  "are cleared to integrate"; D-0111 upgrades rather than removes the manager
  session.
- The commercial reading was nonetheless stated, not merely implied, in
  `24-MEASURED-SERVICE-EXTENSIONS-v3.md` ("sellable candidate versions…
  alongside—not inside—the learning minutes"; manager components recorded as
  `not purchased` / `offered` / `delivered`).
- **No occurrence anywhere in the vault prices the manager session separately.**
  `FINAL-02-pricing-strategy.md` line 65 carries a Leaders' Session row, but its
  price column reads "Included" and its $139 is a *cost* figure. Everywhere else it
  is costed only as an input, at 1.5 facilitator hours inside both products.

So a component had been described as separately purchasable without ever
becoming a line item, while remaining charged for invisibly inside two products.

## Rationale

The return and the report are the product, not extras. The Product and Pricing
Book's competitive defence rests on them: **5.25 participant-contact hours at
$92.38 per person per contact hour at a ten-person cohort** ($67.62 at fifteen),
compared against a $3,500-$7,500 in-house Mental Health First Aid class. Stripping the return and report reduces that to
4.0 contact hours and the differentiation argument goes with it.

Reopening the price was explicitly rejected. It is decided, single-valued,
correct in the paper the committee holds, and sits before them as ask 1 of 4.
No buyer has ever been quoted anything. Reopening a correct price to resolve an
internal documentation inconsistency would spend founder and committee
credibility on a problem no external party has seen.

## Consequences and required propagation

1. The 29 August service packs remain valid as **facilitator scoping** (what
   happens in the room) and are **declined as a commercial boundary**. Their
   "core exclusions" wording must be qualified so it cannot be read as a
   pricing or contracting statement.
2. `24-MEASURED-SERVICE-EXTENSIONS-v3.md`'s `not purchased` manager-component
   status is superseded; the manager session is always included.
3. Stale worked examples computed from the superseded $3,500 base were
   corrected in `110-product-pricing-onepager.html` on 2026-08-30
   ($4,450 → $4,850, $4,925 → $5,325, $328 → $355 per person).
4. `FINAL-06-two-product-model.md` still carries the superseded pair in ten
   places, and `FINAL-09-committee-review.md` records a downstream arithmetic
   error ($5,000 − $4,450 stated as $930; the true difference at the current
   $4,850 is $150). **Open.**
5. `FINAL-02-pricing-strategy.md` remains stale on $2,500/$1,367. **Open.**
6. The Hub pricing and workshop-delivery projections need regenerating. The Hub
   integrity check was failing on unrelated structural drift as at 2026-08-30.

## What this decision does not do

It does not ratify any price. Committee approval of ask 1 remains outstanding
and is a real external gate. It is not market validation: no buyer has paid any
price and zero cohorts have run. It does not clear delivery, which remains
blocked on the escalation and distress-response procedures.

## Unit economics, confirmed 2026-08-30

**The manager session was already inside the price.** It is costed as 1.5
facilitator hours in Foundations (of 15.75) and 1.5 in Essentials (of 8.25).
D-0111's upgrade from 45 to 60 minutes adds 0.25 hours, **$16.53 per run at
$66.10, or $23.14 loaded**. No price increase is warranted.

**Precision note on that +0.25.** It is *verified* for Essentials: D-0111 supersedes a
45-minute Essentials-only briefing with a ~1 hour session, and the Book's 1.5 hours
implies roughly 0.75 delivery plus 0.75 prep, so a 15-minute delivery extension adds
exactly 0.25. It is *assumed* for Foundations, where no source states the leader
session was ever 45 minutes. The assumption errs conservative (it overstates cost).
It may also understate, because D-0111 asks for "a proper meeting, a proper
conversation" with manager-specific implementation planning, which is plausibly more
prep rather than only more delivery. Unifying one design across both products is a
build-time saving, not a per-run one, so no per-run credit is taken. The swing either
way is roughly $16 to $50 a run against $3,000+ retained cash, so the conclusion holds
under every reading.
