## What actually changed, in one paragraph

Four product-architecture decisions landed (D-0109–D-0112): WHO-5 made routine, Foundations
repositioned as individual teaching not organisational readiness, the three products' architecture
clarified (Introduction is a standalone talk; Essentials/Foundations share one thesis at different
depth), and the take-home leave-behind redesigned around how people actually behave (premade, cue
written on their own phone, not a card nobody carries). A three-way debate on core-session time
allocation was reconciled and independently checked (a real contradiction was caught and fixed
before it reached Hunter). A four-pass build-readiness audit then found: session-script content is
built for all three products; visual/print production and facilitator-training/safeguarding
procedures are MISSING for all three, and the second is a **hard blocker** — no facilitator,
including Hunter, can currently be cleared to deliver, because the required escalation/distress
procedures don't exist. A first draft of those procedures was written (not cleared — needs
qualified clinical/legal review). The manager session was rebuilt to the new shared ~1hr spec. Two
slide decks were built from scratch (Introduction, Essentials — neither existed before). Content
was added addressing participants who already meditate/journal, plus a comprehension check. Every
substantive change went through an independent fresh-context check before landing; several real
defects were caught and fixed this way (see "What broke and was fixed" below).

## What broke and was fixed — worth knowing before trusting anything blindly

1. **`workshop-package.html` was found corrupted mid-session** — its entire body content tripled,
   with different edit states in each copy. Most likely a concurrent-write/sync collision on this
   iCloud-synced vault path, not a logic error in any single edit. Repaired by reconstructing from
   the one structurally complete copy; every change from the session was verified to have survived.
   **This root cause is not fixed and could recur on any large scripted multi-write to this path.**
   If you're about to make many rapid edits to a large file in this vault, consider committing
   first so recovery is trivial if it happens again.
2. The Hub's headline status fields (`current_truth`/`state`/`readiness`/`next_action`) were found
   frozen 10 days stale despite every change being logged correctly underneath — the reconciliation
   tool only appends change history, it doesn't rewrite the summary. Fixed by hand-editing the
   register directly. **This will drift stale again** unless whoever does the next material change
   also rewrites these fields, not just files a reconciliation receipt.
3. Multiple independent-check passes caught real, specific defects before they reached Hunter — a
   fabricated write-in space on the leave-behind, an internal contradiction in a time-allocation
   score, a structural failure where the "shared" manager session had no route from one product's
   navigation, a Human Operating System beat that named six components and defined none, evidence
   overreach in citing a single trial as "the only" design in its space (three separate instances,
   found across two review rounds). None of these were caught by the first pass — they needed a
   genuinely separate reader. **Do not skip the independent-check step on future changes just
   because the first draft looks complete.**
