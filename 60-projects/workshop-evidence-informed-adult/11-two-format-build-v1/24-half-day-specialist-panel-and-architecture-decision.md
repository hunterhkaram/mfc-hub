## Founder summary

The current Half-Day is not a bad workshop and it does not need to be rebuilt from zero. It has a
strong human premise, a coherent three-block journey, meaningful practice, careful boundaries and
substantial prior research. The weakness is more specific: **it still behaves too often like a
well-explained curriculum followed by activities, rather than a deliberately interleaved learning
experience.**

The strongest architecture is a **guided recognition–explanation–application spiral**:

1. begin with an ordinary moment people recognise;
2. let them notice or generate something safely;
3. explain only what helps them understand that experience;
4. show one neutral worked example;
5. retrieve the idea through more than one participation mode;
6. use it privately; and
7. return later with greater realism and less support.

This is an evolutionary optimisation, not a parallel workshop. It preserves Hunter's decision that
the meaning must not be stripped out, while correcting the current package's longest stretches of
explanation and school-like retrieval.

## Architecture comparison

| Candidate | What the panel found | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| A. Education-led | Strong on meaning and framework integrity; weakest on attention, energy and delay before meaningful use. | **Narrow, do not discard.** |
| B. Experience first | Strong on relevance; too risky if experience is unguided, personal or treated as broad productive failure. | **Reject as the whole architecture.** |
| C. Recognition–experience–explanation spiral | Best balance of meaning, dignity, retrieval, application and room energy when experiences are bounded and facilitator-supplied. | **Adopt as the organising architecture.** |
| Hybrid A+C | Keeps the three blocks and full conceptual spine, but converts long explanation/drill sequences into guided cycles. | **Recommended implementation.** |

The evidence supports principles—segmentation, signalling, worked examples, explanatory feedback,
retrieval and varied practice. It does not scientifically specify a slide count, exact minute ratio
or perfect workshop sequence. The recommendation is a reasoned product judgement constrained by
that evidence and MFC's approved intent.

## Reconciliation decisions

### Accepted and implemented

1. Restored the Radar to exactly three personally meaningful moments total, with one possible next
   move for each; one chosen cue may support one priority moment.
2. Replaced the most mechanical part of the traffic-light drill with a neutral worked example and
   multimodal retrieval while retaining reconstruction and the three corrections.
3. Replaced absolute or unsupported claims: “Thoughts aren't facts” and “most people's first attempt
   fades.”
4. Made private participation the routine floor; shared writing and team Radar remain separately
   gated modules.
5. Named the architecture as the A+C guided spiral and preserved the reasoning here.

### Accepted as testing requirements, not implemented claims

- actual room flow, cognitive load, projection and facilitator recovery;
- participant comprehension, dignity, accessibility and findability;
- exact Form 2 burden and question comprehension;
- initial real-world opportunity and attempted use; and
- whether exactly three Radar moments feel manageable and useful.

### Preserved boundaries

- D-0016's six original matters remain unresolved; the digital follow-up exposure remains open.
- AI screening does not satisfy clinical, legal, privacy, safeguarding, insurance or accessibility
  approval.
- The workshop cannot claim habit, prevention, wellbeing improvement, competence or organisational
  change.
- The shared/team route is not the routine D-0100 measurement pathway.

### Rejected or deferred

- Rebuild the entire workshop as an unguided experience-first format — rejected as unnecessarily
  risky and inconsistent with Hunter's meaning-first correction.
- Reduce the deck to a target number — rejected; the count follows room and learning jobs.
- Add more theories, tools or a deeper second product before the first pilot — deferred.
- Treat this same-context role panel as independent professional or founder assurance — rejected.
