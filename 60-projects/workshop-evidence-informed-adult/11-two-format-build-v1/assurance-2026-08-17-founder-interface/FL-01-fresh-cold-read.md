## Position

**This mostly does what I asked for, and does it with real fidelity — but it is an addition, not a
correction, and that gap matters.**

I checked every one of the 25 timed beats in `walk3`'s three blocks against `decide3`'s three
tables. All 25 are present, in the same order, with the same time ranges, with no beat invented and
none dropped. Where I could check the authority tag against the underlying walkthrough text, it
held up: "a teaching model, not a brain map" is a near-verbatim match to `walk3`'s own line on the
Driver/Passenger/Engine beat; "the cue's actual effectiveness is untested" for the reminder-card
beat matches `walk3` word-for-word; Route B being disabled by default, the Radar's "three is
Hunter's approved MFC pilot structure, not a scientifically optimal count," and the D-0100 form
placement all match their `walk3` originals exactly. The four-way authority legend (approved
decision / external evidence / MFC design judgement / working hypothesis) is exactly the
distinction I asked Hunter to be able to see, stated in plain language, not jargon. The closing
"what this page is not" section explicitly disclaims that anything is resolved, proven optimal, or
delivery-ready, and links back to my original finding rather than silently absorbing it — that is
the honest self-limiting move I look for and it is genuinely there.

So on the narrow question of "does this table tell the truth about the beats it covers," yes.

But my original recommendation was not "add a legible table somewhere." It named an exact location:
**"replace the current Half-Day `rat3` and `plan3` views... with a current founder-decision
interface."** What was built instead sits alongside `rat3` and `plan3`, unchanged, and says so
outright: *"It replaces nothing — the Walkthrough, Rationale and Measurement tabs are unchanged and
still the operational content."* My original complaint was specifically that the **existing**
Rationale view carried stale, overconfident content — the 63:37 ratio discussed as if it had design
authority, movement-scale benefit implied, older butcher-paper and habit language, measurement tabs
contradicting D-0100. None of that was in scope for me to re-check today (I was pointed only at
`walk3` and `decide3`), and `decide3` itself confirms those tabs are untouched. That means the exact
artifact I flagged as capable of misleading Hunter is still one click away, unchanged, still
carrying whatever it carried on 2026-08-13 — and nothing on `decide3` warns a reader who lands on
`rat3` first, rather than `decide3` first, that the older tab may say something the new one has
since corrected.

## Strongest objection

`decide3` is additive, not corrective. It is a good, honest new front door — but the house behind it
(`rat3`, `plan3`) was never confirmed to have had its stale claims fixed, only left in place with a
newer, better-organised page built next to it. If Hunter (or a future reader) opens "Rationale &
Evidence" directly instead of "Founder Decision" — which is exactly as likely, since it's the very
next nav button — they can still land on the same reconciliation burden I originally flagged, with
no signpost on that tab pointing them to the corrected view. A one-line banner on `rat3` and `plan3`
("superseded for authority/evidence questions — see Founder Decision") would close this for near
zero cost. Without it, this is a real, not cosmetic, gap between what was asked for and what was
built.

## Evidence strength and limitations

I can verify beat-for-beat textual fidelity between `decide3` and `walk3` with high confidence —
that was a direct, exhaustive read of both, not a sample. I cannot verify from this reading alone:
whether the external-evidence citations (generation effect, Bandura mastery experience, Wood &
Neal habit formation, implementation intentions) are accurately sourced — that is A-10/evidence-desk
territory, not mine, and the citations themselves are plausible but I have not traced them to a
source record. Whether `rat3`/`plan3` still contain the specific stale claims from my original
review — genuinely unknown from what I read today; the page's own honesty note ("Walkthrough,
Rationale and Measurement tabs are unchanged") is the only evidence I have, and it argues the
problem is still there rather than that it's been checked and found harmless.

## Alternatives considered

- Replace `rat3`/`plan3` outright, exactly as I originally asked — highest fidelity to the
  recommendation, but loses whatever operational detail lives only in those tabs and is a bigger,
  riskier edit.
- Add `decide3` as a new view and leave the others as historical record but visibly marked
  superseded — most of the benefit of the chosen approach, without the open contradiction risk.
  This is the fix I'm recommending below.
- Do nothing beyond a written reconciliation memo (the linked
  `25-half-day-whole-bench-reconciliation-and-closure.md`) without touching the interactive package
  at all — would have left Hunter back exactly where my original review found him: reading a memo
  instead of the artifact he'll actually decide from. Correctly rejected.

## Recommendation

**Accept with one named fix, not accept-as-is.** The `decide3` content itself is faithful,
well-hedged, and answers the authority question I asked for. Before this is presented to Hunter as
"the founder-decision interface is done," add a short pointer on `rat3` and `plan3` directing to
`decide3` for the authority/evidence/objection view — otherwise the exact defect this page exists to
fix is still reachable by the most obvious alternative click. This is a small, bounded, low-risk
edit, not a rebuild.

## What would change this view

Confirmation (a direct read of `rat3` and `plan3` against my 2026-08-13 findings) that the specific
stale claims I flagged there have actually been corrected at the source, not just superseded
elsewhere, would remove my strongest objection entirely. Absent that, the objection stands as a
disclosed risk, not a confirmed defect.

## Proposed learning

Candidate update: when a specialist recommendation names an exact remedy location ("replace view
X"), and the delivered fix instead adds a new view alongside X, the delivery is not complete until
either X is corrected to match, or X is visibly marked superseded — an additive fix that leaves the
originally-flagged artifact reachable and unmarked reproduces the exact reconciliation burden the
new artifact was built to remove. Whether this rises to a numbered `80-learning/` lesson (versus a
one-off note) should be judged against how often the "add alongside instead of replace/mark" pattern
recurs elsewhere in this vault's remediation work — I flag it here rather than binding it, since one
observed instance is not yet a repeatable-cause finding.
