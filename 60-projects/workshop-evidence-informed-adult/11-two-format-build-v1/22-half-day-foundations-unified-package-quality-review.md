# Half-Day Foundations unified package — quality review

## Review contract

- **Decision under review:** may this package become the single internal Half-Day Foundations
  candidate and progress to independent review and timed rehearsal?
- **Artifacts checked:** current operational arc, 80-screen HTML deck, current facilitator guide,
  current participant-material copy pack and deck preflight/assurance record.
- **Authority checked:** D-0091, D-0094, D-0098, D-0100; K-04, K-05, K-07, K-08; founder/Gina and
  copywriting controls named in the preflight record; existing accessibility specification.
- **Not claimed:** this is an integrated Quality Governor pass in the production context, not a
  fresh independent specialist review and not Hunter, Gina, creative, clinical, safeguarding,
  privacy/legal, insurer or host approval.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH CONDITIONS — internal progression only.** The package is coherent enough to replace
the previous split internal candidates and to progress to fresh independent review and a full
timed rehearsal. It is **not cleared for participant delivery**.

**Scope correction, 2026-08-13:** “internal progression” does not mean the current architecture has
been optimised or approved by Hunter. Before rehearsal is treated as the next design step, the
package must pass the mandatory design-intelligence stage: compare credible architectures, screen
the full bench, execute the relevant bounded challenges, reconcile them and revise the artifact.
This review remains valid evidence of internal coherence; it is not evidence of optimality.

## Prioritised findings

### Blocking participant delivery

1. The participant/privacy/support card contains deliberately unresolved text. No participant may
   receive it until qualified wording and cohort-specific routes are inserted and approved.
2. Measurement forms are described but not approved cohort-frozen field instruments. Privacy,
   safeguarding, adviser, comprehension, storage, retention and access controls remain open.
3. The butcher-paper opening may expose contributions within a table or room. A qualified cohort
   decision and rehearsal must choose or replace that route; the current guide correctly stops if
   it is unresolved.
4. No full timed room rehearsal, projector-distance test, facilitator-load observation or real
   participant comprehension/accessibility test has been completed.

### Required before the build can be called production-complete

5. Convert the controlled participant-copy pack into accessible print-ready artifacts only after
   the blocked wording is approved. Copy completeness is not the same as production completeness.
6. Name and stage the actual contrast-demonstration asset: approved film or a fully rehearsed
   two-facilitator scene. Do not improvise it on delivery day.
7. Run a fresh independent artifact review across deck, guide and participant materials together.
   The prior specialist work informed the build but is not fresh assurance on this version.

### Important design observations

8. Eighty screens is not a quality target. The count is justified by projected-text legibility,
   retrieval pacing and one-job-per-screen. Independent review or rehearsal may add, combine or
   remove screens where evidence supports the change.
9. The shared/team Radar is a separate governed module, not a normal feature of the session. The
   default route is now the individual Radar; this prevents the workshop from inventing data that
   the approved measurement core never collected.
10. Form 2 occurs after the instructional close. Room booking, host expectation and participant
    consent therefore need to cover approximately four additional minutes even though those
    minutes are excluded from the 113:67 teaching balance.

## Progression conditions

The current package may be treated as the sole internal candidate only while all of the following
remain true:

- the live package verifier passes;
- participant-delivery status remains fail-closed;
- future changes are made against the current operational arc and lifecycle pointers;
- the next review is explicitly independent of this production pass;
- rehearsal and qualified-human gates are recorded as evidence, not inferred from artifact quality.

Any failure returns the package to **REVISE**. Only Hunter or the named qualified owner may approve
the decisions reserved to them.
