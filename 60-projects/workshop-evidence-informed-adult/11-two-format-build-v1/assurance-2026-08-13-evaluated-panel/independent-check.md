### Major 1 — The specialist loop is incomplete and execution assurance is overstated

**Evidence:** 25 roles are registered; this folder contains 24 task packets and 24 reviews. SYS-04 has neither. Both synthesis files explicitly await SYS-04 reconciliation. Fourteen packets/reviews use a reduced format that does not satisfy the complete controlled call contract, and the consequence receipt describes one performer context for fourteen roles.

**Consequence:** there is no authoritative accept/narrow/reject/unresolved/escalate disposition, no verified learning return, and no defensible basis for saying the complete evaluated panel has run.

**Smallest effective response:** do not regenerate the workshop or invent missing expertise. Correctly classify the two producer halves as same-context role-lens panels unless distinct execution receipts exist; rerun only the contract-incomplete decision-bearing calls that must carry separate specialist assurance; then give SYS-04 one complete reconciliation packet containing the producer outputs, this independent check, minority concerns and exact current fingerprints. Preserve all rejected and deferred advice.

### Major 2 — Active measurement architecture conflicts with D-0100

**Reproduced in `workshop-package.html`:** the Half-Day and Introduction measurement views call themselves “Finalised” and “decided,” retain WHO-5, a personal-facts backup code, routine team nomination, day-90 material and near-100% response claims (anchors include lines 722–724, 737–748, 807–826, 942–983, 1052–1095, 1421–1475, 1527, 1574–1586 and 1611). D-0100 removes WHO-5 and broad non-core research from the routine first-pilot core, requires a random system ID with separately controlled key or unlinked anonymous fallback, and does not establish near-complete response.

**Active versus historical:** this is a genuine active conflict, not harmless history, because the views are labelled finalised/decided/current and the manifest names the HTML as the canonical arc.

**Cross-artifact check:** the current guide and materials point to D-0100 and correctly keep exact forms unapproved; the deck contains only a bounded optional Form 2 screen. The defect is concentrated in the canonical HTML and its provenance, not the current delivery script.

**Smallest effective response:** replace the active measurement projections in both format views with one concise D-0100-derived current summary and links; preserve superseded detail only behind an explicit historical label that cannot be mistaken for an operational specification. Do not design new forms.

### Major 3 — The canonical HTML instructs a participation route that the current guide forbids

**Reproduced:** `workshop-package.html` line 562 allows “a show of hands or a quick read of the room” for shared-Radar appetite. The current guide at the `3:04–3:10` Route B section requires a private anonymous response and explicitly prohibits raised hands, spoken agreement, visible positioning and majority vote. The deck’s “A private choice” screen agrees with the guide.

The line-619 “Butcher's paper, not anonymous cards” rationale is more nuanced: it labels itself a design argument and says safety is unresolved. It is therefore not an unlabelled operational instruction by itself. It still creates a major retrieval risk because it valorises shoulder-reading inside a page whose footer calls it the current arc and room plan, while the active walkthrough at line 415 and current guide prohibit that behaviour.

**Smallest effective response:** replace the active shared-Radar appetite instruction with the current private/anonymous rule. Recast the butcher-paper paragraph as explicitly superseded historical rationale or move it to dated design history; do not delete the reasoning. The conditional shared route may remain only when cohort-specific privacy/access/participation approval and an approved decision rule exist.

### Major 4 — Active language overstates behavioural transfer and wider impact

**Reproduced:** lines 364–365 universalise what adults were taught and link untrained moments to burnout, conflict, sector burden and fewer costly moments at scale; lines 388–389 call practice no-time and a habit-stacking mechanism; line 502 says participants have done thousands of reps; lines 525–528 imply easy-moment practice will be available in hard moments; line 588 repeats no-extra-time; line 595 says “a practice you keep”; evidence rows 697, 700–702 present conditional mechanisms with causal confidence; equivalent Introduction claims appear at 1179–1218.

**Cross-artifact check:** the current guide explicitly rejects thousands-of-reps and habit guarantees, recognises attention/effort cost, and the current deck uses “practice you can return to.” Participant materials do not carry the scaled claims. The panel correctly located a canonical-arc conflict, not a package-wide need to rebuild the teaching sequence.

**Smallest effective response:** mirror the bounded guide/deck wording; distinguish approved organisational position, mechanism-informed design, working contribution hypothesis and untested outcome. Add one proximal logic chain without claiming causation. Preserve the movement ambition as a testable hypothesis with required conditions and rival explanations.

### Major 5 — The governing decision set omits D-0099 and misstates the safeguarding authority

**Evidence:** the frozen receipt and many packets treat D-0016 as the current approved gate. D-0099 is the later controlling decision: it closes/supersedes D-0016’s formal strategy/build-order framing while explicitly leaving the six underlying delivery protections unresolved. The package also says “D-0016’s safeguarding gate still has to clear” at line 710 and uses similar wording elsewhere.

**Consequence:** the panel reaches the right practical delivery boundary for the wrong current decision description. That is precisely the kind of authority drift the knowledge system is supposed to prevent.

**Smallest effective response:** add D-0099 to the controlled corpus and state the distinction exactly: D-0016 is historical and no longer a formal blocker on strategy/pricing/build order; its unresolved substantive protections remain requirements before real participant delivery. Recheck only roles whose conclusions depend on that authority framing.

### Major 6 — Provenance is broken and the verifier provides false semantic assurance

**Evidence:** `package-manifest.yml` cites `assurance-2026-08-13/SYS-04-integrated-recommendation-draft.md`, which does not exist; the final file exists instead. Despite the active conflicts above and that broken path, `verify_half_day_workshop_package.py` reports that the arc, deck, guide and participant copy “agree.”

**Smallest effective response:** point the manifest to the actual final record; extend the verifier to fail on broken evidence paths and exact semantic invariants for lifecycle, D-0100 measurement, private shared-Radar choice, D-0099/D-0016 framing, bounded behaviour/impact language and closing copy. The checker must report conflicts, not automatically select meaning.

### Minor 1 — Pair-choice equivalence is not yet explicit enough to call pressure-free

The guide permits passing, staying with notes or using a neutral example; the deck says “If you want to.” That is a reasonable internal design. Kai’s stronger “choose your own partner or remain solo; both routes receive the same time and closure” is a plausible refinement, not yet supported as required by participant evidence. Preserve it as a participant-test candidate rather than automatically modifying the package.

### Minor 2 — The ratio and screen count remain facts, not validated design choices

The HTML explains 63:37 as if “education is the intervention,” while the manifest appropriately says 80 is derived, not a target. Neither ratio nor count is proven optimal. Keep both descriptive and test density, transitions and retrieval in rehearsal/participant research; do not reduce or defend them by assertion.
