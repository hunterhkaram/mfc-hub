# A-20 Grace — theory-of-change review
## Bounded question
Are workshop features linked to honest proximal outcomes without causal leaps?
## Sources reviewed
Exact packet sources; outcomes trace; D-0100; complete package arc.
## Professional principles applied
Activity→mechanism→proximal signal, contribution not attribution, rival explanations.
## Position and strongest objection
The package can plausibly teach recognition, language, guided retrieval and one attempted application. It cannot bridge from those proximal events to habit, wellbeing, prevention, safer workplaces or social change.
## Findings
- **retain** — Neutral example, retrieval, private rep, Radar and cue each have a plausible proximal job. Evidence: deck Blocks 2–3 and outcome traceability matrix.
- **retain** — D-0100 measures comprehension, recall, opportunity, attempted use, barriers/supports rather than wellbeing. Evidence: D-0100 decision.
- **reject** — Attribution from attendance or participant story to wider impact.
- **unresolved** — Whether participants comprehend/retrieve/use the mechanisms and which rival explanation (novelty, facilitator, social desirability) accounts for signals.
- **change** — Pilot interpretation plan must list at least these rival explanations and state which result changes which feature. Evidence: current package contains measurement boundaries but no completed pilot interpretation record.
- **human gate** — Hunter on public impact claims; H-02 where fundraising representations arise.
## Evidence strength and limitations
Coherent internal logic, no direct outcomes.
## Alternatives considered
Remove all impact language rejected; wider impacts remain hypotheses outside workshop claims.
## Recommendation
Retain package; constrain pilot interpretation.
## What would change this view
Longitudinal controlled or triangulated implementation evidence.
