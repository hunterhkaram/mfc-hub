## Founder and Gina language control

The candidate preserves the locked definition, expansion, five skill sentences, Stop–Shift–Do
meaning, State Check question, mental-rep definition, Passenger line and Radar prompt. It uses
Hunter's physical-health/fitness parallel and his mechanism order. It restores Gina-endorsed
anchors: “No one is born knowing how to handle every human moment,” skills learned through
guidance/observation/experience/practice/repetition, and the difficult/ordinary/joyful moments
spectrum.

Shared-human explanations prefer we/us; direct exercises use you. “Workable” is glossed where it is
first taught. The deck avoids production labels, the seven deeper skill names, unexplained
proper-nounification and deficit language. One language change made during assurance replaced
“feelings are valid” with the more precise and inclusive “feelings can carry information about what
matters to us; they are not instructions.” This does not claim that Gina independently reviewed the
deck.
