## Material specialist disagreements — carried, not smoothed

1. **A-10 vs. the adopted design on Human Operating System scope.** A-10's professional position is
   that the HOS should not be a taught segment at all, only a compressed framing device, because no
   behaviour on the outcomes spine requires a participant to reproduce the model. **The adopted
   design does not follow this.** Hunter has stated the HOS is an important teaching metaphor
   (`L-026`) — that is the deciding input, and it aligns with `K-07`, which names the HOS as a
   first-class architectural layer and as a teaching model participants never need to reproduce.
   A-10's objection is preserved here rather than resolved away: it is a real cost, paid
   deliberately. Allocation landed at 18 min of 180 in the 3-hour (10%) and 6 min of 60 in the
   1-hour, with three of six components taught at the shorter length.
2. **A-09/A-16 on 1-hour viability.** Judged unable to support habit formation as a standalone
   equivalent. **Accepted and acted on** — the 1-hour is positioned and measured as an
   education-and-entry format, not a peer product. This matches Hunter's own position independently.
3. **A-11 on follow-up timing.** Recommended 6 weeks over the approved 3–4. **Accepted**, with the
   3-week non-outcome re-entry touch as mitigation. Changes the timing of an essential function and
   is therefore recorded in the closing decision record.
