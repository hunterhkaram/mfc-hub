# MFC final service pack — Introduction v1

**Product:** 60-minute broad adult public first encounter  
**Core boundary:** no required participant material, writing, QR, data, follow-up or real-world-use claim.  
**Normative exact-content companions:** facilitator packet v1 and Operations spec v1. Score v6 is audit evidence only.

## Final slide-by-slide sequence

| Slide | Time | Participant experience | Exact learning job | Facilitator instruction |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2 | arrive/observe | permission | “You can join in, think privately, use a fictional example or listen.” |
| 2 | 2–5 | understand scope | non-clinical/no-blame boundary | Name support, safety and context; no disclosure invitation. |
| 3 | 5–8 | recognise | ordinary moment 1 | Fictional message reaction. |
| 4 | 8–12 | recognise | ordinary moment 2 | Fictional conversation/scrolling alternative; quiet recognition only. |
| 5 | 12–16 | reframe | difficulty is learned/contextual, not a defect | State that access varies with capacity, safety, time and relationships. |
| 6 | 16–20 | distinguish | mental health / mental fitness boundary | No diagnosis or outcome claim. |
| 7 | 20–23 | understand | Stop | Notice the moment; not “calm down.” |
| 8 | 23–27 | understand | Shift | Available space/perspective; optional, including support/distance. |
| 9 | 27–31 | understand | Do + worked message | Use the packet’s fictional upsetting-message example; outcome does not decide whether it counts. |
| 10 | 31–41 | guided recall | distinguish process/context/action | Fixed prompt: “You are about to walk into a conversation you are already dreading. You notice your body tightening and your mind rushing ahead.” Prompt Stop, then Shift, then Do; gentle correction only. |
| 11 | 41–52 | guided rehearsal | safe fictional application | Use S5; switch to S1 if trigger applies; call it guided rehearsal, never a Mental Rep. |
| 12 | 52–57 | future bridge | no obligation | A moment may arise or not; support/delay/non-action remain valid. |
| 13 | 57–60 | unaided retrieval/close | recall core language | Hide visual; five silent seconds; reveal/correct only afterwards. |

## Participant-facing non-negotiables

- No participant writes, identifies themselves, shares a personal moment or completes a form.
- S5 is the default rehearsal; S1 is the explicit safe switch.
- The session ends in the room. Nothing needs downloading, taking home or reporting.

## Final product claim

“A one-hour, human first encounter that gives people a simple language for noticing an everyday moment and choosing what to do next when it is useful.”

**Not:** treatment, a wellbeing intervention, proof of use, a team/culture programme, or a follow-up service.
