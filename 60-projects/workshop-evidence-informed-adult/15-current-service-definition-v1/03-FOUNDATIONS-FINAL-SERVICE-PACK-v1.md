# MFC final service pack — Foundations v1

**Product:** half-day (270-minute) deepest individual teaching service  
**Core boundary:** individual learning in a group; not Route B, manager development, culture change or wellbeing intervention.  
**Normative exact-content companions:** facilitator packet v1 and Operations spec v1. Score v6 is audit evidence only.

## Core exclusions

No mandatory sharing, Route B, collective commitment, manager component, routine data, return contact or culture/wellbeing promise is part of this core pack.

## Final product claim

“A half-day, individual-learning service that gives people more time to retrieve, vary, troubleshoot and privately apply Stop–Shift–Do without treating barriers as personal failure.”
