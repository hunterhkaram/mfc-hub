# MFC final service pack — Essentials v1

**Product:** 90-minute contextual team learning service  
**Core boundary:** private participation; no manager-visible personal material; optional measurement/return and manager component are outside this core.  
**Normative exact-content companions:** facilitator packet v1 and Operations spec v1. Score v6 is audit evidence only.

## Pre-session tailoring record

Record setting, accessibility/language needs, selected S-ID 1 and S-ID 2, avoided scenario/reason, fallback, and whether paired work is appropriate. Never request individual health/personal moment information.

## Final slide-by-slide sequence

| Slide | Time | Learning job | Delivery |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1–2 | 0–8 | permission/scope | private/pass/observe route; not therapy or performance management. |
| 3–4 | 8–18 | contextual recognition | two selected fictional context moments; capacity/safety/workload boundary. |
| 5 | 18–22 | Stop | notice, not control feelings. |
| 6 | 22–27 | Shift | optional space/perspective/support/distance. |
| 7 | 27–31 | Do | intentional next move; outcome-independent. |
| 8 | 31–42 | discrimination | Fixed case: “A deadline moves; someone says, ‘Just deal with it.’” Distinguish moment, next move and support condition. |
| 9 | 42–56 | rehearsal | Recorded selected S-ID 1; use Stage 1 then Stage 2 where supplied. Paired/small-group with observe, silent or whole-group fictional-analysis fallback. |
| 10 | 56–68 | private translation | future/recent/hypothetical route, uncollected. |
| 11 | 68–78 | conditions rehearsal | selected S-ID 2; support, delay and non-action are valid. |
| 12 | 78–86 | honest hand-off | no opportunity/use is not a moral score; no promise of team change. |
| 13 | 86–90 | unaided retrieval/close | Hide/turn visual, allow five quiet seconds and pass/answer opportunity, then reveal/correct using Operations protocol. |

## Core exclusions

No baseline/post form in the 90 minutes; no participant plan/card handed to manager; no promised week-3 return; no team/culture outcome claim.

## Final product claim

“A context-aware 90-minute learning service that helps a team understand and privately rehearse a simple process for everyday moments—without making personal disclosure or organisational-change promises.”
