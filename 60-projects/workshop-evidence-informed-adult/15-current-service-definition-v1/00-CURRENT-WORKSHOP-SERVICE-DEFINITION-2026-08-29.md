## What the 90/100 means

Each service core scores **90/100 for internal artefact quality only**. The separate measurement-forms candidate is not given a product-effectiveness score: it is a reviewer-ready proposed evaluation system, not a fielded service or an evidence-of-effectiveness claim. The score assesses what can be made and checked now: definition, sequence, learning jobs, transfer design, measurement logic, accessibility/claim safeguards and evidence traceability. It does **not** claim participant effectiveness, real-world use, delivery readiness, clinical/legal/privacy clearance, partner readiness or founder approval.

The score is deliberately capped at 90 until new internal work is done: independent challenge of material uncertainty, terminology, retrieval/bridge alternatives, whole-journey access/adverse effects, and blind source/alternative review. Those are product-improvement tasks, not later external testing gates.
