# MFC Foundations — half-day facilitator packet v1

**Status:** internal product candidate; deepest individual-teaching service  
**Routine scope:** individual learning in a group setting. It is not a culture, manager or environment-readiness intervention.

**Required companion:** use `MFC-PACKET-OPERATIONS-SPEC-v1.md` for host/access preparation, canonical S-ID scenarios, staged-reveal retrieval text, scenario selection and fallback rules. The companion replaces the shorthand A–D card table below.

## Core promise

Foundations provides the shared Stop–Shift–Do spine at a slower pace and adds retrieval under variation, private translation, fallback planning and non-blaming troubleshooting. The added time must not become extra explanation, group confession, organisational diagnosis or vague reflection.

## Detailed run sheet

| Time | Block and script direction | Distinct individual-learning value | Fallback/guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | Welcome, permission, scope. State: “You may pass, observe or use a fictional situation. This is not a demand to solve every hard situation yourself.” Name support/safety boundary. | shared safety and product scope | No personal check-in round. |
| 15–30 | Recognition: offer three ordinary fictional moments; distinguish moment from story, trait or outcome. | corrects category errors before teaching | If examples do not fit, invite a neutral invented example. |
| 30–45 | Teach Stop–Shift–Do exactly as in Essentials, with two contrasting worked examples. Say: “Shift may be a pause, support, distance, a question or recognising that this is not yours to solve alone.” | permits slower conceptual correction | Never require a specific Shift. |
| 45–55 | Individual unaided retrieval: participants silently recall the three words, then teach them back with a fictional card or in pairs. | makes retrieval visible | Observe/pass route; do not score participants. |
| 55–70 | Discrimination drill: identify the moment, distinguish a next move from an outcome, and name a context/support condition. | distinguishes the constructs that later measurement must not collapse | Use whole group if paired work is unwelcome. |
| 70–85 | Break | pacing | — |
| 85–100 | Scenario rehearsal A: conversation tension. Generate more than one Stop/Shift/Do route. | variation in a relational moment | Safety/leave/support are valid routes. |
| 100–115 | Scenario rehearsal B: overload/competing priorities. Generate routes where the first intended move is unavailable. | flexibility, not repetition | No advice about workload as personal failure. |
| 115–130 | Private translation: future/recent/hypothetical moment; silently name first and fallback next moves. | relevance with privacy | No sharing or collection. |
| 130–145 | Pair or solo explanation: explain the process using a neutral example. Receive facilitator correction. | retrieval plus teach-back | Use prompt cards; pass allowed. |
| 145–165 | Break | pacing | — |
| 165–185 | Breakpoint map: not noticed / no workable Shift / move unclear / capacity or safety barrier / no opportunity. State: “These are places to understand the situation, not grades of the person.” | non-blaming diagnosis | Participants can analyse fictional situations only. |
| 185–210 | Troubleshooting rehearsal: scenario cards where an obvious action is unsafe, unavailable or inappropriate; choose another route, support, delay or non-action. | explicitly practises context-sensitive flexibility | Avoid hero narratives. |
| 210–230 | Integration: spaced unaided retrieval then a neutral case in which participants identify process, context and outcome separately. | consolidates precise language | Correct drift openly and briefly. |
| 230–245 | Future bridge: optional private cue; distinguish intention, opportunity and a real-world event. “No opportunity and no use are not the same.” | protects later interpretation | No required commitment/return. |
| 245–270 | Close: repeat core distinction and boundaries; invite support where needed. | consolidation and safe close | No routine form or day-21 contact embedded. |

## Scenario source

Select three canonical S-IDs and a fallback under the companion operations specification. Use its exact staged-reveal text for F10. No separate A–D card asset exists.

## Facilitation guardrails

1. Keep private reflection private; no person needs to explain a choice or barrier.
2. Do not call a rehearsal, plan or teach-back a Mental Rep.
3. Do not imply that more practice guarantees access, success or wellbeing.
4. Do not sell or score Route B/team components as part of Foundations’ routine value.
5. If time compresses, remove one scenario or shorten debrief; retain retrieval, variation, breakpoint distinction and scope close.

## Optional components outside this core

- Day-21 learning return: a separately specified hypothesis, not a default proof of transfer.
- Manager session/Route B: separate products/components, excluded from participant-core claims and score.
