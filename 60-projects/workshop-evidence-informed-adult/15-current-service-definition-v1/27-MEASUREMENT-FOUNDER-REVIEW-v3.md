# MFC Measurement System v3 — founder review

## The decision in one sentence

Approve, amend or pause the proposal to make private, organisation-approved evaluation a standard part of the measured Essentials and Foundations services—so MFC can report what it delivered and what respondents reported learning/using, while separately building the stronger study needed to test intervention effectiveness.

## What you are approving now

| Approve this | It means | It does **not** mean |
|---|---|---|
| `ESSENTIALS-EVAL-v1` and `FOUNDATIONS-EVAL-v1` | Forms are offered before, immediately after and day 21–28, outside teaching time | Introduction gains routine forms, or any form is field-ready today |
| Standard private evaluation | MFC asks every attendee to complete it; the organisation agrees to protected time and aggregate reporting | A person is forced to disclose, a manager sees completion status, or a response affects employment |
| Three separate records | Participant pathway; delivery/fidelity; Radar/manager implementation | One MFC score, individual tracking, or a manager/Radar record joined to participant responses |
| Aggregate organisation report | Delivery, response coverage, respondent-reported learning/use, barriers and separate component implementation | A causal claim that MFC improved people, wellbeing, culture or performance |
| Enhanced effectiveness lane | MFC will develop a comparative study to test mission-level effectiveness | Routine forms already prove causation |

## What the standard report can say

> “MFC delivered [version] to [attendee count]. Among [immediate respondents], [n/%] selected [named learning response]. Among [return respondents], [n/%] reported a relevant opportunity; among those respondents, [n/%] reported intentionally choosing what to do next.”

It is an honest report about participant-reported signals among respondents. It cannot yet say “MFC caused this result” or “X% of all workers improved.”

## The only four decisions needed from Hunter now

1. **Approve / amend / pause the measured-service model:** `ESSENTIALS-EVAL-v1` and `FOUNDATIONS-EVAL-v1`; standard Introduction stays data-free.
2. **Approve / amend / pause the report purpose:** organisation reports use Level A–B language only—delivery and participant-reported signals, never causal or individual-performance claims.
3. **Choose a build owner:** name the person accountable for selecting the form platform, data custodian/backup, report recipient and accessible private completion route.
4. **Choose the effectiveness path:** authorise planning for a later Level-D comparative study, or keep that lane designed-but-paused until MFC has routine evaluation data.

## What happens after approval

MFC does not collect data immediately. The build owner completes the controlled technical process:

1. Choose and test the platform for hidden metadata, access, deletion, code/linkage and no-tracking controls.
2. Freeze participant/support/retention wording against the actual platform.
3. Run the synthetic and accessibility test protocol.
4. Obtain proportionate qualified human review of platform/data handling, participant notice and support position.
5. Independently re-review the working build and mock report before real collection.

## What you do not need to read now

The exact questions, report tables, agreement clauses, privacy copy, code rules and test cases are already built. They are supporting implementation documents, not founder reading.
