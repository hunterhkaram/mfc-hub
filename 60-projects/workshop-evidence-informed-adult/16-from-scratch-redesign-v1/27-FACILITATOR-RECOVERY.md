# Facilitator recovery — when the room goes wrong

**What this is.** The scripted responses that let one person run the room alone. Thirty-seven
situation rows and ten standing guardrails. Rows 1-23 are recovered from five orphaned source files
and re-anchored to the fourteen-beat spine. **Rows 24-37 were written on 2026-09-10** to cover the
four activities that put a participant inside a real difficult moment and had no scripted recovery at
all, plus the hostile room and the facilitator's own error. They are marked ⚑ and are new writing,
not recovery.

**How to read a row.** `Beat` is where the situation arises under the current spine. `Product`
says where the row can actually happen — the Introduction is a keynote in which nobody writes,
speaks on demand, or performs anything (rulings 7, 8), so rows that assume an exercise is
running belong only to Essentials and Foundations. `Flag` marks a row that breaks a current
ruling or carries an unresolved dependency. **A flagged row is still carried. It is not dropped
and it is not silently kept.**

**Row numbers are stable identifiers, not reading order.** Rows 1-23 keep the numbers they were
recovered under, because other files cite them (`31-FOUNDATIONS-240-STRUCTURE.md` cites row 23 by
number). New rows 24-37 were appended to whichever movement table their beat belongs to, so the
numbering runs out of sequence down the page. **Nothing was renumbered.**

**Product codes:** `I` Introduction (The Mental Fitness Hour) · `E` Essentials · `F` Foundations.

## 1 · The one thing to fix before any of this is used

Four rows below end with *"follow the host's approved support process if one exists."*

`41-EVERY-WORKSHOP-CHECKLIST-v1.md` item 18 (HARD) rules on that exact wording:

> *"A recovery line that defers to 'the host's approved process if one exists' is not a route."*

It is carried verbatim anyway, because it is what the facilitator currently has and deleting it
leaves nothing. **But it is not a route, and this file does not turn it into one.** The real
procedure exists as
`14-build-readiness-and-expert-lens-v1/04-escalation-and-distress-response-procedures-DRAFT.md`,
whose own frontmatter says it is
`draft-not-cleared-requires-qualified-clinical-and-legal-review`. Its open questions to a
clinical reviewer include the exact scripted wording for the in-the-moment check-in — the words
that would replace the deferral line. **That draft is the missing object, and only a qualified
human closes it.** Not this file, and not an agent.

## 2 · Standing guardrails — true at every beat, in every product

Carried verbatim from `40-...-v3.md` §7 items 1-10 (which themselves carry
`07-FOUNDATIONS-FACILITATOR-PACKET-v1.md`).

1. Keep private reflection private. **No person needs to explain a choice or a barrier.**
2. Do not call a rehearsal, plan, teach-back or demonstration a Mental Rep.
3. Do not imply more practice guarantees access, success or wellbeing.
4. Do not sell or score team components as part of Foundations' routine value.
5. **If time compresses:** see row 9. *(Change marked: the original item 5 named specific reps
   and minute-band protections belonging to the retired eleven-part run sheet. The current
   compression authority is `22-THE-PARTICIPANT-FOUNDATION.md`'s C1-C5 / E1-E5 / F1-F6 orders.)*
6. **Never require a specific Shift; never let Shift be explained as "calm down."**
7. **Never say practice will make a new pattern feel natural.**
8. **Do not read, collect, photograph or comment on any participant's written work.** *(Change
   marked: original said "any participant's Rep Plan." The Rep Plan is Foundations-only and is
   gated on the `D-0112` conflict; the guardrail is broader than the artefact and is stated at
   its real scope.)*
9. Breakpoints are places to understand a situation, not grades of a person. Fictional
   situations only.
10. **Avoid hero narratives.** Support, delay and non-action are complete answers.
