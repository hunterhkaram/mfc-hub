# Essentials, as it is said

**Same fourteen beats. Same order. Same words in his mouth.** A participant who sat through the
Hour in March and Essentials in September recognises every beat and finds it deeper, never
different. That is ruling 4 and ruling 5, and it is the only reason this product is affordable to
build at all.

**What Essentials actually buys, and it is four things.**

1. **Time to practise.** The Hour shows the shift menu and says plainly that nobody will be made
   to perform one. Essentials shows it and then has each person use one, silently, on a moment
   they already lived.
2. **The five skills worked**, one example each, and then used as the instrument a participant
   picks their moment with. In the Hour they are named in fifteen seconds and never return.
3. **The return session**, 60 minutes remote, inside the price (`D-0113`, ruling 69).
4. **Depth in three places the Hour compresses to a sentence**: the wider Human Operating System,
   the four places a cue hides, and low friction against high friction.

**Everything else is the Hour.**
