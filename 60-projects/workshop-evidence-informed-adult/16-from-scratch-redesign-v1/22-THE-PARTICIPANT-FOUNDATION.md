# The participant foundation

**What Hunter ruled, 2026-09-08:** *"instead of making that the 60 etc — why can't that, but an
improved version that we test with our agents, be the foundation for all services — and the manager
session is completely different."*

**The inversion, stated plainly.** Until today the 60-minute Introduction was the base and
Essentials (90) and Foundations (240) *widened* it. That is now reversed. **There is ONE participant
foundation.** The 58, the 90 and the 240 are **compressions of it**. Nothing is added as a product
gets longer; things are *given back*.

**The foundation is not a fourth product.** Nobody ever delivers "the foundation." It is the
complete participant argument, at full depth, from which every participant-facing service is cut.

**⛔ The manager session is NOT a compression of this file.** It is a separate product with a
different audience, a different job and a different organising principle — it teaches the
environment, not the practice. It lives in
`60-projects/workshop-evidence-informed-adult/16-from-scratch-redesign-v1/03-MANAGER-SESSION-DESIGN.md`
and must never be derived from, or reconciled against, the movements below.

## Why fourteen movements, and not seventeen

**The foundation has FOURTEEN movements.** They are `D-0130`'s fourteen beats, unchanged in order
and in function.

An earlier unfiled proposal claimed **seventeen movements and 270 "natural" minutes**, with
per-movement compression ranks and floors deriving to 56 / 81 / 194. **Those seventeen movements and
their floors are not reproduced here and are not treated as fact.** A-24 refused them as
UNAUDITABLE, correctly: no file ever existed to check them against, and the totals they derived to
were the totals `02-MERGED` §12 already states — which means the derivation was fitted to a known
answer rather than producing one.

**Where seventeen came from, and why it is not the movement count.** Hunter's own talk
(`15-inputs/hunter-notes/workshop/2026-09-07-founder-the-talk-as-hunter-gives-it.md`) is laid out in
**eighteen numbered steps**, not seventeen. Those eighteen are *the arc as he happens to say it* —
and he disowned them as a running order in the same file: *"that's the arc of what I've said. Right?
It doesn't mean it's the best arc."* The capture's own instruction is explicit: **"Anyone building
from this file must treat the arc as source material, not as a running order."**

**So: fourteen, because fourteen is the only movement count that exists in a governing record.** It
is `D-0130`'s ruling, it is built out in `02-MERGED` §1, and it is written as spoken words in
`03-DELIVERY-SCRIPT-INTRODUCTION-60.md`. Every one of Hunter's eighteen arc steps is carried by one
of the fourteen; the mapping is in the table below, and no arc step is orphaned.

in the room. If a movement's *for* is already achieved, the movement is finished, however many

### The two rules that govern all fourteen at every depth

1. **⛔ A room cannot perform a Do.** Hunter's ruling, 2026-09-08, now written into the hour: the

   co-facilitator (`AG-131` / `AG-160`).

## The compression rule

**Full depth is 194 minutes of content.** That is the foundation at its widest articulation — every
movement fully worked, delivered as Foundations (240 = 194 content + 40 breaks + 6 buffer).

**Compression is narrowing, never removal.**

> **RULE 1 — No movement is ever removed.** All fourteen are present at 58, at 90 and at 240. A
> movement compresses toward its floor; it never reaches zero.
>
> **RULE 2 — Order never changes.** One shape, one order, one set of words in Hunter's mouth, at
> every depth. He never has to relearn where he is.
>
> **RULE 3 — Nothing is ARGUED at 90 or 240 that is not argued at 58.** The argument is the
> invariant. **Depth and practice are what widen.**
>
> ⚑ **AMENDED 2026-09-09 on Hunter's ruling. It previously read "nothing is TAUGHT at 90 or 240 that
> is not present at 58," and that wording was a live defect.** The 58 is a keynote in which nobody
> writes, speaks or performs anything — so the old rule forbade Foundations from containing
> rehearsal, pair work, the written Radar, the moment gallery, application studios or toolkit
> practice: **every strong participant device MFC owns.** This file already broke it three times in
> its own text. Hunter's ruling of 2026-09-09 — that the moment gallery *"has to be in the
> foundations"* and application studios run *"the foundations only"* — settles it: a longer product
> gives the same argument with more depth **and with practice the hour cannot carry.**
>
> **RULE 4 — Compression is monotonic.** No movement is ever *longer* in a shorter product. The one
> deliberately flat row is movement 3, the door: at 58 and 90 it is the same length, because making
> it longer turns a reassurance into a warning.
>
> **RULE 5 — The never-compress floor is a list, not a percentage.** Movements 3, 4, 5, 9, movement
> 6's *"I'm not going to teach you all five in an hour"*, movement 8's environment boundary and
> manager handoff, movement 12's one-in-three number, and movement 14's QR plus the sixty-second
> swap are at their floor already at 58 and cannot be compressed further in any product.
>
> **RULE 6 — The buffer is not compressible.** It is the thing compression protects.

## Ruling 1 — the five skills. Neither competing document was current.

**The conflict.** `04-RUN-SHEETS-AND-DECKS.md` Part B (2–3 Sep): *"The five named skills… not taught

`D-0130` created: `D-0131` restored **skill-referenced moment selection** and said plainly that

are **named, and each given one worked example, used solely as the moment-selection instrument** —
*which of these is the hard part of your moment?* **Never defined one by one as a taught model, never
on a slide as a list, never practised as five things, never returned to.** Part B's slide prohibition
stands absolutely.
