# "Has this been shown to work?"

**The likeliest hard question in any room of adults, and MFC had no scripted answer to it.**
Found by the facilitator recovery sweep, 2026-09-09, absent from every facilitator pack MFC has
ever written. A participant asks it with four minutes left; a buyer asks it in writing. Same
answer, three lengths.

**The position underneath all of it.** MFC has **zero delivered cohorts** and no outcome evidence
of any kind. The mechanism it is built on — repeated practice against a real cue, which **may
make useful patterns more familiar and easier to reach** (`P9`) — draws on established work in
adjacent literatures (`P5`). **MFC's own programme has not been shown to produce it.** Those are
two different claims and the entire answer is built on keeping them apart (`K-09`; `P21`).

---

## 1 · The spoken answer

### 1a · Ten seconds — a participant, hand up, four minutes left

> Not yet. **We haven't measured a group through this.** What's underneath it is well tested;
> the hour on top of it isn't. **We'll publish when we know.**

**P19** + **P21** + **P20**, compressed. Use this in the room unless the person wants more.

### 1b · Forty seconds — the standing version

Four beats. **Learn the shape, not the words.**

> **[1 — answer the question first]**
> Not yet. We haven't delivered a measured cohort. **We have no results to show you and we're not
> going to invent any.**
>
> **[2 — what has been shown]**
> What *has* been shown is the thing underneath it. **Our approach draws together established
> psychological science** — attention, state regulation, values-based action — **into a
> practical, teachable model.** Practising a skill against a real cue, in the moment you'd
> actually use it. **That isn't ours and it isn't new.** What we've done is build an hour on top
> of it.
>
> **[3 — the line that must not be softened]**
> So what the research validates is **the mechanisms we've built on. That's a different claim,
> and you should hold us to the difference.**
>
> **[4 — turn it into the ask]**
> Proof comes from measurement across cohorts, **or it doesn't come at all — and we'll publish
> either way.**

| Beat | Traces to |
|---|---|
| 1 | **P19** (`AG-001`; said once, `AG-028` — as the invitation, not the apology) |
| 2 | **P5** (verbatim spine, `K-08` "may safely say") + **P3** + **ruling 39**, which is conceded first and unprompted rather than defended |
| 3 | **P21**. `13-...-value-proposition` marks this sentence as one that **must not be softened**. Any drift to "backed by research" or "evidence-based programme" breaks `K-08`. |
| 4 | **P20** |

**Why beat 2 carries the room.** It is the only beat that gives a sceptic something to hold, and
conceding that the loop isn't novel *before* anyone extracts it is what makes beats 3 and 4 land
as confidence rather than hedging.

**Register.** Beat 1 is flat and fast — it is not an apology and must not be delivered as one
(*"I don't want us to shoot ourselves in the foot… be precise, be powerful."*). Same register as
beat 10 of `03-DELIVERY-SCRIPT-INTRODUCTION-60.md`: *"It's an hour, and I think it lands. That
doesn't guarantee it leads to behaviour change, and it can't."*

### 1c · ⛔ The fifth beat that is NOT usable today — naming the mechanism literature

There is a tempting extra beat naming if-then planning. **Do not use it yet.**

The registered source (Sheeran, Listrom & Gollwitzer 2024, 642 tests,
`10-sources/2026-09-03-sheeran-listrom-gollwitzer-2024-...md`) permits exactly one public
sentence — *"Choosing a few specific moments ahead of time draws on decades of research on if-then
planning"* — **and only once the record is confirmed. It is `status: draft`,
`approval_status: awaiting-specialist`, `confirmed_by: ""`.** Registered (ruling 45) and confirmed
are different gates.

⛔ **And the record states the evidence is for implementation intentions in goal pursuit and is
*untested* for "notice a moment and do a mental rep."** So *"that's the family we're in"* is
barred outright, now and after confirmation. ⛔ **No effect size, percentage or "X times more
likely" on any public surface until a full-text read** — the record is abstract-level,
secondary-sourced, and carries an unverified flag of substantial publication bias.

**Status: UNSUPPORTED, not usable.** Unblocking it is head-of-research work: confirm the record.

## 3 · The follow-ups

### 3.1 · *"So you're saying it might not work?"*

> **[H]**
> It **can**, not **will**. There are skills that can, not will, improve how you navigate life's
> moments — and I'd rather say that than the other thing.
>
> Where it genuinely doesn't reach: **a skill doesn't replace a bad environment. If the conditions
> are against you, this is not the answer to that.**
>
> What we're aiming at is that **more ordinary moments have more than one option in them.**
> That's the target.

**P30** (the "can, not will" construction is Hunter's own — preserve it exactly) · **P14** ·
**P7**.

⚑ **One boundary, not five.** `P14` is the falsifiable one and it is what buys the credibility;
`P4` (capacity is not perfection) belongs in the room during the talk, not stacked here.
`AG-028`, and `AG-003`/`AG-233` — Eimear has twice found MFC over-reading caution. **Over-hedging
is a defect, not diligence** (`L-084`). ⛔ **Do not add "it's not a magic trick", "it is not a
promise", or any sentence asserting your own trustworthiness** — beat 3 already does that work
without saying so, and defending against an accusation nobody made is the tell of a weak position.

⚠ **If the person presses, the correct move is a diagnostic question, not reassurance:** *where
did it break — did you catch it, did you have something to shift with, did you know what to do
instead?* (`13-...-claim-limits` Layer 2.)

### 3.2 · *"What evidence do you have?"*

> **[H]**
> Two kinds, and they're not the same kind, so let me separate them.
>
> There's the research behind the mechanisms — memory and retrieval, habit formation, planning,
> emotion regulation. **We're evidence-informed and evidence-aligned, and those are the two words
> I'll use.** We hold a working library of it, and **we'll show you any of it.**
>
> Then there's evidence that *our* programme does what we say. **We have none of that. Zero
> cohorts.** And it's the only kind that could settle it.
>
> So: measurement across cohorts, **and we publish either way.**

**P6** (*evidence-informed and evidence-aligned* — **these two words and no stronger ones**) ·
**P21** · **P19** · **P20** · `K-09`'s four evidence roles in plain language.

⚑ **"A working library, and we'll show you any of it" is the founder's own form and deliberately
carries no number.** ⛔ **Do not say a count out loud.** (For the record: `10-sources/` holds
**138** records as of 2026-09-09, up from the 118 verified 2026-08-09 — which is why a spoken
figure goes stale and gets a facilitator caught.)

Named registered mechanism sources if asked: Tulving & Pearlstone 1966; Sheppes et al. 2011;
Roediger & Karpicke 2006; Lally et al. 2010; Burgler et al. 2026; Sheeran, Listrom & Gollwitzer
2024. ⚠ **All are `draft`/`awaiting-specialist`.** Say *"registered in our source library"*, never
*"established findings"* about any one of them, and ⛔ **never describe any of them as proving an
MFC component** (`K-09`, representative-source role).

### 3.3 · Buyer: *"How will I know it worked?"*

> **[H]**
> You'll get a written report, and I'll tell you now exactly what it can and can't tell you.
>
> **We measure comprehension before and after the session, and attempted reps at three weeks.**
> That tells you whether it landed and whether people are reaching for it. **It does not tell you
> behaviour changed over months.** Nobody can tell you that from a workshop, and we won't.
>
> **You get a report whatever the number responds — what changes is what it can carry.** With ten
> or more, numbers. Below that, at the moment, a report about the session and the design rather
> than about the people, and I'll tell you that up front. **Never anything attributable to a
> person, and never broken down by team, shift, site or role.**
>
> Measurement is designed in from the first session rather than bolted on.

**P23** · **P24** · **P22** · ⚑ **`D-0132`** (2026-09-09, Hunter, `canonical: true`).

⚠ **`P25` is now partly stale and this page does not rely on it.** `D-0132` retired the
report-suppression floor: *"There is no report-suppression floor. Every cohort that buys a
measured product receives a written report."* It supersedes `D-0121`'s sub-five clause and
`P25`'s final sentence. **`P25` still needs updating at source in `11-PERMITTED-CLAIMS.md`** —
head-of-research work, flagged not done. ⛔ **The five-to-nine "kinds of moment" tier is NOT YET
IN FORCE** (gated on Debra Fidler and Eimear Quigley, `06` §3.0.8) — which is why the spoken line
above says *"below ten, a report about the session"* and does not offer kinds of moment at four
respondents.

⚑ **Product-scoping, not optional** (`D-0120`, `D-0121`):
- **Introduction** — pre/post only. **No three-week measure and no Form C.** Drop that clause.
- **Essentials / Small Team Essentials / Foundations** — the paragraph above applies in full.
- **The manager session is deliberately not measured** (`P35`). Say so and say why: what each
  manager writes is private, on their own phone, and collecting it would produce the version that
  sounds good.

⚠ **Unresolved and surfaced rather than reconciled** (`CLAUDE.md` §4): `P24` calls the
Introduction artefact *"a delivery and comprehension record, not a report"*, while `D-0121` §2
rules *"A written report goes to every product — Introduction, Small Team Essentials, Essentials
and Foundations."* These may reconcile, since `P24` was rewritten under `D-0121`, but a facilitator
holding both can be caught. **Until it is settled, describe the Introduction artefact by what it
contains — recognition before and after a 60-minute session — rather than by either label.**

### 3.4 · *"So we'd be your first?"* / *"Are we paying to be your test case?"*

**The hardest question in the set, and the direct consequence of beat 1.**

> **[H]**
> Among the first, yes. I'm not going to dress that up. **What you're buying is the workshop —
> the design, the hour, the return session, the manager session, the report.** All of that is
> built and priced on the work in it, not on a discount for being early.
>
> What you'd also be is one of the places this gets measured properly, which is the part I
> actually want. **Proof comes from measurement across cohorts, or it doesn't come at all — and
> we'll publish either way.**

**P20** · **P32** (the return session, report and manager session are inside the price, not
extensions).

⛔ **Do not offer a discount, a pilot rate or a "founding cohort" price.** `D-0122` §4 settled
this: solving for a discount that holds per head at the benchmark *"comes out at zero on both
products."* The commercial route around zero evidence is **not** price — it is the measurement
design, which is what §3.3 sells. ⛔ **And do not say "you'd be helping us"** — that inverts who
is buying what.

### 3.5 · *"Can I speak to someone who's done it?"* / *"How many have you run?"*

> **[H]**
> Not yet — there's nobody to put you in front of, and I'd rather say that than find you someone
> who once came to a talk. **When there are cohorts, you'll get the report, not a testimonial.**

**P19** · **P20**. ⚑ **This is why the anecdote ban in §4 does not leave the facilitator empty:
the replacement for a reference is the report, and it is a better artefact.** There is also a
principled reason, worth giving if the buyer is technical: **individual testimony can never be
MFC's proof**, because the model is structured so no single individual outcome could disconfirm
it (`13-...-claim-limits`). Aggregate measurement is the only thing that can.

### 3.6 · *"Can you send me the evidence in writing?"*

> **[H]**
> Yes. I'll send what we hold and what it does and doesn't cover.

⚠ **`AG-018` (Gina, HARD) requires the evidence summary to be published for consumers.** ⛔ **Do
not send a source record or an internal file.** Until a published summary exists, send only what
is already cleared for external use and say so. **This is a real gap: there is no buyer-facing
evidence one-pager, and this answer is writing a cheque nothing currently cashes.**

### 3.7 · The don't-know exit — sanctioned, use it

> **[H]** I don't want to guess at that. **Let me come back to you in writing.**

⚑ **This exists because a prohibition list with no exit fails live.** The pressure that produces
every banned sentence in §4 is a facilitator with no permitted answer. **This is the permitted
answer.** Using it costs nothing; guessing costs the room.

## 5 · Is the honest answer commercially weak? — the plain verdict

**In a written procurement comparison, yes, demonstrably. In a room, the reasoning says no — but
that half is a hypothesis, not a finding, and this page cannot evidence it.**

- **In a tender or a side-by-side against a vendor willing to overclaim, MFC loses**, and no
  drafting fixes it. A buyer scoring "evidence of outcomes" on a rubric marks MFC zero, correctly.
  That is a real, permanent-until-cohorts commercial cost of `AG-001`, and MFC should carry it
  knowingly rather than discover it in a lost bid.
- **In front of adults, the reasoning is that a checkable concession beats an unfalsifiable
  claim**, and that conceding ruling 39 unprompted converts the weakest moment into the most
  credible one. ⚠ **That is a judgement this page cannot support. Zero cohorts means this script
  has never met an actual sceptic.** The first deliveries settle it.
- **The only real mitigation is a delivered measured cohort.** ⛔ Filling `P27` would *not* fix
  the procurement problem — target-and-cite produces a cited target, not outcome evidence, and
  scores zero on the same rubric. It is a partial improvement, not the answer, and it is currently
  blocked anyway.
- **Price is not the route either.** `D-0122` §4 found a defensible early-adopter discount comes
  out at zero on both products. What MFC has to sell against zero evidence is **the measurement
  design itself** (§3.3) — which is genuinely unusual and is the strongest commercial asset in
  this whole answer.
- **`AG-292` is only half answered.** Gina's point is that a buyer who cannot see the potential
  benefit will not buy. §3.3 gives a concrete deliverable and `P7` gives the target. **Neither is
  a benefit statement.** Whether that is enough is Gina's call, not this page's.
