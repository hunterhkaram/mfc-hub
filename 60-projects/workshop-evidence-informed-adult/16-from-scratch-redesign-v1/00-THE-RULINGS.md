## 1 · Design rulings — structure, duration, exercises, what happens in the room

1. **No minutes.** Show structure and beats, never per-beat minute allocations. *"Get rid of the minutes. Get rid of all these things."* — 2026-09-08, `15-inputs/hunter-notes/workshop/2026-09-08-founder-the-full-content-bank.md`
2. **A run sheet cannot be locked; a spine can.** *"It shouldn't be broken into minutes. It should be broken into the structure and the beats, and those beats just get extended out in terms of the time depending on the session."* — 2026-09-08, `30-decisions/D-0131`
3. **The spine is four movements over fourteen beats. Order never changes; beats widen.** Duration is a property of a beat in a product, not part of the spine. — 2026-09-08, `D-0131`
4. **One participant foundation; the 58, 90 and 240 are compressions of it.** Nothing is added as a product gets longer — things are given back. *"why can't that, but an improved version that we test with our agents, be the foundation for all services."* — 2026-09-08, `22-THE-PARTICIPANT-FOUNDATION.md`
5. **All products follow one shared narrative.** *"introduction is not a different design brief. Change that."* — 2026-08-31, supersedes `D-0111` §1
6. **A room cannot perform a Do. No staged Do exercise, at any depth, ever.** Reps used are ones participants have already done in their own lives. — 2026-09-08, `22-THE-PARTICIPANT-FOUNDATION.md`
7. **It is a keynote, not a workshop.** *"If it's in the sixty minute, we're not pulling out a butcher's sheet."* No butcher's paper, nothing written by participants, nothing asked out loud. — 2026-09-07, `2026-09-07-founder-the-talk-as-hunter-gives-it.md`
8. **Nobody speaks unless they want to. Nothing is collected.** Max 20 with one facilitator; 30 only with a co-facilitator. — `22-THE-PARTICIPANT-FOUNDATION.md`
9. **Experience first, name second.** The breath beat runs before the word Stop exists: *"bring your attention to your breath… we caught the moment. We stopped. Then we name it."* — 2026-09-07, founder capture
10. **The Radar is given, not built.** *"Here's a radar we recommend to start… Just three nice reps. Simple. It's there. So we're not getting them to get out their phone and spend ten minutes trying to build it."* Sixty seconds in the room to swap one for their own. — 2026-09-07, ratified `D-0130`
11. **The room leaves rehearsing ONE rep. The Radar still holds three.** Different objects. — 2026-09-08, `D-0131`
12. **Peak and retrieval, both.** Keep the peak at beat 13; add a silent cold recall between the peak and the Radar. — 2026-09-08, `D-0131`
13. **Both analogies stay** — braking and physical-health-vs-physical-fitness. *"they explain different things — one shows the gap… the other explains the category by comparison to the physical capacity of the body."* — 2026-09-08, `D-0131`
14. **The braking analogy replaces the driving example.** Nobody was ever taught to brake; the cost is a life getting smaller, not a crash. — 2026-09-07, founder capture; built in `D-0130`
15. **The finish does not crescendo.** *"we get to the finish, and the finish doesn't end on something crazy. It's: hey, this is practising, and this is how we can practise."* — 2026-09-07
16. **The promises are distributed, not stacked.** No-disclosure at minute 0, the safety door immediately before the first thing anyone does, the honest limit inside the argument. — `D-0130`
17. **Hands up is allowed for recognition, never for disclosure.** ⚑ **Corrected by Hunter 2026-09-09 — this was previously recorded as a flat ban and that was wrong.** *"If it's a hands up, if you've ever heard of this before, yeah, we want them to feel involved… If I know that you know, that we know together… I think that's actually beneficial."* **Allowed:** have you heard of this, do you recognise this, have you been in a situation like this — questions about shared knowledge and shared experience, which build the social norm. ⛔ **Never:** *"hands up if you've had a bad day recently"* or anything asking a person to disclose their own state. The self-directed poll at beat 2 is unchanged — that one they answer to themselves. — 2026-09-09; supersedes the flat reading of `D-0131` and founder capture §5
18. **Participants do not place themselves in the four positions.** The mental-health/mental-fitness matrix is taught, not self-applied. — standing ruling, `24-THE-PLAN-HUNTER-ASKED-FOR.md`
19. **Push low friction / high friction hard, and put it in the Introduction.** *"I think it's so important that we really push down low friction, high friction… So, so important we practise in the low friction moments."* — 2026-09-08
20. **Length is not the constraint.** *"I don't think eighteen's too much for the flow, if we're speaking and it's interactive and we're explaining."* — 2026-09-07
21. **A slide count is not design authority.** *"this has every right to be more than 38 slides."* — 2026-08-13, `80-learning/L-062`
22. **The personal story is optional, never baked in.** *"I don't think that should be in every single thing, but it's something I can speak to if I want to."* Every card carrying personal history needs a neutral version too. — 2026-09-08 §29
23. **One consolidated take-home leave-behind, premade and identical, nothing written on it.** *"they need to be bringing paper, which is an extra cost for us, and they're probably just gonna chuck it in the bin."* No printed prompts in the hour. — 2026-08-27, `D-0112`; 2026-09-07 (*"We won't have printed ones."*)
24. **The return session is a check-in, not a classification exercise.** Branching design, six modules, thresholds, the eight-response floor and the paper-slip vote are all retired. *"We're not trying to capture… If we can keep things digital, we can keep things simple."* — 2026-08-30, `D-0114`
25. **The manager session is a separate product, never a compression of the participant foundation and never a double-up.** *"The manager session shouldn't be a double up of those other sessions… it's a different session with a different purpose, a different intention, and different outcomes we're wanting."* — 2026-09-09, `03-MANAGER-SESSION-DESIGN.md`
26. **The manager session is a planning session with an intention, and the working block is its centre.** *"I think it's in that manager session where we can work with the people, and they can come up with something that they think works for their team."* — 2026-09-09
27. **No exclusion rule for a manager sitting with their own reports; the session runs identically.** *"I don't really understand that. I don't think this actually has anything to do with a boss or someone seeing it."* — 2026-09-01 / 2026-09-03, `D-0123`
28. **Foundations' differentiator this cycle is the deepest individual teaching, not organisational readiness.** Route B stays built and gated but is not a marketed differentiator. — 2026-08-27, `D-0110`
29. **Route B is agreement, not live design** — *"do we agree we want this, understand it, value it"*, selecting from options worked out with managers beforehand, not inventing them in the room. — 2026-08-27, `D-0112`

## 2 · Content rulings — what is sayable, what is not

30. **The five skills are mentioned in EVERY product, with why they matter — and taught in depth only in Essentials and Foundations.** *"the five skills should be mentioned in all of them… mentioning the skills and why those skills are important. Then in the essentials and the foundations, we can go into a bit more depth or detail about the specific skills… perhaps it's going through proper examples."* In the Introduction: named with a rationale, not defined one by one, not practised, and *"I'm not going to teach you all five in an hour"* travels with the names. — 2026-09-09 extending `D-0130`
31. **The Human Operating System's tier one — Passenger, Engine, Driver — explains the knowing–doing gap in the hour.** *"Why is the knowing–doing gap a thing? And that's what we can explain — the driver, the passenger, the engine."* The wider four stay in Essentials and Foundations. — 2026-09-08 §30; 2026-09-07
32. **Pathways and rewiring CAN be explained as mechanism.** Blocking the mechanism is over-cautious; the *claim* is what `K-08` bars, not the explanation. — standing ruling, `24-THE-PLAN-HUNTER-ASKED-FOR.md`; wording unresolved, 2026-09-08 §15
33. **"We can't teach you skills. We can teach you the process."** *"it's up to you to practise that, to build the skills, and then apply it in the life you're living."* — 2026-09-08 §13
34. **"Can, not will."** *"there are skills that can, not will, improve how you navigate life's moments."* — DURABLE, `90-meta/agent-context-v1/13-founder-voice-v2-claim-limits.md`, 2026-08-09
35. **State plainly what MFC is not claiming.** *"We're not trying to create the most mentally fit individuals ever — you will never, ever feel anxious or overwhelmed. That's not possible."* — 2026-09-08 §18
36. **Where the environment is the real problem, MFC is not the answer, and says so.** *"Just because we have skills, that doesn't replace an intense, horrific environment. That's the truth."* — DURABLE, `13-...-claim-limits.md`
37. **MFC runs alongside treatment, never as a replacement for therapy or clinical care.** — DURABLE, `13-...-explaining-mental-fitness.md`
38. **The claim is the access and retrieval gap for the everyday person, not novelty of the loop.** *"it exists in the therapy rooms… but that's not being made with the intention of every single human being able to have that. That's the gap we're speaking about."* — 2026-08-06, `L-033`
39. **Do not claim the loop is novel, and do not claim Stop/Shift/Do was invented.** The surviving claim: nobody should need therapy to access it, plus Shift through the body. — DURABLE, `13-...-category-and-competitors.md`
40. **The organisational-outcome formula is target-and-cite, never assertion.** *"this is how we think it will… that's an outcome. That's not why we're doing it."* — 2026-08-19, `D-0104`
41. **Nothing may be said about the framework that has not been said and approved.** *"there's some things that I'm reading that we've never ever said before, and it's never been approved before."* — 2026-08-23, `L-081`
42. **A rep counts by attempt, toward what matters — not by outcome.** *"the outcome doesn't tell whether it counts."* Locked definition: *"catching a moment and choosing your next move, on purpose, toward what matters."* — `D-0107`, 2026-08-24
43. **The basis is thinking, feeling, acting, connecting.** *"In every moment, we're either thinking, we're feeling, we're acting, we're connecting. That's a process all humans have."* — 2026-08-31, `D-0117`
44. **The sleep/movement/food/rest layer is called The Basics.** *"i don't love engine basics… if we say engine basics people need to know what the engine is."* — 2026-08-24, `D-0108`
45. **External statistics are wanted and must be real.** *"I think what could be good in these talks is if we bring in a few stats — and I don't mean stats from us."* No number enters a room without a registered source; the hospice-regrets study and the ~85% coffee figure are out until registered. — 2026-09-08 §4; `D-0130`
46. **The manager scope rule holds in full.** Workload, staffing, safety and one person behaving badly are not habits, and a skill does not replace a bad environment. — kept, 2026-09-09, `03-MANAGER-SESSION-DESIGN.md`
47. **A quiet, slow or flat employee is not a technique**, and managers are told not to go looking for what their team wrote. — kept, 2026-09-09
48. **The 120,000 mental reps figure is participation in a 2025 campaign, never evidence of practice or behaviour change.** *"It wasn't crazy defined."* — DURABLE, `13-...-hunter-formation.md`
49. **Publication markings on founder-story items are binding.** The suicide losses are do-not-publish; several other items are ask-first. — DURABLE, `13-...-hunter-formation.md`
