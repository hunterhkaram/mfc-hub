One numbered set for all three products. Everything gets mentioned in every session — depth is the variable, not content. Foundations runs the lot. Essentials runs a subset with room to practise. The Introduction runs the cards a person has to walk out holding.

How to read this

Click a product and the set filters to what that session actually runs. The numbers never change — card 14 is card 14 in all three, so you never relearn where you are.

What the Introduction drops isn't content, it's depth. The Human Operating System, the five skills taught properly, the shift toolkit practised live, the organisational plan — those are named in the hour and worked in the longer ones. The one exception is the Foundations planning block, which only exists because 240 minutes can carry it.

What the hour has to leave behind

Your own test: if they're starting at level zero, how do we take them up — and by the end do they have the capacity to actually practise this in their life?

So the Introduction's cards were chosen against that, not against what's interesting. Stop, Shift, Do. Why it matters to them. A basic understanding of what's happening inside. That a rep is a thing they can start doing. That it lands on other people too. That they weren't born with any of this. Everything else earns its place in Essentials or Foundations instead.

Nothing here is applied. Move cards between products, cut them, add them — tell me and it changes.
