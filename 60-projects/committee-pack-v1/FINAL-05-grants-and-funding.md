# Grants and Funding Strategy: Year One Roadmap

## Executive Summary

MFC's year-one model retains three possible channels: grants, corporate case-study sales, and research partnerships. Grants are not assumed revenue and are not the organisation-wide immediate priority merely because a round is open.

Live primary-source verification on 13 August confirms FRRR "In a Good Place" Round 12 (source: FRRR official page, see §5 below) is open for grants up to $20,000 and closes 18 August 2026 at 5pm AEST. It requires a locally supported remote, rural or regional project. Hunter's recorded 10 August decision was not to commit MFC to regional/rural delivery now, so MFC is **not pursuing Round 12**. Camden Council's Community Grants round (source: Camden Council official page, see §5 below) is open 10 August–7 September for up to $6,500. MFC's incorporation is evidenced in the vault, but no qualifying Camden project/partner, ≥75% Camden participant cohort or $20 million public-liability evidence (Source: Camden Council, Community Grants Program, verified 13 Aug 2026 — see §5 Sources below) was found in the controlled corpus. Parramatta's general 2026 Community Round closed on 15 September 2025; it is not open now. These current facts supersede the dated priority labels retained elsewhere in the analysis history.

Philanthropic funding and individual donations will become relevant once MFC has one measured, published cohort. **Correction, 2026-08-18 (twice-corrected, see §8):** this section originally stated cash runway as four to eight months; a first correction (founder-supplied, later itself corrected) stated an unverified ~$1,000/month burn (estimate) giving ~12 months; Hunter then corrected this to an unverified **~$1,000/year burn** (estimate; website, insurance, small purchases) against an unverified ~$12,000 cash figure (estimate) — giving roughly a decade of runway at current burn. Solvency is not the organisation's binding constraint on current information. See §8 for the full correction history.

## 3. The FRRR-vs-Safeguarding Tension — Explained and Resolved

### What the Tension Is

FRRR's delivery window is 12 months from announcement (December 2026), so delivery would be due by ~December 2027. That's a long runway. **But MFC has a safeguarding gate (the safeguarding review) with six items that must close before any participant-facing delivery:**

1. Clinical-governance input on distress response / referral design
2. Qualified legal input on contract structure and liability  
3. Insurer/broker confirmation on delivery model insurance
4. Committee decision on proceeding and resourcing
5. Measurement gate — enough contact data to act on disclosure
6. Delivery partner confirmation on escalation protocols

**The question:** Can MFC honestly commit to FRRR when safeguarding is still open?

### The Recommendation: Do Not Apply in Round 12

The safeguarding timeline is not the controlling blocker. The current blocker is strategic and evidential: Hunter decided not to commit to regional/rural delivery, and FRRR favours locally initiated or endorsed work with evidence of community support. Applying without a real place, local relationship and community evidence would contradict both the recorded decision and the funder's criteria.

- **Round 12 disposition:** deferred.
- **Re-entry condition:** a genuine regional/rural project initiated with or endorsed by a named local community, plus an explicit reversal of the geography decision.
- **Preparation for a later round:** develop the local relationship and project first; do not start with grant copy.

### But — Different for Council Grants (Camden, Parramatta)

For the shorter-window council grants, **do not accept the grant, or schedule a session against it, until safeguarding items are actually closed.** Their delivery windows are shorter and tighter than FRRR's; the safeguarding gap is a real, not theoretical, risk for those grants.

**In short:** Safeguarding remains a delivery gate, but it is not a reason to reverse the recorded Round 12 deferral. No application should be drafted for a project MFC has not genuinely committed to deliver with the relevant community.

## 10. The Contingency — If the Planned Path Doesn't Work

### If No Grants Arrive by October

- Council grants didn't close, or closed and MFC didn't win
- FRRR application was deferred or not awarded

**Options:**
1. **Pause delivery**, preserve cash, wait for Perpetual (April/May 2027) or 2027 council reopenings
2. **Accelerate corporate sales harder** (the 90-day test in the payer research becomes the primary plan, not secondary)
3. **Seek emergency philanthropic bridge** (individual donors, a foundation contact of Hunter's, corporate sponsorship)

### If Corporate Sales Don't Start by Month 3

- Three conversations happened, zero qualified opportunities
- The revenue half of the model is not working (offer is wrong, price is wrong, or channel is wrong)

**What this means:** The funding path alone cannot support MFC. The access-funding route *requires* the corporate channel to exist, because the access fund has no source otherwise.

**What happens:** Either corporate strategy pivots (new channel, new offer, new price), or MFC enters an extended proof-of-concept phase funded purely by grants and research partnerships, with no revenue goal.
