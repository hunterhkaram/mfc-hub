## The three rep types (added 2026-09-16, `D-0136`, provisional, on Hunter product approval)

**Authority and status.** `D-0136`, decided by Hunter 2026-09-16, named this as a consequence.
Adopted under delegated product authority (`K-11`); committee ratification owed, as for this whole
record. Verbatim source: Hunter's 2026-08-31 capture, where the three types were already articulated
and never given a home.

**The locked rep definition is unchanged.** A moment may be caught *"before it happens, while it is
happening, or afterwards"*, and *"the outcome does not determine whether it counts."* This names
three forms inside that definition rather than narrowing it.

| Type | What it is | Counts? |
|---|---|---|
| **Live** | The moment is caught while it is happening, and the next move is chosen in it | Yes. **The full form** |
| **Rehearsal** | The routine is run against a moment that is expected but not yet here | Yes |
| **Reflection** | The moment has passed and is caught afterwards, with the next move named | Yes |

**Why only the first is called the full form, and why the other two still count.** Naming a
hierarchy without excluding anything is the point. **Live is what the practice is for. Rehearsal and
reflection are how someone gets there**, and a person who only ever manages reflection is practising,
not failing.

**What this replaces.** MFC had begun describing the practice as *drilled*, which imports a
performance standard the permissive definition deliberately refuses, and which MFC cannot observe or
measure. `D-0136` retired that language. **The comparative argument now rests on the practice being
cued by a real moment in the person's own life**, which is true of all three types and does not need
the word.

**Claims boundary.** `K-08` unchanged. None of this claims an effect; it names forms of an attempt.

> *"In every single moment of our life, we have attention. Our attention is on something. And
> depending on what our attention's on, whether that is a thought, whether it's based on a feeling,

> cannot catch and realise what's happening, we won't change what's happening."*

**Stated for the record.** Attention is always somewhere. Where it rests, on a thought or on a

redirect if the moment went by unnoticed.

> *"We could be in the moment and the moment just passes us by because we're in it. Sometimes you

## Why practice matters — the gap between knowing and doing (added 2026-08-05, provisional, on Hunter product approval)

**Authority and status.** Hunter directed this addition, 2026-08-05, `D-0062`, under delegated
product authority (`K-11`); committee ratification remains separately owed, as for this entire
record. This closes part of `LI-10`'s open causal question by naming the *mechanism the framework
is designed around*, explicitly as a stated rationale drawn from adjacent, established science — not
as a claim that MFC's own product has been proven to produce this effect.

**The gap, stated plainly:** understanding a skill (Learn It) changes what you know. It does not by
itself change what you default to under pressure. The Human Operating System's **Roads /
Pathways** part — the routes that get easier the more you take them — names why: your existing
habits and reactions are roads worn in by years of use, and **Autopilot** is what runs on them. A skill you learned ten minutes, or ten weeks, ago has
no pathway yet. When State Check reads "too much" or "too little," access narrows toward whatever
pathway is most familiar — and without practice, that is still the old default, however well you
understood the new skill intellectually. **This is the gap** between knowing something and being
able to do it when it counts.

**This is exactly why Build It exists as its own stage, distinct from Learn It.** A Mental Rep — one
intentional pass through Stop, Shift, Do, in a real moment — is literally the act that builds a new
pathway. Repetition in real moments, not further explanation, is what closes the gap.

**Evidence framing, kept honest:** the mechanism above (repeated, cued practice building automatic
access) is well-established in adjacent literatures — habit formation, automaticity, and
implementation-intentions research (see the framework-integrity-observatory's `05-...md` domain 5,
and `R-07`, which already identifies Radar as MFC's applied form of this exact literature). This is
the **rationale** for why the framework is built the way it is. It is not a claim that MFC's specific
programme has been shown to produce this effect — that remains the open causal question `LI-10`
already names, and nothing here closes it.

**Practise low-friction moments before high-friction ones.** The Radar's own guidance (choosing a
few moments on purpose) carries a design principle worth stating directly: build capacity in
lower-friction moments — the ones with more room to try, get it wrong, and try again — before
expecting yourself to handle the genuinely difficult ones. A person who has never practised
catching, shifting, and choosing in an easy moment has no real basis to expect themselves to manage
that same sequence the first time it matters most. Nothing prevents someone from also trying
higher-friction moments — but the expectation should be set accordingly.

## Existing practices — meditation, mindfulness, gratitude, and where they fit (added 2026-08-05, provisional, on Hunter product approval)

Established practices (meditation, mindfulness, gratitude journalling, breathwork, and similar) are
genuine, independently evidenced methods that can build components of the seven deeper skills —
mindfulness practice building Self-Awareness and Attention, gratitude practice building Relating to
Self, breathwork building State Regulation, and so on. K-07 does not dispute or diminish that value;
they sit within "what helps around the pathway" (Shift tools, exercises, anchors) below.

**The distinction that matters:** building a skill in general is not the same thing as being able to
access that skill in a specific real-life moment that matters to you. General practice (a daily
meditation session) strengthens the underlying capacity; it does not, by itself, guarantee that
capacity shows up in an unrelated, unplanned moment later that day — a heated conversation, a missed
deadline, a hard decision. That second gap — general capacity to moment-specific access — is what
the Radar and Mental Rep are specifically designed to close: practising the actual moments a person
has chosen to work on, not only building capacity in the abstract. Existing practices and MFC's
approach are complementary, not competing — one builds the skill, the other practises using it where
it is actually needed.

### Why each one is foundational — the third layer, corrected `D-0079`, rekeyed `D-0084`

Each deep skill carries two things now, not three: the **formal term** itself, and a **tag** (what it

| Deep skill | Why it's foundational |
|---|---|
| **Self-Awareness** | People read what's around them all the time — a face, a room, how they're going themselves. Catching it while it's happening, rather than working it out afterwards, is the part that takes practice. |

**The four rules that keep this layer safe — rule 1 was rewritten in `D-0079` after the original
version caused a real defect:**

1. **State a mechanism or something true about people. Never promise an outcome.** "This is what
   makes X possible" is allowed; "this will make you happier / calmer / more purposeful" is not.
   The original rule — "name the cost of NOT having it, never the benefit" — was a claims-safety
   heuristic that, applied across all seven, produced a set of deficit statements. The real
   distinction is **mechanism versus outcome**, not cost versus benefit.
2. **True of every person, always** — never true only of people who are struggling (`K-08` rule 3).
3. **No rhetorical universals.** Phrases like "every job ever done well" or "humans have never lived
   alone" are read as evidence claims on an evidence page, and judged as such.
4. **Assume nothing about a reader's life.** Not parenthood, not employment, not physical capacity.

**Removed, unanimously, across three independent lenses: "We're a tribal species."** In Australia
"tribal" carries colonial-ethnographic history in relation to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander
peoples, and in ordinary usage now also denotes in-group hostility. `K-08` rule 11 applies directly.

**Highest-risk line, and it was not obvious:** *Values-Based Action*'s earlier wording —
"somebody doing the hard part when it would have been easier not to" — carried the worst `K-08` rule
3 exposure in the set: effort-and-will framing converts a symptom (executive dysfunction, fatigue,
depression) into a character shortfall. Rewritten to "was built over time" — same weight, cause not
located in the person's will.

## Supporting others — the same process, used with another person (added 2026-08-05, provisional, on Hunter product approval, `D-0064`)

**Authority and status.** Hunter directed this addition, 2026-08-05, under delegated product
authority (`K-11`); committee ratification remains separately owed. **Claim boundary, stated
precisely because this is where it matters most:** this is **peer support, not mental-health
treatment** — the same skill used on yourself, offered to someone else in a moment. It is not a
claim that MFC "does mental health," and must never be worded that way in public copy; `K-08`'s
existing rules already prohibit implying mental fitness replaces therapy, counselling, or crisis
support, and this addition sits fully inside that boundary, not beside it.

**Why it belongs here:** Stop, Shift, Do doesn't have to be run alone. The same three moves work as
a shared sequence between two people — one supporting, one in the moment:

| Move | Alone | With someone |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop** | Catch the moment yourself. | "What's going on for you right now?" / "What are you noticing?" |
| **Shift** | Pick a Shift-menu option. | "Let's take a breath together." / "Want to step outside for a sec?" — offering, not directing. |
| **Do** | Choose your next move. | "What's one next step that could help?" — theirs to answer, not yours to supply. |

**Binding limits, carried directly from `K-08`, not new:** offer, never force (rule 6); use "where
safe and appropriate," name the actual action rather than vague shorthand (rule 5); this is not
diagnosis or intervention (rule 2); when distress or risk is outside the scope of peer support, the
move is to use referral, safeguarding, or crisis pathways, not to keep applying this sequence (rule
10). **Knowing when to stop and bring in more support is part of the skill being taught, not a
separate afterthought.**

## Stop, Shift, Do

> Stop — catch the moment.
> Shift — create enough space where useful and available.
> Do — make the next move with intention.

- **Stop** commonly draws on **Notice** and **Focus** — catching the moment requires both noticing it's happening and having attention available to catch it with.
- **Shift** is the parent verb — the moment you change something to become workable. **You can shift your state, or you can shift your thinking.** At the deep tier it commonly draws on **State Regulation** (body: energy, arousal, tension) and **Flexible Thinking** (cognitive), and the Shift toolkit's four categories reach further — its *attention* and *connection* categories also draw on **Attention** and **Relating to Others**. The toolkit's four categories are the **internal taxonomy**; the public menu names (Move it / Zoom / Ask a different question / Bring someone in) are what learners meet. Facilitators should use the menu names aloud, not the bare category words, since "attention" is also deep-tier vocabulary (`D-0084`).
- **Do** commonly draws, at the deep tier, on **Values-Based Action**, on **Relating to Others** where another person is involved, and on **Relating to Yourself** where the next move concerns you rather than someone else.
- *"These are not fixed mappings or separate skill steps."* At the public tier, Do maps to **Choose**; at the deep tier it draws on three (Values-Based Action, Relating to Others, Relating to Yourself) — not a defect, since acting on what matters is genuinely supported by more than one deep capability, disclosed the same way Shift's and Connect's public folds now are.
- **Choose vs Do:** *"Do is the moment-level step in Stop, Shift, Do. Choose is the public skill underneath that step (Values-Based Action, at the deep tier)."* Restored `D-0081`, rekeyed `D-0084`.
- Misunderstandings named in source: Stop is not freezing; Shift is not always calming down; Do is not the perfect action; SSD is not another set of skills or the whole framework.

## State Check

> "Is my state too much, too little, or workable for this moment?"

| Language | Meaning | Guides Shift |
|---|---|---|
| Too much | More activation, intensity, urgency, tension, overwhelm, reactivity or load than the moment can use well. | Downshift toward workable. |
| Workable | Enough access to choice for this moment. Does not mean perfect calm. | May need no state shift. |
| Too little | Less energy, clarity, connection, alertness or mobilisation than the moment needs. | Upshift toward workable. |

**Lock-in:** does not change Stop, Shift, Do; not a fourth step. *"For this moment"* is the anchor phrase.

### What the delivery scripts teach, added 2026-09-17

**Why this is here.** The table above compresses the State Check into a column headed *"Guides
Shift"*, which reads as routing: a state is classified and the classification selects the response.
**On 2026-09-16 a test of MFC's own no-identification clause was run against this table and concluded
that MFC fails its own clause.** Reading the delivery scripts reversed that verdict the next day.
**Anyone reasoning from this record alone will reach the wrong answer**, so what the scripts actually
teach is recorded here.

**It asks how much, not what.** `03-DELIVERY-SCRIPT-INTRODUCTION-60.md`, spoken:

> *"Is there too much in me right now, too little, or is this workable? That's it. **Not what am I
> feeling, how much.** You're not scoring it and I'm never going to ask you for your answer."*

**That is a magnitude on one axis, not a category lookup.** The distinction is load-bearing: moodgym
requires identifying which of ten named distortions applies and then applying a matched disputation
from six; Positive Intelligence returns a profile that determines what you work on. **A single
intensity reading with one fixed target is a different kind of thing.**

**The response is invariant.** Whatever the reading, the move is toward workable. The direction
follows from where the person already is; it is not selected from a set. **A thermostat is not a
menu.**

**It is offered, never required.** The Introduction offers it as *"a version of that you **can**
ask"*. `29-DELIVERY-SCRIPT-ESSENTIALS-90.md` carries it as a locked workplace boundary:

> **never mandatory, scored, public or performance-based, never a poll, never in the chat.**

**Workable is defined by capacity, not by mood.** *"Workable is not good. Workable is: I could do the
next thing from here."*

**And Essentials teaches what the readings feel like**, because *"too much and too little are not
obvious from the inside"*: too much is fast rather than dramatic, talking over the ends of sentences,
re-reading a line four times; too little is flat, *"not upset, just gone"*, and people miss it
because it feels like being tired.

**Consequence for the clause coding.** On `C2`, no identification step, **MFC clears both halves of
the test**: the check is not required to run the routine, and it does not select the response. Full
working at `60-projects/market-intelligence-2026-10/14-THE-CLAUSE-TEST.md`. **This is not a
differentiator**, because ACT's drop anchor, the three-minute breathing space, 5-4-3-2-1 and DBT's
STOP all clear it as well.

## Mental rep — locked public definition

1. **An immediate benefit, in that moment** — the actual outcome of catching and choosing on purpose,

## Stop, Shift, Do to Mental Rep — the counting rule (added 2026-08-05, provisional, on Hunter product approval)

**Authority and status, stated precisely, same pattern as the Radar addition above.** Hunter
approved this into the product on 2026-08-05, closing `LI-04`/`CF-01` (framework-integrity-observatory,

> caught before it happened, while it was happening, or afterwards.** Shift is not required to have
> been used: if State Check reads "workable," a Stop → Do pass with no explicit Shift step still

> intention-dependent — applied explicitly to its relationship with Stop, Shift, Do, which K-07

**State Check and Shift — the boundary, named explicitly.** State Check and Shift are not two

too little), Shift is the *response*, and both legitimately touch the same domains (body, attention,

material — diagnosing tension in the body and downshifting the body are not the same move. This was
implicit in K-07's existing text; it is now stated so the relationship is not mistaken for
duplication.
