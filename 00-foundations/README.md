# 00-foundations

The minimum MFC must consistently understand. Twelve notes. (Formerly named `00-kernel` —
renamed 2026-08-04 for plain-language clarity; the `K-` note prefix is unchanged.)

**Rules**
- Every statement is a verbatim quotation from a source-verified document. No paraphrase.
- All notes are `provisional`. Only Hunter or the committee may move a note to `confirmed`.
- AI may transcribe and verify. AI may not originate, paraphrase, approve, or resolve a conflict.
- Where these foundations and any other note disagree, the foundations govern and a conflict record is raised.

Storage mode: `dual-controlled`. Drive holds the originals; Obsidian holds the controlled statement.
