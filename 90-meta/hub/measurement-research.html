Why this area matters

Make MFC's mission testable through private organisation-level evaluation, while building toward a credible comparative effectiveness study without overstating evidence.

Where are we?

The measurement approach and the five participant forms are final. Hunter sent the four-page paper and the eight-page question schedule to Debra Fidler, Eimear Quigley and Onno van-es on 16 September 2026. 53 questions across five forms: Introduction post-only; Essentials and Foundations before, after, a short emailed form before the return session, and an in-room block at the return session carrying WHO-5, the matched question, unaided recall and all six IMTTAQ items. Two published instruments reproduced verbatim. The structure is settled; question wording is open to advisor comment until a cohort answers it.

What do we currently believe?

Standard measured services can show what MFC delivered and what respondents reported learning/using; a separate comparative study is needed to test intervention effectiveness.
