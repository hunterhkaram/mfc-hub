How the model is intended to sustain access

MFC goes to market with TWO products, decided 2026-09-18 (D-0140). THE HALF DAY, for organisations: $3,900 base + $95 per person, ex GST, capped at 20, about $4,850 for a room of ten. A 45-minute leader session, half a day with the team, a return session three to six weeks later where the measurement is taken, and a written report. THE ONE-HOUR SESSION, for talks, learning lunches and community groups: $600 up to 20 people, $800 to 50, $1,000 above that. $1,000 is a ceiling. The whole routine taught, comprehension measured before and after, no report, credited in full against a half day booked within 90 days. ESSENTIALS IS RETIRED as a sold product and is not advertised; it remains reachable as a configuration. MEASUREMENT IS NOT REMOVABLE from the half day, because MFC delivers training with measurement; the hour is the only exception. What can come off the half day: the manager session, in which case the managers attend the half day itself; and the report, which takes 2.5 hours off rather than 4 because the form review happens either way. The return session cannot come off. Reports are a half-day product only. THE FLOOR is (hours x $66.10 + materials) x 1.4, which for a full half day is $1,767.18, or 2.74x at ten people. Materials are $50 a half day and $25 an hour, costed 2026-09-18 from what a session needs, replacing an inherited $150/$60/$40. Below pay plus materials with no overhead, $1,262 for a half day, the facilitator works unpaid. Quarterly check-ins are offered, never sold, and MFC chooses who. MFC is registering for GST; every published figure restates on the day it lands. SUPERSEDED IN PART BY D-0140 and marked as such: D-0129, D-0128, D-0125, D-0098, 110-PRODUCT-AND-PRICING-BOOK, 07-BUYER-PACK, K-12, FINAL-08. Take no price or product line-up from any of them.

The cross-subsidy is a strategic intention, not a demonstrated outcome: MFC currently has no customers and no evidenced access ripple.
