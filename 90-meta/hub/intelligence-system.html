MFC Intelligence System

The Hub can keep the vault's organisational picture current, but work in unconnected systems still needs to be checked and reconciled.

Open the readable system record →

What’s next?

Show Hunter the current Half-Day build and founder-readable rationale; continue role promotion retests and real-world evidence gates without treating holdout success as professional approval.

Why this area matters

Give Hunter one dependable place to understand what MFC knows, what has changed, what matters next and what still needs human judgement.

What it can do today

  • Cold-start retrieval
    A new working session can recover the current MFC portfolio and priorities from the vault.
  • Canonical continuation and anti-duplication
    New work updates the existing main record instead of creating a competing version; the grants test demonstrated this end to end.
  • Start/progress/completion reconciliation
    All 15 organisational areas record their latest material change, and an accepted update regenerates and checks the Hub automatically.
  • Specialist assurance
    Every future material task receives a unique task identity and cannot stop without a matching, reasoned whole-bench routing record. This does not depend on the model saying done or complete. The closure gate automatically runs the universal validator, which checks any bounded, independent or restricted call for its five-layer task packet, current sources, exact artifact, bounded question, boundaries, unique substantive review, source trace and learning disposition. Tests prove a missing or invalid receipt blocks and a matching valid receipt passes. A real contract-complete workshop panel must still prove advice quality end to end.
  • External-system continuity
    Checks of sent Gmail and official grant sources corrected MFC's recorded state. Work in unconnected systems still needs a deliberate check.
  • Strategic premise challenge
    The Committee workflow challenged the starting assumption and showed ranked alternatives before recommending a direction. Broader evidence is still limited.
  • Design intelligence before production
    The Half-Day cycle now includes an independent coverage challenge, 18 unique bounded reviews, applied corrections and affected-role rechecks. It remains an internal candidate pending Hunter and field/human gates.
  • Pre-production decision terminology and source-lifecycle reconciliation
    The Half-Day test caught an older rejected workshop sequence before it became the current build. The deck, guide and participant copy now agree; independent review remains open.
  • Minimum-complete Hub visibility
    The Hub covers 15 organisational areas plus the intelligence system, including current priorities, ownership, evidence, open decisions and known limitations.
  • Agent contribution visibility
    The Hub and open-questions tool derive evidenced use, findings and links from the same validated routing records; the bench defines roles and limits but is not a manual usage log.
  • Equipped specialist knowledge and learning
    All 25 roles have source-graded professional equipment and prior MFC learning; all passed a concealed holdout with no observed critical failure. Twenty-three remain baseline and two restricted because the independent checker withheld every promotion until the full standard and real-world gates are met.
  • Archive and supersession safety
    Older work remains available for context but is clearly prevented from presenting itself as the current source.

What it still cannot do automatically

  • Work completed outside the vault can remain invisible until connector readback or explicit reconciliation.
  • A stale local operational artifact can drive a correct retrieval of the wrong organisational state.
  • Two outcome-quality capabilities in the wider suite remain self-test-only and inspect no live artifact.
  • Intermediate progress or waiting is not a universal event stream; it becomes visible when the working session or an external connector records the transition. Once accepted the receipt now regenerates and verifies the Hub automatically.
  • The closure gate depends on the intake classifier correctly identifying material MFC work; live artifact quality still requires accountable judgement.
  • A routing record without a valid date, parseable structure and linked evidence is excluded from contribution scoring and surfaced as a warning until corrected.
  • The first workshop synthesis proves role lenses can change artifacts; it did not complete the bounded specialist call contract and does not establish independent expert performance, participant value or generalise across objectives.
  • A source-linked baseline pack does not prove expert performance; the first panel is one live case, not the full premise-challenge, ambiguity, boundary and stale-source evaluation set.
  • The universal contract validator can prove required context and review evidence were preserved; it cannot prove the advice is professionally correct, which still requires evaluation, independent challenge and qualified humans where applicable.

What we are improving now

  • Bring Hunter the current viewable Half-Day build and rationale; then run the fresh rendered Compass review and the predeclared room, participant and qualified-human gates.
  • Replace the two self-test-only outcome controls with live-artifact evaluations.
  • Map the missing finance people cash-runway delivery-administration and partnership records into operations-governance without creating another database.
  • Reconcile authenticated external work whenever it changes rather than assuming local state is current.
  • Maintain linked routing records for genuinely material specialist work; do not manually update the bench's non-authoritative usage counters.

What recent work has taught us

  • Retrieval accuracy is not continuity if the underlying state is stale.
  • A generated Hub is not useful unless it exposes evidence, uncertainty, transitions and system health.
  • Specialist assurance must represent contract-complete executed work, not a named role, a shared synthesis or a recent file path.
  • A validator that can be skipped is not universal; each material intake must be bound to the evidence required at closure.
  • A historical production source can contain valid current content under a retired label; reconcile it against the controlling decision before building rather than trusting either filename or recency.
  • Artifact preflight must resolve authority and lifecycle before it optimises count, layout or wording; a better rendering of the wrong source is still the wrong artifact.
  • A coherent candidate is not necessarily the strongest candidate; divergence, specialist challenge and a visible rationale must precede founder finalisation.
  • A specialist name and method are not enough; credible advice requires maintained professional knowledge, MFC context, source lineage, prior corrections and live calibration.
