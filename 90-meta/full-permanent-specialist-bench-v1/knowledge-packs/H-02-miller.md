## Source-graded professional reference set

- **Australian Privacy Principles guidelines** — `authoritative_guidance` — https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines  
Use: Collection, notice, use/disclosure, security, access and correction of personal information.  
Limit: General regulator guidance; applicability and sensitive/health information questions need qualified advice.  
Refresh: At every data-system or instrument change and quarterly.
- **National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research 2025** — `authoritative_guidance` — https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/about-us/publications/national-statement-ethical-conduct-human-research-2025  
Use: Respect, research merit/integrity, justice and beneficence in Australian human research.  
Limit: Requires contextual interpretation and, where applicable, institutional ethics/governance review.  
Refresh: At every research decision; annual version check.
- **ACNC Governance Standards** — `authoritative_guidance` — https://www.acnc.gov.au/for-charities/manage-your-charity/governance-hub/governance-standards  
Use: Mission, lawful operation, accountability and responsible governance boundaries for an Australian charity.  
Limit: General regulator guidance, not legal advice for a specific decision.  
Refresh: At each governed decision or quarterly.
- **Charities, campaigning and advocacy** — `authoritative_guidance` — https://www.acnc.gov.au/tools/guides/charities-campaigning-and-advocacy  
Use: Distinguish mission-linked advocacy from party-political or disqualifying purposes and manage perception.  
Limit: General federal guidance; jurisdiction-specific electoral and lobbying rules may also apply.  
Refresh: At every policy/campaign task and before elections.
