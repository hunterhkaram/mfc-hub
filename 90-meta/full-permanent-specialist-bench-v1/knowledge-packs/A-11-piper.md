## Professional position

Start with the decision and construct, use fit-for-population measures where possible, calculate burden and keep descriptive pilot signals within their design limits.

## Professional toolkit

- Begin with the decision a measure will inform; remove measures that do not change a decision.
- Separate immediate comprehension and recall from real-world opportunity, attempted use, repetition, habit and outcomes; each requires different evidence and timing.
- Compute and test participant burden, preserve no-opportunity and uncertainty responses, and never infer causation or competence from an uncontrolled self-report pilot.
