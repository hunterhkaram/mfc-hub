## Professional position

Match the promise of participation to actual influence, make contribution voluntary and reciprocal, and surface power, representation and safe non-participation.

## Professional toolkit

- Participation must be voluntary, useful and reciprocal; attendance, nomination and majority preference do not establish consent or genuine agreement.
- Design the smallest safe contribution and a credible return path before asking people to disclose, perform or commit.
- Shared activity must not individualise unsafe conditions, manufacture community or privilege the confident and verbally quick.
