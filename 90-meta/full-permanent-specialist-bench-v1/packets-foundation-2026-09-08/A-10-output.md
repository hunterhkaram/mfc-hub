# A-10 (Reed) — bounded specialist review, 2026-09-08

**Pack status.** A-10 "Reed", learning and behaviour design. Pack state `evaluated_pack`, last
review 2026-08-13, maintainer A-23. **The limit, once and up front:** that evaluation tests

## Bounded question

What is the honest movement structure of the participant foundation, derived only from the four
grounded sources, and what compression rule produces 58 / 90 / 240? Every number that cannot be
grounded is to be named as ungrounded rather than asserted.

- `03-DELIVERY-SCRIPT-INTRODUCTION-60.md` — the only depth that exists as spoken words.
- `02-MERGED-DESIGN-ALL-THREE-PRODUCTS.md` — §1 (the beats), §12 (the depth ladder), the cut orders,

## Professional principles applied

1. **Segment, signal and sequence interacting concepts.** A structure is only a foundation if the
   order carries the dependency, not the author's convenience — which is why each movement is
   written with what breaks without it rather than a title.
2. **Retrieval supports retention but does not prove real-world transfer.** Nothing in this file may
   imply the room's in-session performance predicts what happens on Thursday.
3. **Progress from high support to lower support.** The compression rule must never produce a
   shorter product that demands *more* of the participant than a longer one.
4. **Do not assert a number that no artefact can check.** A duration with no script behind it is
   design intent, and must be labelled as such.

## Position

**The foundation has fourteen movements, not seventeen — and the reason is evidential, not
aesthetic.**

Fourteen is the only count carried by a governing record: `D-0130` ruled it, `02-MERGED` §1 builds
it, `03-DELIVERY-SCRIPT` writes it as spoken words. The seventeen came from misreading the founder
capture, which is laid out in **eighteen** numbered steps — not seventeen — and which Hunter
disowned as a running order in the same file: *"that's the arc of what I've said... It doesn't mean
it's the best arc."* All eighteen arc steps map onto the fourteen movements; none is orphaned; five
land across more than one movement, which is left as an honest many-to-one rather than forced into
symmetry.

The previously asserted 17 per-movement floors were **not** reproduced. A-24 had already refuted
them as unauditable because no file existed to check them against, and reproducing them would have
laundered an unchecked claim into a vault record.

## Recommendation

File `22-THE-PARTICIPANT-FOUNDATION.md` as written, with six numbered compression rules: no movement
is ever removed; the order never changes; nothing is taught at 90 or 240 that is not present at 58;
durations are monotonic; a named never-compress floor list; and the buffer is not a cut. **State the
manager session as NOT a compression of this foundation**, with the path to
`03-MANAGER-SESSION-DESIGN.md`. Bind Hunter's ruling 1 — *a room cannot perform a Do* — as a rule
constraining the 240 as hard as the 58, not as a note about the hour.

**Do not** amend `02-MERGED` §1/§12 wholesale in this pass. The 90 and 240 columns need a real
re-derivation, and a two-cell patch would hide the gap rather than close it.

## Evidence strength and limitations

**Weak, and specifically so.** Every duration in the structure is ungrounded — MFC has delivered
zero cohorts and nothing is observed. The entire 90 and 240 per-movement ladder is design intent
asserted in `02-MERGED` §12; no 90-minute or 240-minute script exists, so no word count can check
it. Only the 58 is checkable, and only because its rows came from real word counts. 194 and 81 are
internally consistent and externally unvalidated. The 10.5 / 14 / 45 recoverable totals rest on a
120–130 wpm estimate; the 90/240 rows there are duration subtractions of exactly the class that
produced the old C4 defect. The ~85% coffee figure and the hospice-regrets study are both
unregistered in `10-sources/` and both excluded; movement 13 is written to work without the latter.

**A live inconsistency found during the review and deliberately not papered over:** `02-MERGED` §1
and §12 carried 56 + 4 with movement 9 at five minutes, while the script carries 58 + 2 with
movement 9 at seven, shifting every later movement by two. **The 90 and 240 columns were derived
against the 56 version and have never been re-derived against 58.** Movement 9's monotonicity was
checked by hand and survives (7 → 8 → 24). Nothing else in those columns has been rechecked.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep seventeen movements and reconcile downward.** Rejected: seventeen has no governing record
  and originates in a misreading of a capture Hunter explicitly disowned as a running order.
- **Make the foundation 270 "natural" minutes with compression ranks.** Rejected: the ranks and
  floors are unauditable, and inventing a longest-form product that has never been written or costed
  would put a fictional artefact at the root of every real one.
- **Fold the manager session in as a fourth compression.** Rejected on Hunter's own instruction and
  on the design: its audience, its job and its claim boundary are all different.
- **Patch `02-MERGED` §12's 90/240 columns to match 58 now.** Rejected: it would produce numbers
  nobody derived, which is worse than a visible gap.

## Strongest objection

**Fourteen may be deferring to the most recent record rather than to the best structure.** `D-0130`
is eight days old and has never had an adversarial pass; treating it as the grounding authority
risks canonising a structure precisely because it was written down, which is the failure mode this
vault has already recorded twice. The honest answer is that fourteen is the best-*evidenced* count,
not the demonstrated-best count, and the file says so rather than resolving it.

A second objection: a foundation whose every duration is ungrounded may not deserve the word
*foundation*. The counter is that its load-bearing content is the **order and the dependencies**,
which are grounded in the script — the minutes are the part flagged as intent.

## What would change this view

One real delivery. A single room sitting through the 58 would settle the durations, whether fourteen
movements is one too many, whether the *you cannot do a Do here* statement reads as honesty or as an
anticlimax, and whether the peak at movement 13 survives being followed by three minutes of
logistics. Second: a written 90 or 240 script would make those columns checkable for the first time.

all. **The narrow addition worth making: a structure that has been shown to Hunter in an artifact
but has no vault file is not a candidate, it is a draft, and must not be cited as the basis of
another decision.** Offered as a candidate; not written as a new lesson, per the §12 governance
freeze.
