## Position

**The `R6` answer this build was pointed at is superseded and must not be pasted forward.** `R7` carries `supersedes_in_part: "R6 — two facts in R6 are corrected here"`, and §0 verifies three Black Dog programmes, the third staff-facing. **R6's audience distinction is not merely weaker — it is false on the fact it turns on**, and stating it to a buyer would be a misrepresentation of a competitor (ACL s18 territory; charitable purpose is not an exemption).

**A second differentiator also has to go.** Black Dog publishes *"84% of employees or sole traders report they've used at least one wellbeing strategy"*. So *"we measure whether it got used"* is not MFC's differentiator. What survives is verified and narrower: **per-employer measurement, before and after, returned as a document.**

**On two-tier pricing: no external basis constrains it and I am not creating one.** Differential pricing by buyer type is ordinary, lawful and precedented (Evolve WA). Required: published, testable eligibility criteria, applied consistently, with a standard rate commercial buyers are genuinely quoted rather than a decorative anchor. **Not required, and must not be built: a means test, a verification process, documentary evidence per buyer, an ACNC notification, a policy document, or separate committee ratification of the tier structure.**

## Strongest objection

**That after both corrections the surviving distinction is too narrow to sell against a free, psychologist-delivered alternative.** `R7` says so itself — *"real and narrow"*, *"much harder to explain to a buyer."* I partly uphold it: the distinction is honest and licensed, and the builder's follow-through found the harder consequence I had not drawn — **the per-employer report cannot be promised below ten respondents, so the answer does not hold at Essentials 2–5, 6–10 or Small Team at all.** Against a competitor whose eligibility covers NFPs of any size in NSW, that removes the answer from most of the segment §4 spends its words on. **That is a positioning problem, not a claims problem, and it is now carried in §4.1, §4.2 and §5.3.**

## Evidence strength and limitations

Black Dog facts are verified at rendered primary pages (2026-08-10) and the closure date at the NSW page (2026-09-05) — but the two repository records agreeing both trace to that one page, so it is one source, not two. `R7` §104 records **no published methodology, sample or timing** behind the 99%/93%/84% figures: MFC may note that absence and may not characterise the figures as unreliable. **`K-08`'s own red lines BD-01 to BD-08 remain pending, so the boundary this section applies is itself unratified.** My searches were vault-only — not Drive, Gmail or the web.

## Alternatives considered

Keeping R6's audience split with a caveat — rejected; a false statement with a caveat is still false. Saying MFC is *"for organisations Black Dog's eligibility excludes"* — rejected as weaker on the current eligibility facts. Requiring committee ratification of the two-tier structure — rejected under §4a as an internally-generated gate. Reinstating a *"no enterprise sales until PI is held"* gate — rejected; it was already declined for lack of external basis, and **what is owed is disclosure, not abstinence.**
