## Professional principles applied

Nothing external-facing is sent; it is drafted and left for Hunter. A publish is its own gate,
separate from closing the work. A verdict is not reported before the evidence for it exists.

## Evidence strength and limitations

The mechanical half is strong and reproducible: the PDF is built from the committed markdown with no
uncommitted drift, `check_paper_consistency.py` passes 51 of 51, all 15 contents entries match the
page each section renders on, the body is 11 pages of 14, three images are embedded and two
captioned, and there are zero placeholders, TODOs, leaked vault paths or `D-`/`K-` references in
what Jake would read.

The judgement half is outstanding. A lens is also not Jake, and cannot predict what he will say.

## Alternatives considered

Sending on the mechanical checks alone was rejected. They establish that the document is correctly
built and internally consistent, not that it reads well to its recipient, and this is a publish gate.

## Recommendation

Hold the send until the read returns. The covering email is to be created as a Gmail draft to
`jake@mentalfitnesscollective.com.au` for Hunter to send himself.

## Strongest objection

That the mechanical checks plus Hunter's own approval are already sufficient, and a further read is
delay. Answerable on the record: the last four cold reads found 14, 16, 13 and 16 findings
respectively, several of which Hunter's own read had passed over.

## What would change this view

The read returning clean, which would make this a send.
