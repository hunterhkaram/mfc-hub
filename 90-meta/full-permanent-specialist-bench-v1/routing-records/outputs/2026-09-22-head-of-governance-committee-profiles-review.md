# head-of-governance review — committee/board profiles collection plan

### Bounded question

Is the committee-profiles collection plan governance-correct against Onno van-Es's own stated
positions (as Board Chair) and NFP/AICD practice, and does it show unnecessary over-caution
(L-084) rather than accurate, load-bearing caution?

### Sources reviewed

- `60-projects/committee-pack-v1/committee-profiles-plan-v1.md` (the artefact under review)
- Onno van-Es's email "Board/Committee Profiles", 20 Sep 2026 (thread `1a0bc340a5c05d4a`) — the
  original brief the plan operationalises
- `50-authority/advisor-guidance-register/REGISTER.md` entries AG-331 (Rachel Willis), AG-332 and
  AG-374 (Onno van-Es), all M12, 2026-09-07
- `60-projects/committee-pack-v1/GOVERNANCE-committee-roster.md` (current roster status)

### Professional principles applied

NFP board-profile practice per AICD guidance (as Onno's own brief states it): governance framing
over operational bio, succinctness, verified credentials, draft → internal edit → member sign-off
before publish, annual refresh synced to the AGM. Standard advisor-precedence practice: apply an
advisor's stated position directly rather than re-deriving it from scratch when it already exists
in the register.

### Position

The plan is governance-correct in structure but was missing two of Onno's and Rachel's own already-
registered positions. Both are now applied: a LinkedIn-verifiability field in the collection
template (AG-331) and a board-diversity read-across step at the internal-review stage (AG-332).
With those added, the plan matches the Chair's own process and AICD/NFP practice, and does not
invent process beyond what Onno already specified.

### Strongest objection

That naming an unconfirmed Secretary role and a missing Board Skills Matrix in a plan that is
merely internal is itself over-cautious — Hunter could simply be told to send the request and sort
gaps out as they surface. Rejected: sending a profile-collection request that silently assumes an
unresolved governance fact (who holds the Secretary role) risks surfacing that gap to the whole
committee at an awkward moment, which is a real, not manufactured, cost. Naming it before send is
cheaper than discovering it mid-send.

### Evidence strength and limitations

Findings are grounded directly in dated advisor-register entries, not inferred. The roster gap and
missing-matrix finding were independently verified this turn (grep across the vault; roster file's
own frontmatter states `DRAFT_FROM_GMAIL_HISTORY_NOT_YET_HUNTER_CONFIRMED`). No external legal or
ACNC-specific check was run — that wasn't in scope; the plan is internal and nothing here creates a
compliance filing.

### Alternatives considered

Considered recommending the plan be sent immediately without the roster/matrix confirmation step,
to reduce turnaround. Rejected for the reason above. Considered flagging over-caution as a general
tone note; rejected in favour of a specific pass/fail verdict, since a vague "read as slightly
cautious" comment would not be actionable.

### Recommendation

Send when Hunter is ready, after (a) confirming the Secretary role and the rest of the roster and
(b) either locating or starting the Board Skills Matrix Onno's brief assumes exists. No further
governance review is required before send unless the roster/matrix facts change materially.

### What would change this view

If Onno indicates the Board Skills Matrix already exists elsewhere and was simply not shared, the
"start one" fallback in the plan becomes unnecessary — replace with "obtain and use it."

### Adjacent specialist or human boundary

head-of-digital owns the actual website page build once profiles are signed off — correctly out of
scope for this turn. Final send timing is Hunter's own call, not a specialist decision.

### Proposed learning

No new lesson. This is routine advisor-register application (AG-331/AG-332), not a system defect.
