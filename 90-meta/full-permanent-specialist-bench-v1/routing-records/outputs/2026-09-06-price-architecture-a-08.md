## Professional principles applied

Front-load loss, because the buyer's own documented exposure is always licensable; constrain gain to what the product factually is and does. `AG-020`/`AG-025`/`AG-026`: an explicit what's-in-it-for-me, not buried under caveats. `AG-001`: the programme *targets* X and the research *shows* Y — never that MFC produces a result. Answer the buyer's strongest recorded objection inside the passage rather than around it.

## Position

Six passages, one per buyer type, each naming entry product, price, why it fits them and a next step. The purpose-led 20–50 passage is the strongest because it is the only segment where MFC holds a real recorded buyer voice and a named structural asset — the recurring meeting rhythm MFC does not have to build. The mid-commercial passage deliberately **leads with the compliance limit rather than the compliance trigger**, because the SafeWork NSW Code makes training a lower-order control and any vendor claiming otherwise loses in front of a competent WHS adviser.

**On the free competitor: `20-THE-PRICING-CASE.md` §7.2's claim that *"MFC currently has no sentence to answer this with"* is false.** `R6` has carried a drafted answer since 10 August. The real defect is propagation — it is committee-facing, never reached a buyer-facing artefact, and no `D-` record exists either way.

## Strongest objection

**That my answer sentence was built on a distinction that is factually wrong, and would have collapsed on the buyer's first click.** Upheld. H-02 found `R7` §0 supersedes `R6` in part: Black Dog runs **three** programmes, not two, and the third — *Your Mental Health at Work* — is staff-facing and names sole traders and apprentices. R6's *"they own managers, we own staff"* is false. I had half-conceded this in my own §5.5 and still used the audience framing in the answer sentence. **The distinction that survives is one of form — a taught menu versus a rehearsed procedure — plus per-employer measurement returned as a document.**

Second: two of my six passages are warnings rather than propositions. If the brief needed six sellable passages, I delivered four and two honest refusals.

## Evidence strength and limitations

The purpose-led passage rests on one recorded buyer voice and a structural asset **no buyer has ever confirmed matters to them**. The mid-commercial persona is explicitly an inference from regulatory structure, not a held quote. **Zero buyer interviews have ever been conducted and no passage here has met a buyer.** `P27` — the one sentence that would close a sale — cannot be filled: its citation does not exist. The 30 September end date is doubly recorded but both records trace to the same NSW page, and I verified in the repository rather than at source.

## Alternatives considered

Leading the commercial passage with the WHS trigger — rejected; it is the strongest trigger in the market and the one MFC must decline. Using Black Dog's own 84% figure against them — rejected; repeating a competitor's registered claim in a selling context makes it MFC's comparative claim. *"We are for organisations Black Dog's eligibility excludes"* as the position — rejected as the weaker of two: their eligibility covers NFPs of any size, which is most of MFC's primary segment. Treating the 30 September closure as a deadline — rejected; removing a free alternative removes a reason not to buy and is not a reason to act.

## Recommendation

Adopt the six passages and the form-based answer sentence at §5.3. Strike §7.2's false absence claim from the pricing case. **Record the free-competitor position as a `D-` record** — a sentence with no decision behind it will be re-litigated by the next agent that reads `R6`. Carry the report condition into §4.1, §4.2 and §5.3: the differentiator is the per-employer report and it cannot be promised below ten respondents, so the answer is sellable only from Essentials 11–15 upward.

## What would change this view

The first buyer who says *"we can get this free from Black Dog"* — `20-THE-PRICING-CASE.md` §7.2 is right that this is the test that settles it. A successor to the NSW free stack being announced. Any registered comparable study that fills `P27`. And a single buyer interview, which would replace most of §4 with evidence.
