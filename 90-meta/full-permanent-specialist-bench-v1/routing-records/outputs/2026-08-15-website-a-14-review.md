# A-14 bounded review, website direction remediation, 2026-08-15

## Bounded question
Is the strategy-versus-craft ownership split defensible, does the report over-reach into creative territory, and would the proposed direction produce a distinctive brand experience or merely a well-organised one?

## Professional principles applied
Brand and Creative Direction, executed as remediation after the A-24 coverage challenge upheld this exclusion as wrong.

## Position
The exclusion reason given, preserving Alysse's design-execution authority, did not survive contact with the artefact: the report claimed the heading layer, invented a mandatory copy unit, killed two lines of hero copy and renamed the primary navigation. The ownership split was wrong as drawn and right one layer down: strategy owns the claim line, the designer owns every word the reader sees, headings included. The report's Remove list would have deleted MFC's own governed distinctive example, "You were never taught how to train your mind", along with the generic imperative. The experience specification is written entirely in a loss register while the brief requires the win register too, and "mental wins deserve celebration" was protected in section 6 and then architected nowhere. The nav label "Why This Matters" fails the brief's binary category test outright. Red, amber, green is MFC's only native visual system and was treated as a mnemonic.

## Strongest objection
That this is an argument specification with no experience specification, and it will produce a site that is right and quiet. Accepted: the recognition beat now carries a failure test and a first-screen hierarchy rule rather than only a placement instruction.

## Evidence strength and limitations
No live-site inspection since 31 July 2026, and that was a preview environment. No analytics, no moderated
sessions, no participants of any kind. Meeting attributions are contextual inferences, not a labelled record.

## Alternatives considered
Leaving the split as drafted, rejected. Specifying visual direction, rejected as outside this lens's authority and inside Alysse's.

## Recommendation
Add creative direction telling the designer what the recognition beat must do to a reader, not only where it sits, and give her room to decide how.

## What would change this view
A rendered visual candidate; the Audience Intelligence master sheet; any moderated session evidence.

## Adjacent specialist or human boundary
Whether MFC has a visual and verbal identity at all beyond compliance rules is bigger than the website and is Hunter's with Alysse. What a celebrated mental win looks like without becoming a testimonial sits across creative and claims and was unowned.

## Proposed learning
Excluding the lens that would contest a report's own territorial claim is the failure the coverage challenge exists to catch. This pack has no room, print or projector evidence; on design execution Alysse is the authority and this is an input.
