# A-08 — contradiction sweep: what MFC claims and what it sells

## Bounded question

Does the paper assert a position in one place and contradict it in another, on what MFC claims and what it sells?

## Position

**Two contradictions, both introduced by today's funnel work.** Three places said MFC never sells cold: *Nothing is sold cold* in section 5, *every approach starts with somebody who already knows us, rather than a cold approach to a stranger* in section 6, and *MFC sells nothing cold* in the glossary. Section 7 models **200 cold approaches producing four of the twenty sessions**, a fifth of the year. Jake settled which is current in the recording: *Cold is stabbing in the dark. We'll stab, but it might be a really low conversion.* Separately, the executive summary said warm approaches and rooms that already meet feed the email list, while section 7 assigns that to social, the website and the Radar.

## Sources reviewed

- `60-projects/committee-pack-oct-2026/THE-CONSOLIDATED-STRATEGY.md`, and its rendered PDF

## Professional principles applied

The contradicting sentence rarely shares keywords with the claim it contradicts, so the reader was
forbidden keyword search and required to read each claim and ask what disagrees with it.

## Evidence strength and limitations

Every finding quotes both sides from the document. The reader cannot verify any claim against an
external source, having been given none.

## Alternatives considered

A single reader across all axes was rejected: it was tried earlier in this workstream and returned
wording preferences rather than contradictions.

## Recommendation

Apply what was found; the detail is in the Position above.

## Strongest objection

That the cold contradiction is a quibble, since warm is genuinely the main route. It is not: the paper states a rule in two places and a glossary definition, then relies on breaking it for a fifth of delivery. The Treasurer reads the funnel.

## What would change this view

A sweep on an unchanged paper that still returned findings.

subject elsewhere becomes a contradiction candidate. Adding the funnel invalidated three sentences
that had been true the day before.
