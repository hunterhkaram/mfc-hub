# A-10 review — the Introduction product's post-only design

*Executed as head-of-product, which declares A-07/A-10/A-12/A-13/A-15/A-16.*

**The logistics argument is defeasible** — an advisor answers it with "so do it for the
twenty-person ones." The argument that holds is the product contract: the Introduction is defined
as no-material, no-writing, no-QR, no-data, no-follow-up and uncapped in group size. A start form
does not add to that product; it changes what the product is.

**Design cost:** the first block is 0–20 minutes of permission, recognition, reframe, and exists
because a keynote room did not choose to be there. A start form spends permission before it is
earned and puts every phone in a hand at minute two.

**Size is a step change, not a slope.** At 20 seated: 2–3 minutes, line of sight, visible
non-response. At 100: 5–8 minutes door-to-done, 8–13% of a fixed hour from the one block that
cannot be compressed, with invisible non-response. Break point ≈ 25–30, the existing
solo-facilitator cap. *(Derived from the question set's own 7-minutes-for-8-items costing, not
measured.)*

**Do not reinstate the retrospective pair.** Nolte et al. 2011 (*Quality of Life Research*
21(3):487–494, randomised, n=949): adding then-test items to the post form raised post-test scores
significantly on 6 of 8 scales. Hill & Betz 2005 (*Am J Eval* 26(4)): retrospective pretests
introduce greater bias, largest on socially desirable items. Other direction, for honesty:
response shift is real (Howard & Dailey 1979) and then-tests have outperformed traditional
pre/post in several programme evaluations (Pratt et al. 2000; Drennan & Hyde 2008; Lyons et al.
2022; Little et al. 2020). Legitimate in general; bad in this configuration — co-administered,
immediately, alongside the satisfaction items. **Note: "cast your mind back to before today" is
itself a then-test**, so it cannot be offered as the substitute for removing one.

**Where the advisors are right:** §5 already flags, unresolved, that item 1 — the prior-exposure
question, word-identical to Form 2 item 1 so the two products are comparable at baseline — is
asked after the hour that changes the answer. That is the one genuinely corrupted item.

**Recommendation:** hold post-only as the standard Form 1, grounded on the product contract rather
than logistics. Add a counted show of hands on item 1 to the run sheet — ~40 seconds, inside the
opening, works at 20 and at 200, no phones, no accessibility problem. Do not pre-ask the
definition or the Stop/Shift/Do order: pre-testing them primes the room for the two answers about
to be taught and scored. Permit an optional one-item start card for booked seated cohorts ≤30 as
a separate additive sheet, post form unchanged, subgroup reported separately, never pooled. Two
forms for the Introduction: no.

**Honest limit:** post-only measures whether the hour landed, not whether the hour caused it. A
high correct-definition rate at the close is compatible with a room that already knew.

**Open contradiction to name rather than be caught by:**
`15-current-service-definition-v1/19-MEASUREMENT-FORMS-SOURCE-RECONCILIATION-v1.md` rules
Introduction measurement out of the standard service as conflicting with the no-data contract,
while Form 1 puts a form in the Introduction.

## Professional principles applied

Reliability and validity are properties of a scored item set, not of a booklet. A coefficient is
cited to the study that produced it and never adopted as the user's own. Instruments are
reproduced verbatim under their licence or not used. Matched comparison requires byte-identical
administration at both ends. Retrospective self-report is contaminated by the content that
precedes it. An evaluation framework is a scaffold for deciding what to collect, never evidence
of effect. Absence of a control group bounds every statement to association.

## Evidence strength and limitations

The subscale-administration argument is strong and rests on the source's own construction method.
Its limit is that published reliability was estimated inside a different administration context
and cannot be asserted to transfer. The Kirkpatrick mapping is verified item by item against the
built forms. The then-test finding rests on one randomised trial with a directly analogous design
plus converging non-randomised evidence, with contrary evidence stated. The delivery-time figures
are **derived** from the question set's own costing, not measured. Zero cohorts have been
delivered, so nothing here is field-evidenced. The evidence-library counts are a live verified
API read; the exclusion record is not proportionate and is flagged as such.

## Alternatives considered

Administering the full IMTTAQ — rejected on participant burden against a 60-minute product and a
return session already at capacity. Rewriting the two subscales into MFC wording — rejected: a
rewritten subscale is not the instrument and forfeits the only borrowed validity in the design.
Reinstating the retrospective pre/post pair on Form 1 — rejected on Nolte 2011. A second
Introduction form at the start — rejected in favour of one facilitator-recorded number, which
survives every delivery format. A new evidence-library rubric — rejected: one already exists and
the governance freeze requires extending it.

## Recommendation

Hold the IMTTAQ position with the reliability caveat stated aloud. Send a permission email to the
LTSI rights-holder and point Eimear's offer at WHO-5's non-commercial licence, which is the
sharper problem. Hold Form 1 post-only on the product contract; add a counted show of hands on the
prior-exposure item; do not pre-ask the definition or the step order. Adopt the ten consistency
rules and fix the eight violations. Ratify the existing admission standard with the two questions
put to the advisors. Above all: raise the participant-code contradiction first, unprompted.

## Strongest objection

That MFC has let the hardest delivery case — a 100-person keynote — write the measurement rule for
every case, including booked rooms of twenty where none of those constraints apply; and that
without a baseline the two knowledge items report whether the room already knew rather than
whether MFC taught them, which is the only thing the form was said to be for. Both halves are
conceded, and answered by the format-conditional rule plus the show-of-hands count.

## What would change this view

A published subscale-extraction study showing material reliability degradation for these two
subscales. Obtaining the fielded IMTTAQ stem, which would make benchmarking available. A
five-person comprehension test of the built forms contradicting any wording position here — that
test is ordered in the question set, has never been run, and would outrank every argument above.
Cohort-one data showing the clarity item ceilings at baseline, which would invalidate the
knowing–doing frame outright. An advisor ruling that the recipe code is acceptable in MFC's actual
threat model.

## Proposed learning

Candidate, not yet a lesson: a specification and its build diverged silently at least four times
in this artefact set — the ruled-out participant code, the conditional display, the comprehension
item's distractors, and an item recorded as removed but built. The common cause is that the build
was regenerated from a later draft without a diff against the governing record, and no control
compares built artefacts to their specification. Whether that warrants a control is a judgement
against the governance freeze; the cheaper answer may be a single diff step in the form generator.
