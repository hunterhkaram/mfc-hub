# H-02 — claims boundary on the consolidated strategy

## Bounded question

Does any claim in the paper breach the claims boundary, and is every comparative claim stated at a
strength MFC's own census can carry?

## Position

**Mostly inside the boundary. One breach, now fixed.** The paper bounds every comparative claim to
the census and then drops the qualifier once, two sentences after modelling how to do it properly.

## Sources reviewed

- `60-projects/committee-pack-oct-2026/THE-CONSOLIDATED-STRATEGY.md`, read in full
- `00-foundations/K-08-claims-red-lines.md`
- `50-authority/advisor-guidance-register/REGISTER.md`, AG-001, AG-008, AG-027, AG-292, AG-293

## Professional principles applied

A comparative claim is stated at the strength its evidence carries. Over-hedging and overclaiming
are symmetric failures and both are reported. A claims read is not legal advice.

## Alternatives considered

Prefixing the offending sentence with "Among the 58", as first proposed. Rejected: its subject is
free techniques, which are not the 58, so that bound the claim to the wrong set and left it equally
unsupported. Adding a caveat sentence was rejected under AG-027 and L-084.

## Recommendation, and what was applied

**Blocking.** *"Nobody teaches them, cues them, follows them up, or arranges conditions around
them."* An unbounded universal negative about every actor in the world, where the surrounding
sentences all say "we found", "of the 58", "the census found".

The proposed fix was to prefix "Among the 58". **Not taken, and the reasoning matters**: the subject
of that sentence is free techniques — blog posts, apps, social content — which are not the 58.
Bounding it to the census would have bounded it to the wrong set and left it equally unsupported.
Applied instead: *"They come with nobody to teach them, cue them, follow them up, or arrange
conditions around them."* A statement about the artefact rather than about the world; true by
inspection; no hedge added.

**Worth fixing, applied.** The executive summary said *"publicly reports whether people use the
technique as an outcome"* where section 2 says *"whether people actually used what they were
taught."* Now identical, so the claim cannot drift between the two places a reader compares.

## What was checked and cleared

- The permitted-claims statement in section 4 against AG-001: uses the aims framing, and explicitly
  refuses the outcome form *"this reduces absenteeism"*.
- The risk table, the twelve-month picture and the glossary for stray outcome claims: none. The
  Mental Rep entry actively guards against outcome framing.
- The measurement claims against AG-008: what section 2 describes is process and adherence
  measurement, not a clinical outcome, and is correctly distinguished.

## Evidence strength and limitations

Strong on the text, which was read in full. This is not legal advice and not clinical clearance;
Eimear and the committee hold that authority.

## Strongest objection

That recasting the sentence dodges the question rather than answering it — MFC still believes
nobody does this. True, and the belief may be right; but the paper cannot evidence it, and the
recast says the part MFC can stand behind without retreating from the argument.

## What would change this view

Evidence that MFC can substantiate the unbounded claim about free techniques, which would make the
original sentence defensible as written.

## Adjacent specialist or human boundary

Whether the recast still sells is A-08's judgement. Hunter approves; the committee governs. This is
not legal advice and not clinical clearance.

## Proposed learning

No new lesson. The failure is already covered: an unqualified absolute about third parties is the
class `check_absolutes` exists for, and it fired correctly on a replacement sentence in this same
session.
