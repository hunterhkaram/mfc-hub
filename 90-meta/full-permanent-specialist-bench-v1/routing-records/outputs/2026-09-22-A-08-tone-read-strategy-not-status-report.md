# A-08 tone read — strategy, or status report?

## Bounded question

Does the paper read as the organisation's strategy or as a status report hedged with what has not
been done yet, and is its scope clear in the opening?

## Position

**It reads as a strategy, and passed.** Eight local phrasings pulled against it, and one sentence
appeared twice. All are now fixed. The opening states the document type, the audience, the
twenty-four-month horizon and the year-one detail above the fold.

## Sources reviewed

- `60-projects/committee-pack-oct-2026/THE-CONSOLIDATED-STRATEGY.md`, read end to end
- `50-authority/advisor-guidance-register/REGISTER.md`, `AG-027` on tone
- `60-projects/committee-pack-oct-2026/HUNTER-REVIEW-2026-09-22.md`
- The task packet at `.../packets/2026-09-22-A-08-tone-read-strategy-not-status-report.yml`

## Professional principles applied

An honest statement of fact stays. The fix for a negative-sounding true thing is confident phrasing,
not deletion. Every finding quotes the document.

## Evidence strength and limitations

Strong on register, because every finding is anchored to quoted text and each came with the same
fact restated. Bounded deliberately: the reviewer was told not to judge content, structure, numbers
or whether the plan is any good.

## Alternatives considered

Deleting the limitations outright was rejected. `K-08` and `AG-001` require the no-cohorts position
to be stated, and a paper that reads confidently by omitting what it cannot claim would fail a
harder test than tone.

## Recommendation

Eight phrasings changed, all applied:

1. *"Three things stand in the way, and all three are finishable"* created a worry then reassured
   against it. Now *"Three things complete it, and we are in the final stages of all three."*
2. *"the year MFC finds out"* → **"Year one is a proving year."**
3. *"One constraint we are managing in the open"* → **"What year one is for."**
4. *"**What we have not done yet is deliver a measured cohort**"*, with the absence in bold, →
   **"The first measured cohort is what year one delivers."**
5. Four stacked negatives in the claims paragraph → *"We state these as aims, and cite the research
   separately."*
6. *"It is tempting to say the pay is not really an expense..."* — the paper arguing with an imagined
   sceptic about the founder's own pay. Reasoning kept, self-litigation removed.
7. *"This is a floor rather than a forecast"* duplicated the limits bullet below it. Collapsed.
8. *"the softer of the two"* → *"carry a wider tolerance than the half day's."*

**The no-cohorts fact appeared seven times; it now appears where a reader is asking for it.** The
word *floor* had been doing hedge duty four times and is now stated once.

## Strongest objection

That trimming hedges shades toward overclaiming. It does not: no limitation was deleted, the claims
discipline is intact and now stated as a choice (*"A claim we can stand behind is worth more than one
we cannot"*), and the cash, cohort and projection positions all remain in the paper.
