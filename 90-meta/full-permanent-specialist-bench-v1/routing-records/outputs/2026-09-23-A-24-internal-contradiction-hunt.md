# A-24 — does the paper contradict itself?

## Bounded question

Judged only against itself, does any number, rule, claim, cross-reference or label in this document
disagree with another place in the same document, and does every sum hold?

## Position

**Five raised, four real, all four fixed. The fifth was wrong and was not acted on.**

The four: the online Hour's "two and a half hours against nearly five" was labelled as preparation
when those are totals; section 7 had the product still finishing to month six while section 8
requires it finished at month three; section 8 listed the return measurement as first true at nine
months when reports are delivered at six; and a standing commitment to "report back what it showed"
on every engagement was broken by the Hour, which produces no report.

## Professional principles applied

Both sides quoted or it is not a contradiction. A number appearing once cannot contradict anything.
The document is the source of truth: a figure is not wrong because an older record disagrees.

## Evidence strength and limitations

Strong on arithmetic, because every sum was recomputed independently: the price ladder, the component
removals, both Appendix C tables, the loaded cost, the contribution table, the year-one totals, the
paid-work figure, the multiples and all fifteen contents entries. Bounded deliberately to
contradiction and arithmetic; tone, repetition and missing content were out of scope.

## Alternatives considered

Accepting the fifth finding and "correcting" the $1,000 figure was considered and rejected on
checking. The reviewer computed the cost with both components off as roughly $838 by removing the
whole $325 report row. Only **2.5 of the report's 4.92 hours** come off, because the cohort's forms
are reviewed either way: $1,262 less $165 less $99 is **$998**, so "about $1,000" is right. The real
defect was that the paper had stopped saying why, having lost the explanation when the table was
trimmed. The explanation was restored and the number left alone.

## Recommendation

All four real contradictions are closed and the fifth is documented as a false positive. The
arithmetic is sound throughout.

## Strongest objection

That a checker briefed to ignore the vault could let a figure stand that contradicts a live decision
record. That is accepted and deliberate: Hunter ruled on 2026-09-23 that this document supersedes
older material. The known live instance is tracked separately, in that `D-0140` carries neither the
$500 online Hour nor the Foundations-only reading of the cap of twenty.

## What would change this view

A number appearing in a place this pass did not treat as a duplicate, or a figure that is internally
consistent but externally wrong.

## Adjacent specialist or human boundary

This checks consistency, not whether the strategy is right. Hunter's own sign-off remains the gate
(`AG-227`, HARD), and Jake has not seen this version.

## Proposed learning

Candidate: an adversarial finding is a lead, not a verdict. One of five here would have replaced a
correct figure with a wrong one had it been applied unchecked, and the genuine defect underneath it
was a missing explanation rather than a bad number.
