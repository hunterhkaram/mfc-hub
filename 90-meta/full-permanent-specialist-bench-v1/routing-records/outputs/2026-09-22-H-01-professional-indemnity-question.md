# H-01 - the professional indemnity question, and who can actually answer it

## Bounded question
Should MFC carry professional indemnity before delivering paid workshops; is this a question for
Debra Fidler; do the declared occupation and declared turnover matter; and is the broker the right
person rather than the advisors?

## Position
**MFC is not in a dangerous position on insurance, and the email must not read as though it is.**
MFC holds more public liability than most organisations its size, bought properly through a broker,
and the committee approved both policies. **The professional indemnity gap is real but it is far
more likely to cost MFC a contract than a claim**, which reframes the whole question: this is
procurement readiness with a price tag, not a safety failure.

**The advisors own exactly one question and the broker owns the rest.** Eimear Quigley and Onno
van-es can answer whether MFC should carry PI given what MFC actually does: that is a judgement
about risk appetite and about how "professional service" reads against a non-clinical educational
workshop. They cannot answer whether BUP026 bites the way the schedule reads, whether paid delivery
in client workplaces is a disclosable change in the nature of the risk, or what PI would cost.
**If they try, MFC ends up with an internally generated position instead of an insurer's.**

## Professional principles applied
An exposure stated more strongly than the record supports is as much a failure as one understated.
A question put to the wrong person produces an answer that looks like assurance and is not.

## Evidence strength and limitations
The insurance position was captured 2026-09-01 directly from the policy schedules, and schedules
change at renewal. **The broker brief's current contents were not re-verified in this pass** and
should be read before sending.

## Alternatives considered
Putting the question to Debra Fidler as well - **rejected.** Her authority is evidence design,
measurement and the resilience distinction. None of it bears on whether MFC carries a policy. Copy
her if she is already on the thread; do not address the question to her.

## Recommendation
Ask Eimear and Onno the yes or no. **Send the broker email in parallel this week**, because it does
not depend on their answer and it retires two things that are more urgent than the PI debate.

## Two things more urgent than the PI gap
1. **The declared occupation.** The policy reads "Community Health Centre Operation" and "Mental
   Health Promotion - Support Network". **Neither describes paid training delivery in third-party
   workplaces.** If that is a change in the nature of the risk and it is not disclosed, the exposure
   is not "no PI" - it is that **the public liability cover MFC does hold could be argued down at
   claim time.** One email to the broker retires it.
2. **No certificate of currency has ever been filed**, despite a July 2025 committee action.
   **Corporate and council procurement asks for it before it asks about PI.** Same email.

**Declared turnover of $20,000 does not matter yet.** It is accurate against MFC's current position
and becomes wrong only when commercial revenue exceeds it. A diary item for the July 2027 renewal
or first material paid delivery, whichever comes first. Owner: Hunter, with Rob Dooley as incoming
Treasurer.

## Strongest objection
That asking two advisers a yes or no on an insurance question invites an answer neither is
qualified to give. **The answer is that the question put to them is deliberately the only half that
is theirs** - risk appetite and how the exclusion reads against what MFC delivers - and that a split
or unsure reply is not a stalemate: it resolves to the broker by default.

## What would change this view
The broker confirming that paid workplace delivery is disclosable and materially changes the
premium. A client requiring PI as a procurement condition, which converts this from a judgement into
a price.
