# A-24 — the paper Onno approved, against the paper after Jake's review

## Bounded question

Are Jake's three changes implemented soundly, completely, and without contradicting the rest of the
document?

## Position

Running at the time of writing. The comparison is briefed on six specific checks: whether all
thirteen approved April priorities survive the regrouping into five focus areas; whether the funnel
recomputes to twenty; whether every surviving reference to the old hundred-approach framing was
found; whether the education/evidence split broke any other section that still says four focus
areas; whether the contents page and the Appendix D pointers resolve; and whether anything outside
the three authorised changes was altered.

## Sources reviewed

- `60-projects/committee-pack-oct-2026/THE-CONSOLIDATED-STRATEGY.md`, current and at git HEAD
- The amended version and its rendered PDF
- The April 2026 committee-approved pillar document, read directly from Google Docs
- The 28 September recording, in which Jake raised the three points

## Professional principles applied

A change can be correct in itself and still break a sentence in another section. Arithmetic is
recomputed rather than read. A clean verdict is acceptable and is not to be padded.

## Evidence strength and limitations

Strong on what can be checked mechanically: the thirteen priorities, the funnel arithmetic, the
surviving references, the cross-references. Weak on prediction: the conversion rates have no MFC
history behind them, and the paper says so.

## Alternatives considered

Applying my own self-caught finding before the review returned was rejected, because changing the
artefact mid-review makes the review's findings stale and the two would then have to be
reconciled by hand.

## Recommendation

One finding is already established without waiting for the review, self-caught while the agent ran.
**The funnel yields fifteen warm and five cold, and three lines below it the paper says fifteen
Introductions and five half days.** Two different cuts of the same twenty, both fifteen and five,
adjacent. Rob is precisely the reader who would conflate them. The fix moves the funnel to sixteen
and four by changing the final conversion rates to 64 and 40 per cent, which still totals twenty and
still has warm outperforming cold at every step. It will be applied together with the review's
findings.

## Strongest objection

That amending a paper the chair has already approved risks reopening a closed decision. The answer
is that Jake's three points were raised in a recorded session with the co-founder, none changes a
number the chair approved, and the covering email asks Onno only to confirm he is still happy for
it to go out.

## What would change this view

A finding that one of the thirteen priorities has no home, or that the funnel does not reconcile.

## Adjacent specialist or human boundary

Onno's permission to send is human. This review does not supply it.

## Proposed learning

Candidate: when a new table is introduced near an existing one, check whether its totals collide
numerically with the neighbour's. Two correct tables can mislead by adjacency.
