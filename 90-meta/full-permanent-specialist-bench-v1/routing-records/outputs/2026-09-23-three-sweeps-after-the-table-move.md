# Three parallel sweeps after the costing tables moved to Appendix C

## Bounded question

Judged only against itself, does the paper hold together on numbers and product rules, on
cross-references and defined terms, and section by section?

## Position

**The figures are sound; the signposting was not.** The numbers pass found every price, ladder,
multiple, hour total, margin and year-one projection reconciling, and all fifteen contents entries
correct against the rendered PDF. What broke was pointers left aiming at where content used to be,
which is exactly the failure a large structural move produces.

## Sources reviewed

- `60-projects/committee-pack-oct-2026/THE-CONSOLIDATED-STRATEGY.md` and `.pdf`
- The three task packets at `.../packets/2026-09-23-{numbers,crossrefs,drift}-sweep.yml`

## Professional principles applied

One failure mode per reader, so none of them skims. Quote both sides or it is not a finding. Judge
the document against itself only.

## Evidence strength and limitations

Strong on arithmetic, which was recomputed independently and agreed. Weaker on the softer findings,
several of which are judgement about repetition rather than error. Deliberately bounded: tone,
strategy quality and missing content were out of scope for all three.

## Alternatives considered

A single reader covering all three dimensions was rejected. Earlier single-pass reads in this session
found fourteen, sixteen and thirteen items and still missed the class another reader caught; splitting
the failure modes is what surfaced the swapped contents subtitles and the self-referential appendix.

## Recommendation

Applied in full:

**Pointers left behind by the move.** The contents subtitles for sections 4 and 7 were swapped
against their headings. Section 7 sent the reader to section 4 for returns now in Appendix C.
Appendix C pointed at itself. Section 2 promised Appendix A holds the census when it holds a pointer
to it.

**Real contradictions.** The manager session was 45 minutes in one place and an hour in three.
Section 6 committed to a report section 4 says a client may decline. Section 1 promoted the hundred
organisations from an aim to a floor. Section 9 carried an orphaned "All four are Operational", the
runway sentence twice, and an unsupported comparative about national facilitator pay.

**Definitions.** Five terms used in the body were missing from the glossary: the manager session, the
return session, the cards, a focus area, the ambassador programme. The quadrant caption defined
mental fitness in words the glossary does not use.

**Presentation.** The traffic lights had no caption. Appendix C's online column summed to $166
against a stated $165, now disclosed as per-row rounding.

## Strongest objection

That three readers on one document is over-processing. It is not: each found a class the others
missed, and the two most consequential findings, the swapped contents subtitles and the appendix
pointing at itself, came from different readers.

## What would change this view

A fourth sweep returning nothing, which would say the document has converged.

## Adjacent specialist or human boundary

Consistency only, not whether the strategy is right. Hunter's sign-off remains the gate (`AG-227`),
and Jake has not seen this version.

## Proposed learning

Candidate: after a structural move, the arithmetic is rarely what breaks. The pointers are. A reader
briefed on one failure mode finds what a reader briefed on everything skims past.
