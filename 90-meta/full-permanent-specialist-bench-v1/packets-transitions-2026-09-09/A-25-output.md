# A-25 (Founder Voice) — the nine spoken transitions, 2026-09-09

**Pack status.** A-25, founder voice, built from sixteen transcripts, 2,643 of Hunter's messages and
both interviews. **The limit, once and up front:** it models how he explains things. It is not a
model of how a *room* receives them, and MFC has delivered zero cohorts — so every claim below about
what a room hears is reconstruction.

## Bounded question

For each of the nine approved sections: the spoken transition in, the device that makes it audible as
a section start without reading as an agenda, and the question it leaves hanging for the next.

## Sources reviewed

  delivering the whole talk.** Read for how he turns a corner: a flat *"So"*, a question stated

- `15-inputs/hunter-notes/workshop/2026-09-08-founder-the-full-content-bank.md` — all 31 sections.

## Professional principles applied

1. **Experience precedes the name.** The breath is never framed; naming it first closes the gap the
   next section exists to fill.
2. **A device heard four times stops being heard.** Nine identical constructions would be worse than
   none, so every join uses a different mechanism and the inventory proves it.
3. **Teaching register throughout.** The 25 May transcript is committee register *about* the talk, so
   it was mined for corner-turning mechanics, not wording.
4. **The room's objection gets spoken before the room can raise it.**

## Position

**Nine transitions, nine distinct devices.** Section 1 has no transition — the cold open is the
device, a rule rather than an omission. Two hanging questions are spoken (out of 2, into 6); seven
are implied and must stay implied.

Devices: cold open · question asked flatly and answered immediately · register change into withdrawal
and silence · callback naming what they just did · concession then turn · abrupt sentence-length drop
· flat refusal then concrete list · deliberate pause on one short sentence · deliberate deflation on
a monosyllable.

## Recommendation

Adopt all nine. **Two joins carry the structural risk:**

**2→3, into the breath.** An argument has momentum and ninety seconds of silence dumps it. It must
not be framed (⛔ not *"let's try something"*); the safety line is the bridge, not the framing; and it
must land as relief rather than as a task — **section 2 has just said it was never their fault, and
silence is the physical form of that release.** If section 2 ends on argument, this join fails.

**8→9, out of the peak.** Harder, because every instinct pushes the other way. ⛔ **The peak must not
be summarised** — restating it flatter is what kills it. One monosyllable, a real pause, then the
most ordinary sentence in the hour. **The flatness must be concrete, not modest:** *"there's a sheet
in front of you"* works; *"I just want to leave you with one thing"* is a crescendo in a flat costume.

## Evidence strength and limitations

**Weak, and specifically so.** No transition has been heard by any room. The only delivery recording
is to four committee members who govern Hunter — a governance audience, not strangers.

## Alternatives considered

- **One repeated transition formula.** Rejected: the room learns it by the fourth use.
- **Every hanging question explicit.** Rejected as exhausting.
- **Closing on the peak.** Rejected — Hunter's locked decision, and the flat close is the only ending
  consistent with a product claiming this is ordinary practice rather than an event.

## Strongest objection

**Nine named sections may be one granularity too many.** The four movements were locked on the
argument that three hides the peak and five makes the breath droppable; nine is more than either. The
defence is that the nine are *audible* while the four are *architectural* — but that has never been
tested, and if it fails, it fails by making the hour feel administered.

## What would change this view

One delivery to real participants. It would settle whether the deflation lands, whether the
concession at section 5 alienates a room that genuinely did not know the material, and whether nine
sections is a map or a timetable.

## Adjacent specialist or human boundary

Committee ratification and qualified clinical review are unmet external gates. Hunter's founder
judgement governs the arc. **No participant has sat through any version.**

## Proposed learning

**Candidate, and it is this system's error rather than a design finding.** The nine-section
architecture places *why knowing isn't enough* **before** *what you actually do* — reversing beats 9
and 10 and contradicting `D-0131`'s rule that nothing is reordered. Introduced when the sections were
authored, not noticed, caught here. **The narrow lesson: a structure authored on top of a locked
decision must be checked against that decision's ordering rule before anything is written to it.**
Surfaced to Hunter. Offered as a candidate, not written as a lesson, per the §12 freeze.
